# T-091 — Regulatory & Deal Certainty: clearance screen, divestiture viability, and covenant tracking (core module)
Engine: claude

**Lane:** domain
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** domain, packs, spec
**Needs:** none. Its acceptance fixture is T-092 Case 1 — build the two together.

## Origin (2026-10-26)

Kumar: "Albertsons and Kroger had a merger and it soured. Can there be lessons and tasks that can help our packs?" Then: "Can we unearth this during diligence?" — analysis delivered and approved the same day. The verified case: Kroger-Albertsons, $24.6B, announced Oct 2022; blocked Dec 10, 2024 by federal *and* Washington state courts after the FTC + nine AGs sued; terminated Dec 11, 2024. The divestiture package (579 stores to C&S Wholesale) failed on: unused headroom under its own 650-store cap (71 stores unspent), cherry-picked weaker stores, an incoherent patchwork of banners/assets, and a buyer without the operating record to run them. Albertsons' ensuing suit (Delaware Chancery, trial Oct 2026, $9.3B sought incl. the $600M termination fee) alleges willful breach of the agreement's maximal efforts covenant. Every fatal defect was computable or documented *before* the courts ruled. This module exists so a Pactlab user sees them during Pre and Diligence — not in a post-mortem.

## Part 1 — Pre-phase regulatory screen

- **Regulator map:** for the pact's industry + geographies, the authorities who can block or condition the deal (federal antitrust, state AGs, sector regulators), each with the theory of harm they'd run. Map content comes from the per-pack regulator registry (Part 5) + deal inputs, presented as a checklist with evidence slots — not a horoscope.
- **Local-market concentration:** competition is local in most of our packs. Footprint inputs (store/site/facility locations for both parties, as structured data or CSV) produce per-local-market overlap and concentration views; national share is shown but never leads. Overlap pockets above threshold raise a -1 finding at Pre with the markets named.
- **Posture signals:** enforcement climate as *evidence with dates* — current merger-guideline regime, recent comparable rulings/consents, sector-specific signals (analyst-entered, source-cited). Foresight's government-posture trend (T-073) consumes the same entries; there is one posture record, not two.
- **Labor exposure:** union density, CBA coverage/expiry, and bargaining-structure effects as a first-class theory of harm (the FTC ran it in Kroger; it is no longer exotic). Pack evidence where it exists; structured inputs where it doesn't.
- **Screen output:** feeds the T-071 Pre verdict with a clearance-risk section: GO / GO-WITH-REMEDY-PRICED / NO_GO, where a remedy is *priced into the bridge* from day one when overlap demands it (Part 2 costs it), instead of discovered at month 20.

## Part 2 — Divestiture / remedy workbench

- **Scenario builder:** carve-out sets (sites, assets, banners, contracts) assembled into candidate packages; multiple scenarios side by side.
- **Package quality checks (the Kroger autopsy, encoded):**
  - *Headroom:* package size vs the covenant cap from the merger agreement — unused headroom is stated in units and % ("you promised up to 650; you're offering 579; regulators have noticed smaller gaps").
  - *Cherry-pick test:* performance distribution of the divested set vs the retained set — a divested set that skews materially weaker raises a finding with the distributions shown.
  - *Coherence:* does the package stand alone as an operating business — shared distribution, banners/brands, systems, supply contracts, geographic clustering — scored from the asset graph; a patchwork scores as a patchwork.
  - *Buyer viability:* the divestiture buyer assessed like any counterparty — operating track record in this exact business, capital, existing footprint, and precedent flags (a prior failed remedy involving the same parties/buyer class is surfaced, e.g. the 2015 Haggen collapse after the Albertsons-Safeway divestitures).
- **Regulator feedback log:** every regulator communication on the remedy, logged with date and substance, linked to the package revision that followed it — unaddressed feedback ages visibly ("feedback round 2 called the package incoherent; two revisions since, coherence score unchanged").

## Part 3 — Covenant and deal-certainty tracking

- The merger agreement's deal-certainty obligations become **register items**, tracked exactly like claims (agreement says / observed conduct): efforts standard (reasonable best efforts vs hell-or-high-water), divestiture caps, outside (long-stop) date, termination-fee architecture (who pays whom, how much, on which triggers), interim operating covenants.
- Status derives from evidence: feedback log entries, package revisions, elapsed time. The long-stop countdown and elapsed-deal-age are always visible — 26 months of drift should look like something.
- **Bridge integration:** termination-fee exposure and expected remedy cost (from Part 2 scenarios) appear as explicit scenario lines in the valuation bridge. Certainty risk is priced, not narrated.

## Part 4 — Commitment quality

- Pledges made to regulators or counterparties (price commitments, investment promises, job guarantees) get an **enforceability/verifiability score**: is there a measurement mechanism, an enforcement party, a duration? Washington's court dismissed Kroger's $1B price pledge as "unenforceable, impossible to verify, and temporary" — a pledge scoring UNVERIFIABLE is discounted in regulator-facing strategy and flagged as a claim that will earn no credit.

## Part 5 — Pack integration

- **Regulator registry per pack:** hospitals/small clinics (state AG + health regulators, local-market pattern is native there), telecom/media (FCC + state), pharma/biotech (FTC + FDA posture), hotels/commercial (franchise consent + local land-use), fintech (banking/fintech regulators + CFPB posture), professional services/e-commerce (lighter — antitrust only where concentration is real). Each pack registers its authorities and theories of harm; the module supplies the machinery.
- **Proportionality:** screen depth scales with regulatory surface. A Venture Raise seed round gets a light version (no local-market machinery); a two-chain grocery merger gets the full pass. Depth is a function of the deal, not a fixed tax.

## Guardrails

- **Risk surfacing, not legal advice, never outcome prediction.** No "probability of clearance" percentage pretending to precision — qualitative bands, each showing the findings and evidence under it. Judges are not a data source.
- Every check shows its inputs; a user can disagree with a coherence score and see exactly what to change in the package to move it.
- Nothing here reaches outside the pact's confidentiality; regulator-facing exports are deliberate, audited acts (same rule as T-084).

## Acceptance

- On T-092 Case 1 (the synthetic Kroger-Albertsons analog), the Pre screen flags the overlap pockets and labor exposure before any package exists.
- The workbench flags all four planted package defects — headroom, cherry-pick skew, incoherence, buyer viability — with evidence, and the feedback log shows the planted unaddressed round aging.
- Covenant tracker computes the unused headroom and long-stop status from the fixture agreement; the bridge carries the fee-exposure and remedy-cost lines.
- A planted UNVERIFIABLE pledge scores as such and is excluded from the regulator-credit view.
