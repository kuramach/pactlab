# ADR 0053: Hospitals: cost reports, payer contracts, the medical staff and the plant (T-162)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the hospitals pack of T-069 (spec §19 11a) on the healthcare pack (T-061, T-160); released on merge.

## Context
- Hospitals carry evidence no other pack reads: filed cost reports, payer contracts that expire mid-hold, a medical staff whose clinical privileges are renewed department by department, regulated quality reporting, and a physical plant whose deferred capex hides in the P&L trend. The T-069 pack read the census and payer mix, the accreditation record, an aggregate staffing summary and the committed regulatory capex in the cost report; nothing reconciled the cost report to the deck or the P&L, read a payer agreement's term, read the medical staff, or read capital spend across years.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `HOSPITALS` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (`PAYER_MIX_EBITDA`, DCF). The T-069 targets and rules, and the T-061 healthcare rules it reuses by id (medical-director fee, privacy assessment), stay first and unchanged. The T-160 rules are not adopted: a hospital is paid on its payer mix, not on remittances by clinician, and its staff are read by department, not by collections.
2. **What the pack adds, by registry entry only:**
   - **Import (CSV, ADR 0010):** `HOSPITALS_MEDICAL_STAFF` (→ `hospitals.medical_staff`: pseudonymous staff reference, department, role, the licence's status and expiry, the clinical privileges' status and reappointment date). Never a name or licence number; the people-column guard applies.
   - **Document categories:** cost report, payer contract (the healthcare category), property condition assessment, quality reporting and licence verification; intake expects each, so a missing one is a checklist gap.
   - **A calculator, `hospitals-operations-1`:** trailing net revenue and the largest payer's share, admissions, average daily census and length of stay, capital spend ÷ revenue in the latest P&L year, and the medical staff at the analysis date (lapsed privileges, departments lapsing together, expired licences). Occupancy needs the beds the cost report states, so it is read on the Hospital tab and by the rules.
3. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page. The analysis date is the last day of the latest census or payer-mix month, never the clock.
   - **Cost-report reconciliation** (`hospitals-cost-report-reconciliation`): a document headed "… cost report …" is read for its fiscal year, beds, inpatient days, occupancy, the Medicare, Medicaid and commercial shares of inpatient days, and total costs. The same figures the deck and other seller documents state are claims-register entries against it (the report governs): within a point VERIFIED (+1, confirmed value), beyond three points CONTRADICTED (−1, a haircut on the volume or mix the scenarios assume), with no cost report UNVERIFIED (0). The report's total costs against the imported P&L's cost of revenue and operating expenses for the same fiscal year, beyond 2%, is −1.
   - **Payer contract register** (`hospitals-payer-contracts`): each contracted payer in the payer-mix export (not a government programme or self-pay) at 5% or more of trailing net revenue, matched to a document headed "… agreement …" that names it, read for its rate basis, the end of its term and its renewal or renegotiation window. Measured from a document's stated closing date (else the analysis date): a term ending inside twelve months of closing with no automatic renewal, or already ended, is −1 — a payer renegotiating its rates early in the hold, tested as a rate change in the payer-mix-adjusted EBITDA scenario or made a closing condition. HIGH for the largest contracted payer.
   - **Medical staff** (`hospitals-medical-staff`): the T-160 credentialing pattern at hospital scale. Three or more of a department's staff sharing one privilege reappointment date that passed with no reappointment is −1 (the department may not be able to admit or operate: a closing condition or a holdback); falling due together within 90 days is 0, a closing deliverable; licences expired at the analysis date (a verification document's date governs) are −1.
   - **Plant and capex** (`hospitals-plant-capex`): capital spend on plant and equipment ÷ revenue in every fiscal year the imported P&L covers in full; falling every year to three quarters of its first-year ratio or less is −1, the trend defect a single year misses. The deck's "modern plant" sentence and the property condition assessment's statements are quoted beside it; priced as remediation at the deferred maintenance the assessment states, otherwise by the reviewer.
   - **Quality claims** (`hospitals-quality-claims`): each star rating the deck claims, as a claims-register entry mapped to the quality-reporting documents supplied: the same overall rating VERIFIED (+1), another CONTRADICTED (−1), none supplied UNVERIFIED (0).
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `hospitals-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the hospital's key expenses: salaries and wages, contract labour, medical supplies and drugs, capital spend, and repairs and maintenance.
4. **Directions.** Every T-162 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`hospitals.payer_month`, `hospitals.medical_staff`, the cost-report and capex signals on `finance.pnl_line`, and the claim signals on `document.finding`); `apps/api/src/packs/hospitals-directions.test.ts` runs every rule in the pack's registry entry over the demo data and checks each proposed direction against the map. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
5. **The Hospital tab** (`/deals/:id/hospitals`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/hospitals`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded: one row per department, the 25 largest payers, the first 50 staff exceptions, one entry per cost report, condition assessment and quality report. "Run hospital checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No clinical, survey or legal judgment.** The pack reconciles documents, dates and arithmetic. It never predicts a survey or accreditation outcome, assesses the quality of care, or says what a payer agreement means in law. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states (no cost report, no closing date, no agreement, no assessment) is shown as missing, never filled in.
- **People data (rule 7).** Medical staff are pseudonymous references; their rows stay in deterministic code and never reach a model; rules read only business and financial documents, never HR-classed ones.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-069 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/hospitals/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (St. Brennan Health) gains the medical-staff register, a three-year P&L, a fuller cost report summary, the management presentation, two payer agreements, the property condition assessment and the quality reporting, with planted defects (a) the largest contracted payer's agreement ending seven months after the 31 December 2026 target closing, (b) the cost report's 66.4% occupancy against the deck's 82%, (c) capital spend falling from 6.1% to 2.6% of revenue across FY2023–FY2025 while the deck claims a modern plant, (d) all seven orthopaedic staff's privileges lapsed together on 30 June 2026. `apps/api/src/packs/hospitals.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the hospitals pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270112000000_hospitals_cost_reports_staff_plant` adds one `import_target` value; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum value stays unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for cost reports and agreements the sentence patterns do not read; census-derived occupancy as a finding (the census rarely covers the cost report's fiscal year); the payer contract register as an import (the healthcare register stays its own pack's); QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
