# ADR 0068: Commercial property: the rent roll against the leases, recoveries, obligations, the expiry cliff and condition (T-165)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the commercial property pack of T-066 (spec §19 8a); released on merge.

## Context
- Commercial property diligence is a documents-versus-summary discipline, the direct sibling of the Multifamily sale pack with commercial lease mechanics: the rent roll against the leases, the recoveries against the lease terms, the expiries against the hold, and the condition reports against the seller's capex plan.
- The T-066 pack read the rent roll, one year's CAM reconciliation, a lease abstract and the Phase I; nothing recomputed a tenant's rent from its lease, checked recoveries across years against the leases' shares, measured the expiries from the closing, listed the landlord's unpaid obligations or read a condition report against the capex schedule.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `COMMERCIAL_PROPERTY` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (NOI ÷ cap rate, DCF). The four T-066 rules stay first and unchanged; the Multifamily pack is untouched (its property evidence and calculator are not reused because the commercial rent roll's shape differs; the NOI ÷ cap-rate method is shared through the method table as before).
2. **What the pack adds, by registry entry only:**
   - **Import (CSV, ADR 0010):** `CRE_CAPEX_SCHEDULE` (→ `cre.capex_item`: year, item, building element, amount, committed or planned). The rent roll and CAM reconciliation are the T-066 targets; the CAM reconciliation now carries one row per year and category for every statement year.
   - **Documents:** the leases (the common `LEASE` category, now a required input for this pack), and the property condition assessment and environmental reports (`PROPERTY_CONDITION_REPORT`, shared with hospitals; `ENVIRONMENTAL_REPORT`, shared with energy).
   - **A calculator, `cre-leases-1`:** in-place rent, the largest tenant's share, WALT and rent expiring within a year from the rent roll; the latest year's recovery ratio and unrecovered expenses from the CAM reconciliation (with every year beside the P&L, ADR 0057); committed and scheduled capex. A missing figure names the import it needs. The leases are documents: the rules read them.
3. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). A lease is a document whose heading says "lease agreement" and names a rent-roll tenant; its term, initial rent, escalation, recovery basis and share, and any unpaid tenant improvements or leasing commission are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page. An abstract, a condition report and the extracts' copy are never read as leases. Checks whose answer a document can change wait for the documents (`docs/agent/packs.md` §8b).
   - **Rent-roll verification** (`cre-rent-roll-verification`): each suite's rent recomputed from its lease at the analysis date (the end of the latest billed month) — the initial rent stepped up by the escalation on each anniversary reached, rounded half-up to the cent at each step — against the rent roll. More than 0.5% (and a unit) above the lease is an overstatement (−1, unpriced: NOI on the leases' rent, the reviewer prices it at the cap rate). Every fiscal year of the imported P&L's base rental income against the rent the leases make due in it, beyond 2% above (−1). A rent-roll tenant with no lease that states its rent, and a roll expiry the lease does not give, are recorded (0).
   - **Recovery reconciliation** (`cre-recovery-reconciliation`): by statement year, recoveries billed against the recoverable pool × the triple-net tenants' proportionate shares the leases state. A shortfall of 2% or more in every one of three or more consecutive years is a persistent leak (−1, unpriced); the P&L's recovery line is shown beside each year. The T-066 single-year rule stays.
   - **Obligations register** (`cre-obligations`): unpaid tenant improvement allowances and leasing commissions the leases state, and the committed lines of the seller's capex schedule — priced into the bridge as a price reduction at their sum (−1).
   - **Concentration and the cliff** (`cre-rent-cliff`): rent share by tenant from the rent roll, and the share of rent expiring in each year of the hold, measured from the expected closing a document states (else the analysis date) for the hold a document states (else five years), a lease's own term governing its end date. A tenant at 25% of rent or more ending before the closing or in the first two years of the hold is a cliff (−1). No view on whether the tenant renews.
   - **Condition evidence** (`cre-condition-capex`): items a property condition assessment or environmental report flags with an estimated cost, by building element (roof, HVAC, paving, elevators, envelope, fire, electrical, plumbing, structure), against the seller's capex schedule. Omitted is −1, priced as remediation at the report's estimate; scheduled below 90% of it is −1 at the difference. Nothing is drafted before the schedule is imported.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `cre-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses repairs and maintenance, real estate taxes, insurance and utilities, and `cre-recent-run` (T-185).
4. **Directions.** Every T-165 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`cre.lease`, `cre.cam_category`, `cre.capex_item`); `apps/api/src/packs/commercial-property-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: the below-market anchor stays confirmed value, never a price increase.
5. **No new screen.** The calculator's figures and the years table show on Finances, the drafts on Findings, as for every pack; the view stays buyer-side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`).

## Guardrails
- **No appraisal.** The pack reconciles documents and arithmetic; it never values the property. The reviewer sets the cap rate and the price.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states (a lease, an escalation, a closing date) is shown as missing, never filled in.
- **People data.** Tenants are businesses; no people data is read.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-066 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/commercial-property/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Quayside Estates, eleven tenants on triple-net leases) gains the eleven leases, three years of CAM reconciliations and of P&L, the seller's capex schedule, a property condition assessment and the offering memorandum, with planted defects (a) the rent roll overstating two tenants' rent against their leases (an escalation at 3% where the lease says 2%, and a step a fixed-rent lease never granted), (b) 41% of rent from one tenant whose lease ends six months after the 31 December 2026 closing, (c) recoveries short of the leases in every one of 2023, 2024 and 2025, (d) a roof the condition assessment flags at a stated estimate with no line in the seller's capex schedule; and a P&L trend a single year misses (repairs and maintenance outpacing revenue). `apps/api/src/packs/commercial-property.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270125000000_commercial_property_leases_recoveries_capex` adds one `import_target` value; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum value stays unused.
- **Site mismatches corrected:** the page listed zoning documents and zoning flags, above-market lease checks and model-drafted lease abstracts, none of which the pack does; they are removed, and the page describes the lease, recovery, obligations, cliff and condition checks.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for leases the sentence patterns do not read; a dedicated property tab; percentage rent, caps on recoverable expense growth and gross-up clauses; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
