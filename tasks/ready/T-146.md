## Why
- **The need:** near signing and closing, a counter-proposal or a waiting signature should reach the person within minutes, even when they aren't at their desk.
- **The basis:** T-145 builds the events, recipients, urgency and preferences. This task adds two phone channels for **"act now" events only**.

## Content rule (both channels)
- **Content-free:** a phone notification shows on a lock screen, and SMS isn't secure. Every message is content-free, e.g. "Pactlab: an action is waiting for you on a pact. Open Pactlab to see it.", with a link that requires sign-in.
- **What must never appear:** a company or pact name, a counterparty, an amount, terms, or any document or people data.
- **Testing:** a test asserts that the payload builders can't include them.

## Part 1: web push (no cost, no carrier registration)
- **The app:** Pactlab becomes installable (a web app manifest and a service worker, scoped to notifications).
- **Subscribing:** Settings → Notifications → "Alerts on this device" subscribes the browser with the standard Push API and VAPID keys. Keys go in Secrets Manager per environment, wired through `infra/cdk` like the other provider keys, with a `scripts/*-key.sh` helper.
- **Platforms:** iPhone support requires the app added to the home screen (iOS 16.4 or later). Say so on the settings screen.
- **Lifecycle:** subscriptions are per device, can be revoked, and expired ones are removed when the push service says so.
- **Verification first:** the Web Push protocol, the VAPID header and the iOS limits are checked against current official docs (MDN, WebKit) before code. Nothing is assumed.

## Part 2: SMS (opt-in, paid; Kumar approves the spend before it's switched on)
- **The provider:** AWS End User Messaging SMS, behind a connector seam with a fixture adapter. It's switched off by default.
- **Paid dependency:** costs are per message. CLAUDE.md requires asking before adding a paid dependency: record Kumar's approval in this file before enabling it in any environment.
- **Opt-in:**
  - the person enters their number and confirms it with a one-time code;
  - explicit consent is recorded (time and text shown);
  - STOP or HELP are handled (TCPA and carrier rules), and STOP immediately stops all texts;
  - consent and opt-outs are audited.
- **Before live sends:** US sending needs a registered origination (10DLC brand and campaign registration, which takes weeks). Document the steps in a runbook, and give Kumar the part he must do in the AWS console as a script or checklist (not a long paste-in command).
- **Limits:**
  - a per-person cap, e.g. 10 texts a day;
  - one text per pact per 15 minutes; later events are folded into the next text;
  - texts are metered in Hosting cost.
- **Verification first:** the AWS End User Messaging SMS API (send, opt-out lists, registration) is checked against current official docs before code. No invented endpoints.

## Acceptance
- **Push:** a counter-proposal notifies a subscribed device within a minute, with a content-free payload (test with the fixture or a mock push service).
- **SMS:** after opt-in, an "act now" event sends one content-free text; STOP stops further texts; the daily cap holds; "for your information" events never text (tests with the fixture adapter).
- **Content rule:** tests prove no pact name, party, amount or document text reaches either channel.
- **Docs:** the runbook covers VAPID keys, SMS registration and costs; `.env.example` is updated.
