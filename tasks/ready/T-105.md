# T-105 — Buyer personas: deal-making style that prices the findings
Model: opus
Engine: claude

**Lane:** core
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** prisma schema (buyer-org default persona, pact persona, valuation-run persona stamp), valuation engine (Sextant scenario weighting), term-proposal generation, negotiation brief generation, web (buyer org settings, pact setup, valuation page)
**Needs:** nothing open. Builds on the merged valuation direction map (T-037) and Sextant. Kumar designed and approved this on 2026-11-06.

## Why

A buyer cannot be helped to price a deal until the platform knows their deal-making style (Kumar, 2026-11-06). Two buyers looking at identical findings legitimately land on different prices and different terms. Today the findings -> price/terms translation is one-size-fits-all; this task makes it a named, selectable buyer persona.

## The three personas

Customer-facing names are the archetypes, not the people. The picker shows each name with its one-line descriptor.

1. **Quality Compounder** (the Charlie Munger style) — "A wonderful business at a fair price." Quality first: happily pays a fair premium for a durable business; does not chase the bottom price. Pricing posture: only material, verified drags move the price, at the conservative end of the range; prefers structural fixes (remediation, specific indemnities, key-person retention) over blunt price chips; scenarios weighted toward business quality and durability.
2. **Value Investor** (the middle; Buffett / Lynch GARP) — "Value at a good price." The standard translation: balanced scenario weights, balanced escrow/earnout mix. This is the default persona.
3. **Grave Dancer** (the Sam Zell style; also Wilbur Ross) — "Price to survive the bad case." Chases the cheapest defensible price on troubled or ordinary assets alike. Pricing posture: every drag priced at the aggressive end of its range and stacked; the downside scenario anchors the bid; leads with large escrows/holdbacks, earnouts and seller financing that push risk back onto the seller; tight walk-away thresholds.

Do not use "Shylock" as a label anywhere: it is the same archetype as Grave Dancer, but it is a villain's name buyers will not pick for themselves.

## What a persona controls

All persona parameters live in ONE config registry (a PERSONA_PROFILE map, the same pattern as VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP): every value is data, reviewable in one place, no scattered conditionals. Four knobs:

1. **Drag translation** — the materiality threshold a -1 finding must clear to move price, and where inside its pricing range the finding is priced (conservative / standard / aggressive, per persona above).
2. **Scenario weighting** — how Sextant weights downside / base / upside when a persona-priced valuation is produced (durability-weighted / balanced / downside-anchored).
3. **Term lever preference** — the order term proposals are generated in across PRICE_REDUCTION, REMEDIATION_COST, ESCROW, INDEMNITY, plus earnout and seller-financing proposals for Grave Dancer, retention-flavoured terms for Quality Compounder.
4. **Brief tone** — the framing of the AI negotiation brief (quality-and-risk language / balanced / downside-and-leverage language). Tone follows posture; the underlying findings text never changes.

## Guardrails (hard requirements)

- **Persona never touches evidence.** Intake, extraction, findings and their -1/0/+1 directions are persona-invariant: the same evidence produces the identical findings set under all three personas. Persona only parameterises the findings -> price/terms translation.
- **The asymmetry is persona-invariant.** Cushions (+1) never raise the price and never touch the bridge under any persona (the standing rule from 2026-10-10). A Quality Compounder's willingness to pay for quality lives only in the buyer-confidential confirmed-value ledger, exactly as today.
- **Buyer-confidential.** The persona, and anything derived from it, never appears in seller-facing output: shared views, seller briefs, exported position papers. Same confidentiality class as the confirmed-value ledger.
- **Auditable.** The active persona is stamped on every valuation run and every term-proposal batch. Changing persona mid-pact never mutates prior outputs; it produces a new run.
- **Selection and switching.** Buyer org carries a default persona, editable any time in buyer org settings. Pact setup offers the picker, defaulting to the org default (Value Investor if unset). Switching must be effortless — re-evaluating a deal through another lens is the point of the feature:
  - The buyer-side valuation page carries a "Priced as: <persona>" control (buyer org only). Choosing a different persona runs a **fresh evaluation of the same findings set** under the new persona: no re-upload, no re-extraction, and prior runs are never mutated.
  - **Compare view:** the buyer can evaluate the pact under any or all three personas and see the runs side by side — price and term-mix deltas against the active run — then **adopt** any run as the pact's active pricing. Every run stays in history with its persona stamp.
  - **Proposed terms are sticky:** if terms were already proposed to the seller from an earlier run, switching persona re-prices internally only. Nothing changes seller-side until the buyer explicitly proposes new terms from the adopted run; proposals are versioned.

## Acceptance

- Seed pacts (HealthyCo, TroubledCo) priced under all three personas: findings sets identical across personas (contract test); resulting prices order Compounder >= Value Investor >= Grave Dancer on the same drag set; term mixes differ in the directions specified above.
- From the valuation page, a buyer evaluates the same pact under a second persona in one action, sees the price/term delta against the active run, and can adopt it; the original run is unchanged and the findings set identical (test). Proposed-to-seller terms are unaffected by a switch until explicitly re-proposed (test).
- Seller-facing output snapshots contain no persona name, id or derived parameter (test).
- Persona registry contract test: every knob is defined for all three personas; a missing knob fails the build.
- Existing valuation and direction-map tests stay green.
