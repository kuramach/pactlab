## Why
Kumar (2026-11-26): *"All the synthetic data is only for current 12 months. In a deal how do u determine the strength unless u look at historicals? Last 5 years of Income & Expense. Last 5 years of Rent, Last 5 years vacancy etc. So this style of data expectancy is good for all business and we shd think about accepting data beyond just current year and also make the synthetic have it."*

**Today:**
- **The shared multi-year P&L trend** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/pnl-trend.ts`) is used only by the seven packs built on 2026-11-26.
- **Multifamily** reads a T-12 and a single rent-roll snapshot. Nothing reads rent, occupancy or vacancy over time.
- **The demo data** (`kuramach/pactlab-demo-data`) holds 12 months: `multifamily/*/t12.csv` is flat month to month, with one `rent-roll.csv`.

This task sets the rule and builds it end to end on multifamily, as the pattern. T-184 applies it to every other pack.

## 1. The rule (an ADR, extending ADR 0042)
- **Every pack expects up to five fiscal years of history,** plus the current year or the T-12:
  - **Income and expenses:** the P&L or operating statement, by year (or by month).
  - **The pack's operating drivers:** for multifamily, rent and occupancy (vacancy).
- **Less history is accepted, never refused.**
  - Every metric states its coverage, e.g. "3 of 5 years".
  - A missing year is an **ask of the seller** through the existing ask-the-seller flow, e.g. *"Ask the seller for FY2022–FY2023 operating statements"*, never a guess.
- **Deterministic, as ADR 0042 requires:** the AI proposes the structure of a multi-year file (which columns are years, which rows are lines); code reads every value; a confirmed format is saved and reused.
- **Imported years stay separate:** imports overlapping the same period follow the existing rule (the newer row wins). Withdrawn imports never count.

## 2. Imports
- **Operating statements, multi-year:** the existing P&L / T-12 import accepts:
  - years as columns (FY2022 … FY2026);
  - a long file of month and line rows;
  - one file per year.

  Check what `finance.pnl_line` and the import targets already accept (`packages/domain/src/imports/targets.ts`), and extend that, not a new parallel path.
- **Rent and occupancy history,** in any of these three forms:
  - a **rent roll per period**: several rent-roll snapshots, each with its as-of date; or
  - a **monthly occupancy and rent summary**: month, units, occupied units, average in-place rent (overall or by unit type), and concessions; or
  - **annual figures**, if that's all the seller has.

  This becomes one import target (e.g. `property.occupancy_period`, with the canonical fields documented in the target registry), mapped like every other target with the plain-words mapping from #281.

## 3. The multifamily calculator and checks
- **Metrics, each cited to the imported rows behind it, decimal-only and versioned:**
  - effective gross income, NOI, and the expense ratio, per year;
  - 5-year NOI and revenue CAGR;
  - average in-place rent growth per year;
  - occupancy per year, as the average and the low point;
  - the trailing-3-year average vacancy.
- **Draft findings** (never accepted automatically):
  - **occupancy erosion:** down 3 points or more over the history, or below 90% in any of the last 3 years;
  - **expenses outpacing income:** reuse `pnl-trend`'s rule;
  - **a last-year spike:** the latest year's NOI growth well above the history's average. This is the "dressed for sale" pattern, so the reviewer checks one-off items and concessions;
  - **rent growth below expense growth.**
- **Wiring:**
  - multifamily joins the shared `pnlTrendRules`;
  - thresholds are named constants, documented in `docs/agent/packs.md`.

## 4. On screen
- **Home → Property metrics:** a small **History** strip per key metric (NOI, occupancy, average rent), five points, with the coverage stated ("FY2022–FY2026", or "FY2025–FY2026: ask the seller for earlier years").
  - Use the approved patterns in `docs/ui-patterns.md` and the existing chart library (ECharts). Charts follow the existing metric cards, not a new look.
- **Finances:** the years side by side, as a table: revenue, expenses, NOI, margin, occupancy and rent.

## 5. Synthetic demo data (in `kuramach/pactlab-demo-data`, pinned in `packages/domain/src/packs/demo-data.ts`)
- **For multifamily and multifamily-syndication, in USD, EUR and GBP:**
  - five fiscal years of operating statements (FY2022–FY2026, monthly), realistic rather than flat: seasonality, a rent-growth step each lease year, expense inflation, one capex year;
  - monthly occupancy and average rent for the same 60 months;
  - quarterly rent-roll snapshots for the last two years;
  - the dataroom exports (`dataroom/02 Finance/Exports/…`) updated to match.
- **Planted defects** for the new checks, each listed in the pack's demo-data README like the existing ones:
  - occupancy slipping from about 95% to about 89% over three years;
  - repairs and maintenance outpacing rent;
  - an FY2026 NOI jump driven by deferred maintenance and a one-off insurance refund.
- **The data stays consistent:** the existing 12-month files remain, equal to the last 12 months of the new history, so nothing that reads them breaks.
- Every name and figure is invented, as today. Raise a PR in the demo-data repo and bump the pin.

## 6. Website
The multifamily industry page says Pactlab reads five years of income, expenses, rent and occupancy, and flags erosion, expenses outpacing rent, and a last-year spike. Nothing beyond what ships.

## Acceptance
- **Imports:** a five-year operating statement (years as columns) and a 60-month occupancy file import deterministically. A re-import with the saved format makes no AI call.
- **Metrics:** CAGR, occupancy per year, rent growth and the expense ratio match hand-computed values from the demo data in all three currencies.
- **Findings:** each planted defect surfaces as a cited draft, and a re-run adds nothing.
- **Partial history:** two years only gives metrics with "2 of 5 years" coverage and an ask-the-seller entry for the missing years; nothing is extrapolated.
- **Screen:** the History strip and the years table render. One e2e journey covers it if no existing spec does; otherwise extend the existing multifamily spec.
