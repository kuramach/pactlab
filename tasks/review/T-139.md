## The problem

- **Per-document jobs** (extraction, consistency checks, classification) read one document at a time, and it fits in the context window. They stay full-text: the consistency check must see the whole agreement to spot a missing Section 9.
- **Pact-wide jobs** (document Q&A, Foresight, negotiation briefs) send every eligible document together, up to `MAX_CONTEXT_CHARS = 600_000` (about 150k tokens, `apps/api/src/documents/documents.service.ts`). Above that, the API refuses with a 422.
- **What fails:** a real data room (hundreds of documents, millions of characters) gets "too much to read" on its first question across the whole pact.

## Outcome

A deal team asks a question across the whole data room, of any size.

- **The answer:** cites exact pages, as it does today.
- **The coverage:** it says what was searched and which pages were read, so "not found" never looks like "not in the data room".
- **Small pacts:** a pact that fits the context window behaves exactly as today. Retrieval starts only above the budget.

## Work

1. **Plan mode first.** This adds a schema index and changes what reaches the model. Write an ADR on retrieval: why keyword search first, when vectors, and the data rules.
2. **Keyword search over pages first (no new service, no embedding provider).**
   - **Index:** a `tsvector` column on `document_pages` (expand migration, generated column or trigger) with a GIN index, under the existing RLS. A tenant can only ever retrieve its own pages.
   - **Why keyword first:** contract language is precise ("change of control", "assignment", "Section 9"), so keyword ranking does well. Use `websearch_to_tsquery` with `ts_rank_cd`.
   - **Page selection:** when a pact-wide job's sources exceed the budget, the API chooses the top-ranked pages for the question until the budget is full, plus neighbouring pages of strong hits (clauses span pages). Selection is deterministic: the same question on the same data gives the same pages.
   - **Scope:** retrieval only decides which pages are sent. The gateway, prompts, grounding and citation checks are unchanged. Every citation is still verified against the exact page text.
3. **Coverage in every answer.** The response and the screen show:
   - documents and pages searched;
   - pages read;
   - when the read was partial (over the budget): "searched N documents; read the M most relevant pages".

   An UNANSWERABLE result over a partial read says so, and offers to narrow the question to chosen documents (`documentIds` already exists).
4. **Evaluate before adding vectors.**
   - **The eval set:** a synthetic large data room in the eval suite (generated synthetic documents only, never customer data). Questions with known answer pages include paraphrased ones ("can they walk away if we buy them?" against change-of-control text).
   - **Measures:** retrieval recall@budget (are the answer pages among the pages sent?) and end-to-end answer correctness.
   - **Recorded on the run:** the strategy (FULL or KEYWORD) and the pages selected, with ids and hashes, never content.
5. **Vectors only if the evaluation says keyword search misses** (a separate PR, gated on step 4).
   - **Storage:** `pgvector` embeddings per page chunk in Postgres (no separate vector database), under RLS.
   - **Ranking:** a hybrid of keyword and vector rankings (reciprocal rank fusion), never vectors alone.
   - **The embedding provider is a provider:**
     - behind an adapter with a fixture implementation;
     - in the catalog with the same data-terms gate (DPA, zero retention) as model providers (ADR 0034);
     - metered in Hosting cost.

     Bedrock Titan or Cohere embeddings on AWS when Bedrock is unblocked; otherwise a cleared provider.
   - **Staleness:** re-embed when a page changes. A purged document's chunks are deleted with it, and retention applies.

## Rules that must hold

- **No people data in the index or in embeddings** (rule 7). HR, compensation, performance and named-contribution pages are never indexed and never embedded. The gateway's data-classification check already covers embedding calls, so keep it there and test it.
- **Citations:** every citation must resolve (rule 4). Retrieval never weakens the citation gate.
- **Exclusions:** AI-excluded documents (`aiExcluded`) and target-contributor visibility rules apply to retrieval exactly as they do to sources today. Test both the allow and deny paths, including cross-tenant.
- **Large lists:** the T-126 pagination rule applies to any list of pages or documents.

## Acceptance

- A synthetic data room over the budget answers questions with cited pages instead of returning a 422. Coverage is shown on the answer.
- Under the budget, the behaviour and the runs are identical to today (test).
- Recall@budget on the synthetic set is recorded. A decision note says whether vectors are needed, with the numbers.
- RLS: another tenant's pages never rank, and excluded or people-data pages are never sent (tests).
- ADR, runbook and `.env.example` (any budget knob) are updated.
