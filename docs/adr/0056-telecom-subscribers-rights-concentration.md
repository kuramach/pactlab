# ADR 0056: Telecom & media: subscriber claims, rights windows and revenue concentration, from billing exports and agreements (T-168)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the telecom and media pack of T-062 (spec §19 3a); released on merge.

## Context
- Telecom and media deals price subscriber metrics and the agreements content and distribution run on. The failures are checkable: churn the billing system contradicts, a carriage or affiliate agreement ending just after the closing, one advertiser carrying the P&L, content costs rising while the price per subscriber stands still.
- The T-062 pack read billed subscribers by month, the KPI pack's reported subscribers and churn, regional concentration and the wholesale re-pricing clause; nothing recomputed churn or ARPU from movements, read an agreement's end date against the closing, or measured revenue by advertiser or distributor.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `TELECOM_MEDIA` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (ARR multiple, DCF). The T-062 targets and rules stay first and unchanged. The pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `TELECOM_SUBSCRIBER_MOVEMENTS` (→ `telecom.subscriber_movement`: month, region, plan, opening and closing subscribers, gross additions, disconnects, service revenue), `TELECOM_AGREEMENTS` (→ `telecom.agreement`: the rights and carriage register — reference, counterparty, kind, whether the fees are received or paid, annual value, start and end dates, automatic renewal), `TELECOM_REVENUE_STREAMS` (→ `telecom.revenue_month`: advertising and affiliate revenue by month and counterparty) and `TELECOM_CAPEX` (→ `telecom.capex_year`: capital expenditure by fiscal year and category). Counts and amounts only; counterparties are businesses; the people-column guard applies.
   - **Document category:** content, affiliate or carriage agreement (`RIGHTS_AGREEMENT`, route FILE).
   - **A calculator, `telecom-subscribers-1`:** closing subscribers, monthly churn, ARPU and net additions over the trailing twelve months, the largest advertiser's and distributor's share of their stream, the largest agreement against trailing revenue, and the latest year's content cost per subscriber and capex intensity. A missing figure names the import it needs. Subscriber revenue still runs the SaaS metrics as before.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page; a document whose heading says it reproduces the extracts is never read as a claim or an agreement.
   - **Subscriber claims register** (`telecom-subscriber-claims`): the subscriber count, monthly churn and ARPU a deck states, recomputed from the movements export — subscribers against the latest month's closing (verified within 1%, contradicted beyond 2%), churn as disconnects ÷ opening subscribers over the trailing twelve months (within 0.1, beyond 0.25 points; an annual figure compared as a twelfth), ARPU as service revenue ÷ subscriber-months (within 1%, beyond 3%). Contradicted −1, verified +1 confirmed value, unverified 0, each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`). A KPI line written with a colon ("Monthly churn: 0.9%") stays the T-062 rules' to read.
   - **Rights window** (`telecom-rights-window`): each register row's end date — a sentence in an agreement naming the reference governs over the register, and so does its renewal sentence — measured from the expected closing a document states, else the latest month in the exports. A material agreement (annual value 5% or more of the trailing twelve months of billed, advertising and affiliate revenue) ending before the closing or within twelve months after it, and not renewing automatically, is −1, unpriced: the reviewer prices it or makes a renewal a closing condition. The check waits until documents have been read.
   - **Counterparty concentration** (`telecom-counterparty-concentration`): advertising and affiliate revenue by counterparty over the trailing twelve months; one counterparty at 30% or more of its stream is −1, unpriced.
   - **Content squeeze** (`telecom-content-squeeze`): the P&L's content and programming cost ÷ the year's subscriber-months, rising every year to 1.15× its first year or more, while ARPU from the movements export is flat (within 3%) or falling — −1. A single year cannot show it.
   - **Capex squeeze** (`telecom-capex-squeeze`): capex ÷ the P&L's revenue rising every year to 1.25× its first year or more against flat ARPU — −1. Each squeeze is its own draft (the T-164 lesson).
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `telecom-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses content and programming, network, carriage and retransmission, and marketing and subscriber acquisition.
3. **Directions.** Every rule outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (the T-062 evidence `telecom.subscribers_month` is mapped now too); `apps/api/src/packs/telecom-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds.
4. **The Subscribers tab** (`/deals/:id/telecom`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/telecom`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded (25 agreements and 25 counterparties per stream at most; one row per year). "Run telecom and media checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No content or market judgment.** Documents, dates and arithmetic only; the pack never judges a programme, a market or a counterparty, or whether an agreement will be renewed. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; a claim nothing checks is UNVERIFIED, an agreement with no end date is shown as such.
- **People data (rule 7).** Subscribers appear only as counts by region and plan; nothing reaches a model.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-062 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/telecom-media/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Northlake Fibre) gains subscriber movements from January 2023, the rights register, advertising and affiliate revenue by counterparty, capex and a three-year P&L, the investor presentation (subscribers, churn, ARPU and the closing date) and the Rivermark affiliation agreement, with planted defects (a) the deck's 0.9% churn against 1.5% from the movements, (b) AF-01, the largest affiliate agreement, expiring nine months after the 31 December 2026 closing while the register says 2028, (c) Brightline Motors at 34% of advertising revenue, (d) content cost per subscriber rising across FY2023–FY2025 while ARPU stays flat. The T-062 data and findings are unchanged. `apps/api/src/packs/telecom.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the telecom pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270115000000_telecom_subscribers_rights_concentration` adds four `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: live subscriber-billing, ad-server or rights-management adapters (each a future adapter producing the same records); a model-proposed register for agreements the sentence patterns do not read; cohort retention by plan; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
