# T-120 — Site positioning: say plainly it is the PE / corporate-development deal platform

Model: sonnet
Engine: claude
**Lane:** site
**Context:** light
**Touches:** apps/site (lib/content.ts and every surface that renders the descriptor/hero copy, layout metadata, public/llms.txt)
**Needs:** nothing

## Why

External review (Kumar's PE contact, 2026-11-11): "The web site is not
clear." Kumar's direction (2026-11-11): the site must carry clear
verbiage that this is a PE / corporate-development platform for deal
management and execution.

The current copy never says who it is for or that it runs the whole
deal. In `apps/site/lib/content.ts` today:

- DESCRIPTOR: "Diligence for mergers and acquisitions" (drives the
  eyebrow, the page `<title>`, and the meta description)
- HERO eyebrow: "Diligence for software and SaaS acquisitions" (narrows
  to one vertical, though the packs span twelve industries)
- HOME body: a feature verb list ("reconciles the revenue, reads the
  code and delivery history, cites the contracts and prices what it
  finds") with no audience and no lifecycle

A private-equity or corp-dev reader cannot tell in five seconds that
this is for them, or that it covers deal management and execution end
to end — pipeline, diligence, valuation, terms, and closing — not just
a diligence step.

## What

Rewrite the descriptor-level copy so these three elements are explicit
everywhere the positioning appears:

1. **Audience:** private equity firms and corporate development teams.
2. **Category:** a deal platform — deal management and execution.
3. **Scope:** the full lifecycle (diligence, valuation, terms,
   closing), not diligence alone.

Working line for the descriptor (polish allowed, elements mandatory):
"The deal platform for private equity and corporate development —
manage and execute acquisitions end to end: diligence, valuation,
terms, and closing in one place."

Apply consistently to: the DESCRIPTOR constant and HERO/HOME eyebrow
and body in `apps/site/lib/content.ts`, the layout title/description
metadata that renders them, `apps/site/public/llms.txt` (same
positioning for AI discoverability), and any other page intro that
repeats the old "software and SaaS acquisitions" framing. Drop the
software/SaaS narrowing as the lead frame on the home page.

## Guardrails

- The tagline is locked: "AI rigor. Human judgment." does not change.
  This task is the descriptor/audience copy only.
- Positioning language only: no new feature claims, no invented stats,
  customers, or proof points (standing honesty bounds for growth work).
- Keep the voice: plain, confident, no hype words ("revolutionary",
  "cutting-edge", "supercharge").

## Acceptance

- The home page hero/eyebrow and the meta title + description name
  private equity and corporate development and say deal management /
  execution across the lifecycle, in the first screen and first
  sentence respectively.
- llms.txt carries the same positioning statement.
- No home-page surface still leads with "software and SaaS
  acquisitions" as the frame.
- content.test.ts updated for any copy it pins; site builds green; PR
  includes rendered proof (screenshot or extracted hero/meta text).
