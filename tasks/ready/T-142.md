**Needs note:** a two-party acquisition pack on the existing pack framework, with two evidence families (generation assets; oil & gas interests) sharing one title/environmental core. Nothing open blocks the start. Kumar directed this pack on 2026-11-19.

## Why

Energy assets — coal and gas plants, renewables, and oil & gas mineral, royalty and working interests — trade on evidence Pactlab's machinery handles well: title chains, production and reserve data, contracts, and large, quantifiable liabilities (environmental remediation, decommissioning) that map directly onto price reductions, remediation terms and escrows. The diligence is specialised but highly structured, and no current pack covers it.

## Shape

One pact, two parties: a buyer acquiring energy assets from a seller (utility, independent power producer, E&P company, or mineral/royalty owners). Two evidence families inside one pack, because they share the title and liability core:

- **Generation:** power plants (coal, gas, renewables) and energy businesses — PPAs, interconnection, fuel and transport contracts, emissions and compliance records, plant performance.
- **Oil & gas interests:** mineral, royalty and working interests — leases, division orders, title opinions, production history, reserve reports.

New company type `ENERGY_GENERATION` (label "Energy generation & rights", slug `energy-generation`), released when merged.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Asset and interest register.** Every asset is a register entry: plants (capacity, fuel, age, capacity factor, PPA status) and interests (legal description, lease, working interest / net revenue interest decimals, operator, production history). Built from title opinions, leases, division orders and seller schedules; production and commission data via CSV import targets per ADR 0010.
2. **Reserves and production model** (`packages/calculations`): decline-curve analysis on production history; reserve categories (PDP / PDNP / PUD) carried as the third-party engineering report states them; reserve-based DCF through the standard bridge. The platform never generates its own reserve estimates — engineering figures are third-party reports, cited as such.
3. **Liability model.** Environmental obligations (remediation, coal-ash/CCR, reclamation) and asset-retirement/decommissioning obligations are quantified from the evidence and compared against the seller's booked figures; shortfalls price directly as REMEDIATION_COST, escrow and PRICE_REDUCTION proposals.
4. **Decimal and lease verification.** Division-order NRI decimals recomputed from the lease royalty chain; lease status analysed for held-by-production coverage and expiry inside the hold period.
5. **Extraction.** Prompts in `packages/ai/src/prompts/` read title opinions, leases, PPAs and reserve reports, proposing register entries and terms under the standard grounding rule (a number is kept only if it appears in its cited quote). Humans confirm; only confirmed entries feed the math.

## Finding rules (registered in the pack)

1. **Unbooked environmental liability:** remediation or CCR obligations evidenced in reports/permits but absent from the seller's liability schedule → drag, quantified; feeds REMEDIATION_COST and escrow proposals.
2. **ARO shortfall:** the evidence-based decommissioning estimate exceeds the seller's booked asset-retirement obligation by a material margin → drag; feeds an escrow proposal.
3. **Decline mismatch:** actual production decline is materially steeper than the reserve report's assumption → drag on the affected interests.
4. **Lease expiry risk:** material acreage/value not held by production and expiring inside the hold period → drag, quantified as the exposed share.
5. **Royalty decimal error:** a division-order NRI differs from the recomputed lease chain → finding with the over/underpayment direction and size.
6. **PPA cliff:** a plant's PPA expires inside the hold period, leaving merchant exposure → finding quantifying the contracted-vs-merchant revenue share.
7. **Title defect:** a break in the mineral or asset chain of title in the title opinion → drag; feeds a cure-period / holdback term proposal.
8. **Compliance capex gap:** emissions or environmental controls required by permit/regulation are missing from the seller's capex plan → drag, quantified from the evidence.

Valuation: reserve-based DCF for interests and $/kW plus contract coverage for plants, through the standard bridge and personas. Verified cushions (clean title, over-booked ARO, contracted revenue) go to the confirmed-value ledger only, per the standing asymmetry.

## Guardrails

- **Diligence support only.** Not legal, engineering or regulatory advice; the pack makes no title determination and certifies no reserves — it organises evidence and flags gaps for the parties and their counsel/engineers.
- **No invented figures.** Production, reserves and liability numbers come only from reports, permits and adapters; a missing report is an explicit gap finding.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is written as available, in the same style as the other pack pages (standing Kumar posture), with the diligence-support guardrail wording.
