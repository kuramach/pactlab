# T-185 — History of any length: use every year provided, and never ask for years before the business existed

Engine: claude
Model: opus
Lane: core
Context: deep
Touches: packages/contracts packages/domain packages/db packages/calculations apps/api apps/web apps/site scripts/demo-data docs tasks
Needs: T-184b
Checks: pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test && pnpm test:integration && pnpm test:fixtures && pnpm test:e2e

## Why
Kumar (2026-11-27): *"I can have 20 years of history for a company or 2 years or 5 years. All possibilities supported in the pack right?"*

**Today** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history.ts`, `multifamily/history.ts`, ADR 0057):
- **The window is fixed:** `HISTORY_YEARS = 5`. Every year imports and is stored, but metrics, trends and findings read only the latest five. Fifteen of twenty years count for nothing.
- **Missing years are asked for even when they never existed:** "the 3 missing years" are requested from the seller even when the company was founded two years ago.

## What changes (an addendum to ADR 0057)
### 1. Five years is the minimum expected, not the maximum
Every complete fiscal year provided is read, with no cap below a sanity limit of 30 years. Per year it stays as built: stated years read as stated; months need all twelve; the newer import wins; withdrawn imports never count.

**Metrics over the full history**, alongside the existing five-year ones, each with its coverage:
- **Long-run growth:** a 10-year CAGR when 10 or more years exist, and a full-history CAGR.
- **The worst year:** the largest year-on-year fall in revenue and NOI, or the pack's key driver, and the years to recover to the prior peak ("not yet recovered" when it hasn't).
- **Through-cycle averages:** margin and the pack's key driver (e.g. occupancy) averaged over the full history, beside the last five years.

**Trend findings:**
- **Unchanged:** the existing rules stay on the latest five years, which is the period a buyer prices on.
- **New:** **"the recent run is better than the long run"**, a draft finding when the last 3–5 years' margin or driver average sits well above the full-history average, so the reviewer asks whether the price assumes the peak will last. Thresholds are named constants in `docs/agent/packs.md`.

### 2. Never ask for years before the business existed
- **A history start on the pact** (a new nullable `history_start_year`, expand-only, audited): the year the business started, or the property was built or acquired, or the earliest year the seller can provide.
  - **Set by:** the deal lead or analyst, in Finances or the pact's settings, or prefilled from a pack field that already holds it (e.g. year built), where one exists.
  - **Asked:** once, as part of the existing ask-the-seller flow: *"What's the first year this business traded?"*
- **The expected years** become *max(latest − 4, history start) … latest*:
  - **A company with 2 years since its start:** "2 of 2 years: complete". No ask, no finding.
  - **With no history start set:** behaves as today (asks for five), and the ask says *"…or tell us the business started later"*.
- **A gap inside the history,** e.g. FY2019 missing from FY2010–FY2026, is asked for as today.

### 3. On screen
- **The History strip:** shows up to the last 10 years; the full history opens in the years table.
- **The years table:** scrolls horizontally beyond about 10 years, using the shared components (`docs/ui-patterns.md`). No new look.
- **Coverage wording:**
  - "FY2007–FY2026 (20 years)";
  - "FY2025–FY2026 (2 of 2 years since the business started)";
  - "FY2022–FY2026 (5 years; earlier years not provided)".

### 4. Demo data
In `kuramach/pactlab-demo-data`, with the pin bumped:
- **Long history:** give **one pack** (multifamily, USD/EUR/GBP) 20 years of annual history, FY2007–FY2026. Plant a 2009 downturn with a multi-year recovery, and a recent run well above the long-run average, so the new finding fires.
- **Young company:** give **one pack** (the venture raise or software) a business that started two years ago, with its history start set, so "2 of 2: complete" is demonstrated.
- **Everything else keeps its five years.**

## Rules
Deterministic, decimal-only, cited to the imported rows, versioned calculators (bump the version), and no AI reading any value (ADR 0042).

## Acceptance
- **20 years imported:** every year is read; 10-year and full-history CAGR, the worst year, its recovery and the through-cycle averages match hand-computed values; the five-year metrics are unchanged.
- **2 years with a history start two years back:** coverage is complete, with no ask and no missing-year finding. Without a history start set, it asks as today.
- **A gap year inside a long history:** asked for, and only that year.
- **"The recent run is better than the long run":** fires on the planted 20-year demo data and stays quiet on a flat history. A re-run adds nothing.
- **Screen:** the History strip shows the last 10 years and the table scrolls to all of them. The existing multifamily e2e is extended if it doesn't cover this already.
