# T-093 — Reseller program: attribution, declining commissions, payouts, and the reseller portal
Engine: claude

**Lane:** platform
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** api, domain, web, admin, site, spec
**Needs:** T-087 (billing, checkout, entitlements — attribution and discount hooks live there). Money mechanics build on T-087's Stripe stack in test mode; live payouts gated like T-087's go-live.

## Kumar's program (2026-10-27)

Resellers resell Pactlab for commission on the revenue from customers they bring:

- **Commission schedule (per customer, by customer-year): Year 1: 15% — Year 2: 10% — Year 3: 5%.** After year 3, no commission on that customer. Rates are **config data** (like T-087's price book), never code, never public.
- **Customer inducement:** a customer who signs up through a reseller gets **10% off their first year** (applied to year-1 charges: per-pact purchases and subscription).
- **Key resellers:** equity participation for long-term alignment (Part 6 — framework here, counsel papers it).
- **Public page:** generic partner marketing — **zero commission or discount numbers anywhere public** (page, llms.txt, SEO copy — T-085 surfaces included). The numbers live only in the authenticated reseller agreement and portal.
- **Definitions (pinned here so the build doesn't guess):** "revenue" = amounts actually **collected** from the tagged customer, net of the 10% discount and of refunds. Customer-years run from that customer's first collected payment. Commissions accrue on collection, never on booking.

## Part 1 — Attribution (how a customer becomes a reseller's)

- **Promo code:** every approved reseller gets a unique code (human-readable, theirs to share) plus a **referral link** carrying the same tag. The customer enters the code at registration/checkout (T-087 surfaces) or arrives via the link; either way the org is tagged to the reseller at signup.
- **Discount mechanics:** a valid tag applies the 10% first-year discount automatically at checkout — the customer sees it priced in, never a post-hoc rebate.
- **Collision and lock rules:** the first valid tag wins and locks for the customer's commission life (3 years); a second code cannot re-tag. Deal registration (Part 5) can pre-tag a prospect before signup. Admin can reassign with a stated reason — audited, and the reseller sees it happen (no silent re-tags).
- **Integrity rules:** a reseller never earns on their own org(s) or on orgs owned by their principals (self-dealing check at tagging and at payout); codes are not transferable; tagging a customer who was already in active direct negotiation is an admin decision, not an automatic steal (the deal-registration timestamp is the evidence).

## Part 2 — Commission engine

- Per tagged customer, per collected payment: commission accrues at the rate for that customer's current year (15/10/5), into a **commission ledger** with states: accrued (in clearance) -> payable -> paid; reversed on refund/chargeback (**clawback**: paid-out commissions are recovered against future payouts; a reseller balance can go negative and is shown as such).
- **Clearance window:** a commission becomes payable only after the underlying payment has cleared a configured refund-risk window (default 30 days). Statements per period (monthly) itemize customer, charge, rate, amount, state.
- Everything is computed from Stripe events via T-087's mirror — the ledger never trusts a browser or a spreadsheet.

## Part 3 — Reseller portal (their admin page — post-login, reseller role)

- **Dashboard:** tagged customers (commerce metadata only: org name, plan/pacts, amounts, dates — **never** the customer's diligence content, findings, or documents; that boundary is absolute), sales totals by period, the commission ledger by state, year-rate per customer shown plainly ("Customer X: Year 1 — 15% until Mar 2027").
- **Payouts:** the reseller connects their bank via **Stripe Connect** onboarding inside the portal (KYC, tax forms, and bank details live with Stripe — Pactlab never stores bank accounts or tax IDs). "Transfer" = a payout of the payable balance to their connected account, self-serve above a configured minimum, with payout history and statements downloadable.
- **Assets:** their promo code + referral link with copy buttons, the co-marketing kit (logos, one-pagers, case-study links from T-092), their own demo org (pilotMode) to show the product, and partner training access (T-090 series).
- **Agreement:** the program terms (with the actual numbers) are accepted in-portal at registration and versioned; acceptance is recorded. A rate change applies to future attributions; the portal shows the schedule the reseller actually agreed to.

## Part 4 — Public page and application

- `/resellers` on the marketing site: who the program is for (advisory firms, consultants, ecosystem partners), what partners get (a product worth reselling, demo access, co-marketing, a real portal — described without numbers), how it works in three steps, and the **application form**.
- Application -> admin queue (Part 5) -> approval issues the code, portal access, and agreement. Rejection is a status, communicated; no code exists before approval.

## Part 5 — Admin and program ops (T-072 console)

- Reseller applications (approve/reject/request info), reseller list with tier, attribution overview, reassignment, code reset/revocation, ledger + payout oversight, fraud review flags (self-dealing attempts, code-abuse patterns, refund-rate outliers).
- **Deal registration:** a reseller can register a named prospect; if approved, that prospect is pre-tagged for a configured window (default 90 days) even if they sign up without the code. Admin approves/denies with the timestamp trail — this is what stops direct-sale poaching disputes before they start.
- **Termination rules (in the agreement + enforced here):** on voluntary exit or termination without cause, commissions on already-tagged customers continue to term (the 3-year schedule) — alignment shouldn't be a hostage; on termination for cause (fraud, brand abuse), trailing commissions stop. Status changes are admin actions, audited.

## Part 6 — Key-reseller equity (framework only)

- A **strategic tier** in the data model: tier flag, equity grant reference (instrument, shares/options, vesting schedule — recorded, not computed), and the criteria slot Kumar fills (volume/strategic value).
- **Hard boundary:** the system records and displays grants Kumar and counsel have actually approved; it never promises, calculates eligibility for, or communicates equity to a reseller on its own. The public page and portal say "strategic partners" exist only in the generic language Kumar approves. Vesting design (e.g., 4-year, partnership-contingent) is counsel's paper, noted in the program doc as his decision.

## Guardrails

- No commission/discount numbers in any public artifact (site, llms.txt, emails to prospects). A test greps the public surfaces for the figures.
- Resellers see commerce metadata about their tagged customers — nothing about deals, findings, or documents, ever.
- No bank details or tax IDs on Pactlab servers (Stripe Connect holds them); payouts only from collected, cleared revenue; every ledger movement audited.
- Equity: no system-generated promises (see Part 6).

## Acceptance (Stripe test mode throughout)

- End-to-end: apply -> admin approves -> code issued -> customer signs up with code -> 10% applied at checkout -> payment collected -> 15% accrues -> after clearance window, payable -> reseller connects via Stripe Connect test onboarding -> payout transfers; statement itemizes it all.
- Renewal into customer-year 2 accrues at 10%, year 3 at 5%, year 4 at 0% (test clocks).
- Refund on a year-1 purchase reverses the accrual; a post-payout refund creates a negative balance recovered from the next payout.
- Self-dealing: a reseller's own org tagged with their code earns nothing and flags for admin.
- Deal registration: an approved prospect signing up without a code is tagged to the registering reseller within the window, and not after it.
- Public-surface test: the figures 15/10/5 (as commission rates) and the customer-discount terms do not appear on /resellers, llms.txt, or other public copy.
