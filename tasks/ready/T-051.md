# T-051 — Website: separate industry pages for the five new pact packs

Status: ready
Needs: T-050
Touches: apps/site tasks
Lane: web-ux

- The five new packs from T-050 (hotels, multifamily, commercial property,
  hospitals, small clinics) get their own pages on the marketing site,
  following the exact pattern of the existing industry pages — no new page
  design. The `[slug]` route already exists
  (`apps/site/app/industries/[slug]/page.tsx`); this task is content.
- One `INDUSTRY_STORIES` entry per pack in `apps/site/lib/industry-stories.ts`,
  slugs: `hotels`, `multifamily`, `commercial-property`, `hospitals`,
  `small-clinics`. Each entry complete, in the file's established shape:
  headline, subhead, illustrationAlt, pains (icons from `apps/site/lib/icons.ts`),
  walkthrough of the evidence → finding → valuation → term loop, example
  findings (labelled illustrative on the page, as the existing ones are),
  deliverables, faq.
- Voice rules are the file's own: direct, no hype, no invented statistics or
  customer claims. Content mirrors T-050's pack definitions — same evidence
  sources, finding types and valuation drivers; nothing promised beyond the
  pack spec. Because these packs are planned (sequenced after the baseline,
  like the other deferred packs), the faq uses the existing `PLANNED_FAQ`
  pattern — planned means labelled planned, same as the other five deferred
  packs on the site today.
- One illustration per pack in `apps/site/public/illustrations/<slug>.svg`,
  drawn in the same style as the existing industry illustrations.
- The industries index (`apps/site/app/industries/page.tsx`) lists all
  eleven packs; any other enumeration of industries on the site (nav,
  footer, cross-links) is updated to match.
- Tests: extend the site content tests — every story slug resolves to a page
  and a committed illustration file, and the index lists every slug (a pack
  added to the record but missing from the index fails the build).
