# T-045 — Professional iconography: module marks on the site and app empty states

Status: active
Needs: none
Touches: apps/site apps/web packages/ui tasks
Lane: web-ux

## Decision (revised 2026-10-21 — replaces the 3dicons plan)

- The founder dropped 3D icons: PE and corporate-development buyers expect a
  professional, secure-SaaS look. Use **Lucide line icons only** (already in
  both apps, ISC licence, no new dependency, nothing bought or downloaded).
- One treatment, the **mark**: a white line icon (stroke 1.75) on a rounded
  navy-to-indigo gradient tile (#081127 → #4a58e8). Used where a module or
  industry is introduced and on workspace empty states.
- Decorative only: `aria-hidden`, always beside a visible text label.
- Placement: site module cards (home, product, product detail "other
  modules"), industry cards (industries list, industry detail "other
  industries"); app empty states for deals, sources and evidence.
  Not in tables, nav, buttons, tabs or error/access states — those keep the
  existing soft tiles or plain text. Brand logos (T-042) stay flat SVG.
- Every module and industry has its own distinct mark.

## Work

- Site: `ModuleMark` in `components/icon.tsx`; swap it into the module and
  industry cards. Content test: every module and industry mark is registered
  and distinct.
- UI: `Mark` and an optional `icon` prop on `EmptyState`.
- Web: `deals/_lib/empty-marks.tsx` registry (deals, sources, evidence) with
  a test; wire the three empty states.
