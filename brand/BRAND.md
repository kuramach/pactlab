# Pactlab brand kit

Hand this directory to Claude Code when building the marketing site or any brand surface.

## Identity
- Name: Pactlab (one word; lowercase in the wordmark)
- Tagline: "Test the pact before you sign it."
- Pillar: "AI diligence. Human instinct."
- Story: a pact is a promise; a lab is where promises get tested.

## Logos (`logos/`)
- `pactlab-logo-keeper.webp` — primary wordmark for light backgrounds. Lowercase "pactlab": "pact" in deep navy bold, "lab" in indigo; the "b" bowl is a flask with a rising bubble.
- `pactlab-logo-keeper-light.webp` — reversed wordmark for dark/navy backgrounds.
- `pactlab-symbol-keeper.webp` — standalone symbol: deep-navy round-bottom flask with indigo liquid, white business handshake submerged (the pact being tested in the lab). Use for favicons, avatars, app icons, social.

## Colors
- Deep navy `#081127` — primary text, headlines, dark surfaces
- Indigo `#6978ff` — accent: the "lab" in the wordmark, CTAs, highlights, links
- White `#ffffff` — text on dark, light surfaces
- Light section backgrounds: `#f8faff`, `#f7f9fc`

## Typography
- Manrope (sans-serif) throughout. Bold for headlines and the wordmark; regular for body.

## Voice
- Direct, expert, zero hype. The machine does the diligence; the human makes the call.
- Never: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "supercharge", or anything that sounds like a pitch deck.
