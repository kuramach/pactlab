# T-171 — Marketing site in Japanese, German and French (English for US, UK and India)

Engine: claude

## Why

Kumar's target markets (2026-11-26): US, UK, Germany, France, Japan and India. The marketing site is the front door in each: German, French and Japanese versions, on top of the existing English (which serves the US, UK and India — Indian deal business is conducted in English; India gets INR product support via T-170, not a Hindi site). Marketing posture unchanged: every page written as available, no invented claims, in every language.

## Build

1. **Locale routing and framework** in apps/site: `/de`, `/fr`, `/ja` prefixes with English unprefixed as today; a message-catalog setup consistent with what T-172 will use in the app (same library and conventions — coordinate via the catalog format, do not invent a second system); a visible language switcher; correct `lang` attributes, hreflang tags and per-locale metadata for SEO (builds on T-085's discoverability work when that lands; do not block on it).
2. **Full surface translation:** home, how-it-works, guides, FAQ, compare, about, case-studies shell, and all 17 industry pages including their walkthroughs, example findings and FAQs. The locked tagline "AI rigor. Human judgment." is translated once, recorded in the task PR, and used consistently (Kumar approves the three renderings at review).
3. **Translation method and honesty:** translations are produced in the build (AI translation) and each locale PR carries a native-review checklist; pages ship behind their locale route only when the checklist is complete for that locale. No locale is linked from the switcher before its checklist passes.
4. **Automatic country detection (Kumar, 2026-11-26):** a first-time visitor is routed to their market's locale automatically, and can always override it. Implement at the edge — the site is CloudFront + S3, so use the `CloudFront-Viewer-Country` header in a CloudFront Function on viewer requests (no third-party geo-IP service, no client-side lookup call). Rules: mapping DE/AT/CH → de, FR/BE → fr, JP → ja, everything else → English (US, UK, India and the rest of the world); detection applies only when the URL carries no locale prefix and only on entry/section-root paths, redirecting (302, path-preserving) to the same page in the detected locale; **a manual switcher choice is stored in a cookie and always wins** — once set, detection never overrides it, and the cookie also suppresses repeat redirects; detection never fires for a URL that already has a locale prefix (no loops, no double redirects). The detected country also pre-selects the country on the pilot/contact form. SEO: 302 (never 301) for geo redirects so no locale becomes canonical by accident, hreflang remains the indexing source of truth, and crawlers following hreflang are unaffected.
5. **Layout proof:** screenshots per locale for home + two industry pages (German length, Japanese line-breaking) in the PR; no clipped or overflowing text.
