## Why
T-184c gave hospitals and commercial property their five years of history (ADR 0057, its T-184c addendum). Hotels was left out because its rebuild (T-166) was still in `tasks/ready`: adding history to the old pack would collide with the rebuild's demo data and rules. Once T-166 lands, do hotels the same way, with the shared module (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history.ts`, `docs/agent/packs.md` §4d).

## Scope
- **Hotels:** occupancy (rooms sold ÷ rooms available), ADR (room revenue ÷ rooms sold) and RevPAR (room revenue ÷ rooms available) by year from `HOTELS_OPERATING_HISTORY`, beside the P&L by year; History strips on the matching figures; a driver trend rule where a reviewer would want one (RevPAR or occupancy falling every year).
- Five years of P&L and operating history in the demo data (all currencies, own random generator), one or two planted trend defects in the README, and the industry page (with de/fr/ja entries) stating the history it reads.

## Acceptance
As T-184: a five-year history imports deterministically; history metrics match hand-computed values; each planted defect surfaces as a cited draft; partial history states its coverage and asks the seller.
