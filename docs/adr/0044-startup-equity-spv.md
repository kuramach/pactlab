# ADR 0044: Startup equity SPV: a pooled-vehicle pack, investor seats behind a data-layer wall, accreditation tracked not built (T-141)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-19; scope widened 2026-11-20).

## Context
- Much early-stage investing happens through a special-purpose vehicle: an organizer pools many investors into one (usually Delaware) vehicle that takes one line on a startup's cap table for one round.
- The investors' diligence problem is double — the startup's round and the vehicle's own economics — and its failure modes are checkable: cap-table math, SAFE terms, fee and carry that differ between the summary and the operating documents, side letters, accreditation gaps.
- Venture Raise (T-083, ADR 0012) is the founder's side of a round; Multifamily syndication (T-106, ADR 0040) is a real-estate sponsor raise. Neither covers the pooled-vehicle seat. T-106's investor side (its PR 2: investor seats, the published investor view) was never built, so this pack builds those shared pieces, generically, following the patterns ADR 0040 set out.

## Decision
1. **A new company type, `STARTUP_SPV`** (label "Startup equity SPV", slug `startup-spv`, cluster `capital`), released on merge. One pact, three parties, two walled directions: the organizer opens a buyer-led pact (the organizer is the pact's own side); the startup is the seller (a target contributor) and sees the vehicle as a single investor; investors are seated as guests.
2. **The startup's evidence is Venture Raise's, by reference.** The registry entry reads `VENTURE_RAISE.importTargets` (billing, the cap table with its SAFEs and notes, monthly cash and burn); nothing is copied. Six intake categories are offered by this pack only: round summary or deck, SAFE or convertible note, SPV operating agreement, SPV investor summary, side letter, Form D.
3. **Documents are read deterministically** (`@pactlab/calculations` `packs/spv/documents.ts`): each document is recognised by its heading, and each figure is read from one sentence by a fixed pattern and carries that sentence as its citation, which the API verifies on the page. No model reads these: a figure no sentence states is absent, never estimated. A model-proposed, person-confirmed register (the `offering.extract` / `music.extract` pattern) is a follow-up for documents the patterns do not read.
4. **Round math** (`spv-round-check-1`, decimal only, conventions returned with every result): pre-round shares from the cap table; each SAFE or note converted at the lowest of its cap price (cap ÷ pre-round shares), the discounted round price and the round price, MFN instruments taking the best later terms (Venture Raise's `effectiveTerms`); new shares × price against the stated round size; price × pre-round shares against the stated pre-money; the recomputed post-round share count against the stated one, naming the instruments whose conversion is exactly the difference. Pro-rata rights recorded per instrument.
5. **Vehicle economics** (`spv-economics-1`): the management fee, carry, expense cap and organizer commitment from the operating agreement (which governs), set against the investor summary; the fee load recomputed on total commitments (the active seats plus the organizer's commitment). Net-of-fees return scenarios on exit paths the organizer states (multiples of the round's post-money), each labelled "Scenario computed from stated assumptions — not a projection or promise". No later dilution, preference stack, hurdle or tax is modelled, and the result says so.
6. **Finding rules** (drafts only, cited, `spv-*` version 1): cap-table mismatch, round-size mismatch and pre-money (VERIFIED or CONTRADICTED) — drags; instrument terms against the summary's prior-round claim (CONTRADICTED drag, VERIFIED cushion, UNVERIFIED gap with no signed instrument); the round claims register (ARR and MRR against billing, runway against cash and burn; retention, margin and customer claims UNVERIFIED gaps); fee or carry mismatch — a drag; an undisclosed side letter granting economics — organizer-side, neutral; no Form D filing evidence — a gap, neutral; and, run by the SPV service over the seats, an accreditation gap in a 506(c) vehicle (or an investor recorded as not accredited in a 506(b) one) — organizer-side, neutral. Vehicle disclosure defects never change the round's price.
7. **Claims may be UNVERIFIED.** A rule's claim (`RuleClaim`, `PackRuleProposal.claim`) may now be UNVERIFIED as well as VERIFIED or CONTRADICTED: a register claim with no underlying document is a gap the claims register shows, for every pack.
8. **Investor seats** (generic, reusable by T-106): a new deal role `INVESTOR` and `pact_investors` rows (label, pseudonymous reference, commitment, tracked accreditation status, a specialist's link and an evidence pointer); a membership names its seat (`deal_memberships.investor_id`, the BIDDER/`bid_id` pattern of ADR 0015) and so does the invitation. `INVESTOR` holds only `DEAL_READ` and is a counterparty (`isCounterpartyRole`); `app_is_own_side`, `app_is_buyer_side` and `app_is_buyer_analyst` exclude it, so every own-side policy closes to investors with no new rule.
9. **The wall, in the data layer.**
   - Investors never see each other: an investor's membership is visible only to the own side and to people on the same seat (`deal_memberships_select`, with `app_current_investor_id`), and an investor reads only their own seat row. The startup never sees the investor list.
   - An investor reads none of the pact's material: restrictive policies on `connections`, `evidence_items`, `documents`, `document_pages`, `document_sources`, `source_uploads` and `import_formats` refuse every row of a pact where the caller is seated as an investor (`app_investor_deal_ids()`), even what the startup shared.
   - The published investor view (`investor_view_versions`) is a versioned, frozen snapshot: a CHECK allows only the allow-listed top-level sections (the claims register, the cap-table verification, the vehicle economics check and the return scenarios, plus their meta), a trigger refuses any change, and only the deal lead publishes. The builder never reads seats, side letters, findings, terms or notes, so none of them can enter it; one investor's terms never appear in another's view.
10. **Accreditation is tracked, not built** (best-in-class rule): Pactlab records what an outside verification specialist found and determines nothing. The checks flag gaps for the organizer and counsel.
11. **Guardrail.** Diligence support only: not legal advice; no securities-offering, broker-dealer or investment-adviser function; no accreditation determination. Shown on the SPV page, in the investor view and on the site.

## Consequences
- The SPV tab (`/deals/:id/spv`) is the organizer's; an investor's pact opens on the same path as their own seat and the latest published view, with no deal tabs. Seats and published versions are keyset-paged (T-126).
- Investor guests in API tests are organization members holding only the INVESTOR deal role (as bidders are in the sale tests); real guest isolation under RLS is proven in `packages/db/src/spv.test.ts`.
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/startup-spv/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Halyard Syndicate pooling five investors into a ~$2M vehicle for Lumen Labs' seed round) with planted defects (a) the cap table off by the SAFE conversion, (b) carry 20% against 15%, (c) an undisclosed side letter waiving one investor's fee, (d) one 506(c) investor only self-certified. `apps/api/src/packs/startup-spv.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end with the wall tests.
- **Migrations** `20270105000000_investor_role` (enum values, on their own) and `20270105000100_startup_spv` are expand-only. Rollback: drop `investor_view_versions`, `pact_investors`, `spv_vehicles`, the `investor_id` columns and the new functions and policies, and restore `app_is_own_side`, `app_is_buyer_side`, `app_is_buyer_analyst`, `deal_memberships_select`, the `deal_invitations` checks and `app_accept_deal_invitation` from `20261114000100_seller_led_sale`. The enum values stay unused.
- Follow-ups: T-106's investor side can reuse `INVESTOR`, `pact_investors` and the published-view table as they are; a model-proposed register for SPV documents the patterns do not read.
