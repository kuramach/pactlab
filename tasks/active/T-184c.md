## Why

Once they land, give them the five years of history every pack expects (ADR 0057 and its T-184 addendum), using the shared module (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history.ts`, `docs/agent/packs.md` §4d).

## Per pack
- **Hospitals:** admissions, average daily census and payer mix by year from the census and payer-mix exports.
- **Commercial property:** occupancy, average rent per square foot and lease expiries by year (the multifamily occupancy machinery where it fits, by reference).
- **Hotels:** occupancy, ADR and RevPAR by year from the operating history.

Each: five years of P&L and drivers in the demo data (all currencies, own random generator), one or two planted trend defects in the README, the shared History strip and years table, and the industry page (with de/fr/ja entries) stating the history it reads.
