# T-050 — Five new pact packs: hotels, multifamily, commercial property, hospitals, small clinics

Status: ready
Needs: none
Touches: docs apps/site tasks
Lane: packs

- Add five industry packs in the spec §19 pack format (each pack = evidence
  adapters + finding types + valuation tweaks on the unchanged
  findings→valuation core; no core changes). Wherever packs are enumerated in
  content or code (site industries today mirror the existing six), add the
  five consistently. Like the existing packs, these are definitions sequenced
  after the tech-M&A baseline — no full adapter builds in this task.
- **Hotels (hospitality)** — Evidence: PMS/STR reports, franchise & brand
  agreements, management contracts, occupancy/ADR/RevPAR history, F&B P&Ls.
  Findings: deferred brand PIP capex, franchise encumbrance/termination
  windows, demand seasonality/concentration. Valuation: RevPAR & GOP-margin
  trends; PIP capex lands as remediation/price terms (−1 drag).
- **Multifamily** — Evidence: rent roll, T-12, lease files, delinquency
  reports, utility bills, unit-condition inspections. Findings: loss-to-lease,
  deferred maintenance per door, delinquency concentration. Valuation: NOI /
  cap rate; loss-to-lease upside is a +1 cushion (confirmed-value ledger,
  never a price increase); capex per door as remediation.
- **Commercial property** (office/retail/industrial) — Evidence: lease
  abstracts, rent roll, CAM reconciliations, Phase I environmental, zoning.
  Findings: tenant concentration, near-term rollover vs WALT, above/below-
  market leases, environmental flags. Valuation: WALT and rollover schedule,
  cap-rate sensitivity.
- **Hospitals** — Evidence: payer mix, census/ADC, Medicare cost reports,
  licensure & accreditation, malpractice history. Findings: payer
  concentration, licensure gaps, key-physician dependence. Valuation:
  payer-mix-adjusted EBITDA, regulatory capex.
- **Small clinics** — Evidence: provider contracts, patient volume, billing/
  RCM reports, equipment schedules, compliance (HIPAA/Stark). Findings:
  provider concentration, coding/billing risk, aged equipment. Valuation:
  revenue per provider, post-close retention risk.
- Relationships: hospitals and small clinics extend the existing healthcare
  services pack with a real-estate + operations hybrid profile; hotels,
  multifamily and commercial property form a new real-estate cluster.
- Proposed sequence (Kumar may re-rank): multifamily first (cleanest data —
  rent roll/T-12 map almost 1:1 onto existing billing imports), then
  commercial property, hotels, small clinics, hospitals (regulatory weight
  ascending).
- Tests: if any machine-readable pack registry exists, a consistency test
  that all eleven packs (six existing + five new) are present with
  adapters, finding types and valuation notes non-empty.
