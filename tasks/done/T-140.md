## Why

Music catalogs (publishing and master recording rights) are a mature alternative-asset deal type — funds and catalog acquirers buy royalty streams the way PE buys cash flows. The diligence is document-heavy and error-prone in specific, checkable ways: chain of title, writer splits, royalty accounting, and US copyright termination rights. That is exactly the shape Pactlab's evidence → findings → valuation machinery is built for, and no current pack covers it.

## Shape

One pact, two parties: a buyer (fund, catalog acquirer, label) acquiring rights from a seller (artist, estate, publisher, label, or a prior fund). The seller is a TARGET_CONTRIBUTOR guest, as in the other acquisition packs. New company type `MUSIC_RIGHTS` (label "Music rights", slug `music-rights`), registered by spreading an existing two-party pack entry where the registry pattern allows, released in `RELEASED_COMPANY_TYPES` and `RELEASED_INDUSTRY_SLUGS` when merged.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Works and rights register.** Every work/recording in the deal is a register entry: title, writers and splits, publisher(s), PRO affiliation, ISRC/IPI identifiers, grant/assignment chain, and the income it produced. Built from Copyright Office records, split sheets, publishing/admin agreements and royalty statements; CSV import targets per ADR 0010 for statement data.
2. **Royalty income model** (`packages/calculations`): trailing income by work and by source (performance, mechanical, sync, neighbouring rights, streaming) over the statement history, with decay analysis by catalog age. All arithmetic deterministic; figures only from statements and adapters — absence is shown as absence, never estimated silently.
3. **Termination exposure analysis.** For US works, compute the §203/§304 termination windows from grant dates: income whose underlying grants become terminable inside the buyer's hold period is quantified separately and priced as its own line in the bridge.
4. **Extraction.** A prompt in `packages/ai/src/prompts/` reads royalty statements, split sheets and publishing agreements, proposing register entries and terms with the standard grounding rule (a number is kept only if it appears in its cited quote). Humans confirm; only confirmed entries feed the math.

## Finding rules (registered in the pack)

1. **Chain-of-title gap:** a work producing income with no assignment/registration evidence behind it → drag; feeds a title holdback/escrow term proposal.
2. **Split mismatch:** splits on a work do not sum to 100%, or statement splits differ from the split sheet → drag.
3. **Termination exposure:** grants over a material share of trailing income become terminable within the hold period → drag, quantified as the exposed income share; feeds PRICE_REDUCTION on that share.
4. **Concentration:** the top work (or top writer) exceeds a stated share of trailing income → risk finding with the share.
5. **Recoupment mismatch:** the seller's stated unrecouped advance balance differs from the publisher/label ledger → drag.
6. **Royalty leakage:** registered works with no corresponding PRO/mechanical income where comparable works earn → gap finding.
7. **Undisclosed encumbrance:** a lien/UCC or admin claim in the evidence that the deal summary does not disclose → drag; feeds an INDEMNITY proposal.

Valuation: royalty-multiple based (net publisher's share / net label share) on the trailing, decay-adjusted income, through the standard bridge and personas. Cushions (verified growth, clean title across the register) go to the confirmed-value ledger only, per the standing asymmetry.

## Guardrails

- **Diligence support only.** Not legal advice; the pack makes no determination that title is good or that a termination notice is valid — it organises evidence and flags gaps for the parties and their counsel.
- **No invented income.** Royalty history comes only from statements, registries and adapters; a missing statement period is an explicit gap finding.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is written as available, in the same style as the other pack pages (standing Kumar posture), with the diligence-support guardrail wording.
