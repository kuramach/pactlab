## Why

A hotel's price rests on its flag and its comp-set performance. The franchise agreement's PIP and termination terms, and the STR report behind the RevPAR claim, are documents no current pack reads.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Franchise register:** the franchise agreement's term, termination windows and fees as evidence; the property-improvement-plan (PIP) obligations extracted and priced into the bridge as findings.
2. **Performance claims register:** RevPAR, ADR, occupancy and comp-set index claims mapped to the STR/performance reports supplied — VERIFIED / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED.
3. **Management contract:** the operator agreement's term, fees and termination rights as evidence; terms that survive a sale are findings.
4. **Trend alignment:** occupancy/ADR and fee lines across the imported P&L years; franchise and brand fees growing faster than rooms revenue is a trend finding; monthly seasonality shown where monthly statements are imported.

## Guardrails

- **No valuation or brand judgment:** documents, dates and arithmetic; the pack never opines on the flag's worth.
- **Evidence and absence:** figures come only from documents and adapters; absence is shown as absence, never filled in.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is already written as available; verify it against the built pack and flag mismatches in the PR.
