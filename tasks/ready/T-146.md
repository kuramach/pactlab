## Why
- **The need:** near signing and closing, a counter-proposal or a waiting signature should reach the person within minutes, even when they aren't at their desk.
- **The basis:** T-145 builds the events, recipients, urgency and preferences. This task adds two phone channels for **"act now" events only**.

## Content rule (both channels)
- **Content-free:** a phone notification shows on a lock screen, and messaging apps are outside Pactlab's control. Every message is content-free, e.g. "Pactlab: an action is waiting for you on a pact. Open Pactlab to see it.", with a link that requires sign-in.
- **What must never appear:** a company or pact name, a counterparty, an amount, terms, or any document or people data.
- **Testing:** a test asserts that the payload builders can't include them.

## Part 1: web push (no cost, no carrier registration)
- **The app:** Pactlab becomes installable (a web app manifest and a service worker, scoped to notifications).
- **Subscribing:** Settings → Notifications → "Alerts on this device" subscribes the browser with the standard Push API and VAPID keys. Keys go in Secrets Manager per environment, wired through `infra/cdk` like the other provider keys, with a `scripts/*-key.sh` helper.
- **Platforms:** iPhone support requires the app added to the home screen (iOS 16.4 or later). Say so on the settings screen.
- **Lifecycle:** subscriptions are per device, can be revoked, and expired ones are removed when the push service says so.
- **Verification first:** the Web Push protocol, the VAPID header and the iOS limits are checked against current official docs (MDN, WebKit) before code. Nothing is assumed.

## Part 2: Telegram and WhatsApp (Kumar, 2026-11-26: "SMS cost money but WhatsApp, telegram does not and so we support that")
- **No SMS.** It costs per message, so it's dropped.
- **Contact details come from T-180:** the person's verified Telegram link and WhatsApp number on their profile, with consent already recorded there.
- **Channels, for "act now" events only:**
  - **Telegram:** free, and on for anyone who has linked it.
  - **WhatsApp:** Meta charges per business-initiated message, so it stays off until Kumar's approval is recorded in T-180. When it's on, it uses one approved content-free utility template.
- **Content-free:** "Pactlab: an action is waiting for you on a pact. Open Pactlab to see it." plus the link. No pact name, party, amount or document text, on either channel.
- **Limits:**
  - a per-person cap, e.g. 10 messages a day across the phone channels;
  - one message per pact per 15 minutes, with later events folded into the next one;
  - WhatsApp messages metered in Hosting cost.
- **Preferences:** on the notifications page, each person picks web push, Telegram or WhatsApp for "act now" events. Email stays as T-145 built it.

## Acceptance
- **Push:** a counter-proposal notifies a subscribed device within a minute, with a content-free payload (test with the fixture or a mock push service).
- **Telegram and WhatsApp:** an "act now" event sends one content-free message to each linked channel; the daily cap and the 15-minute fold hold; "for your information" events never go to a phone channel (tests with the fixture adapters).
- **Content rule:** tests prove no pact name, party, amount or document text reaches either channel.
- **Docs:** the runbook covers VAPID keys, and the Telegram and WhatsApp setup and costs; `.env.example` is updated.
