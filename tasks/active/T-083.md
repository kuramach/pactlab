# T-083 — The Venture Raise pack: founder-side diligence for VC, angel, and incubator deals (pack + website)

**Lane:** packs
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** packs, connectors, domain, site, spec, demo-data
**Needs:** none

## Kumar's ask (2026-10-25)

"One more pack. It is for a startup founder getting a deal done with VCs, angels or incubators. Call it a good name, add it to the website and create the task with depth."

## Name

**Venture Raise** (slug `venture-raise`). It names the deal from the founder's seat — raising venture money — and covers all three counterparties Kumar named (VCs, angels, incubators/accelerators) without privileging one. Alternates if Kumar overrides: Founder Raise, Capital Raise. Build with Venture Raise until he does.

## The idea

Every existing pack is buyer-side: an acquirer diligencing a target. This pack flips the seat. The "pact" is a financing: the founder is the one being diligenced, and the deal dies or reprices on what investors find. The founder's pains are specific: a scattered data room, deck metrics that don't tie to the billing system, a cap table that doesn't reconcile, IP that was never assigned to the company, and term-sheet mechanics (pool shuffle, SAFE conversion) they can't see until the money moves.

Pactlab's job, founder-side: **self-diligence before the investor finds it, and a claims register for everything the deck says.** The machinery is the same — evidence -> findings -> valuation/terms — with the confidentiality flipped: outputs split into an **investor-facing** view (the clean data room, the reconciled numbers) and a **founder-confidential** view (weaknesses, runway stress, fallback positions) that never leaves the founder's side. That split reuses the existing direction architecture (-1 drags feed the counterparty-facing position; +1 cushions stay in a confidential confirmed-value ledger) with the parties renamed.

## Part 1 — Pack definition (framework fit)

- Register the pack in the pack framework (T-057 registries: evidence adapters, finding types, valuation tweaks, per-deal pack selection; spec SS19 pack list amended). Counterparty types on the deal: VC / angel / incubator-accelerator, and round types: pre-seed, seed (SAFE / convertible note / priced), Series A. These are pack-level enums, not a new deal model — a Venture Raise pact is an ordinary pact with this pack selected, and the T-071 phases apply unchanged (Part 4).
- Counterparties differ and the pack knows it: angels move on trust and speed (lighter evidence set, faster checklist), incubators run a fixed program checklist, VCs run the full institutional pass (legal, financial, technical, market). The pack's readiness checklist and evidence requirements vary by counterparty type.

## Part 2 — Evidence adapters

Reuse before building, per the standing rule:

- **Cap table** — CSV import in the Carta/Pulley export shape (the CSV import machinery and pack imports already exist): shareholders, instruments, option grants, SAFEs/notes with cap, discount, and MFN flags.
- **Billing/financials** — the existing Stripe connector + billing CSV templates and QuickBooks-shaped exports: MRR/ARR, churn, burn, cash balance -> runway. Deck metrics are imported as *claims* (Part 3), never as facts.
- **Corporate record** — document evidence via the existing document pipeline: certificate of incorporation, bylaws, IP assignment agreements, founder/employee/contractor agreements, 83(b) confirmations, prior round documents, term sheets received.
- **Product/engineering** — the GitHub + Jira adapters (T-082): the same code-vs-delivery evidence investors' technical diligence will pull, seen by the founder first.

## Part 3 — Findings and the claims register

Finding types with the direction map enforced (VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP registration is part of this task):

- **-1 (drag — will cost valuation or terms when the investor finds it):** IP assignment gaps (founder or contractor IP never assigned to the company); cap table that doesn't reconcile to the corporate record; SAFEs/notes stacking beyond what the founder thinks is outstanding; deck metrics contradicted by billing/bank evidence (the credibility killer — a metric the deck states that Stripe refutes); runway materially shorter than the raise narrative assumes; missing 83(b) filings; customer concentration the deck doesn't disclose; side letters or promises outside the documents.
- **+1 (cushion — confirmed value, founder-confidential until the founder chooses to surface it):** cap table reconciles to the cent against the record; deck metrics tie exactly to billing and bank; IP chain complete; data room complete against the counterparty-type checklist before the investor asks.
- **0 (informational):** market-standard term variances, cosmetic record gaps with no legal effect, each with rationale.
- **Claims register (T-071's said-vs-observed, founder edition):** every number and statement in the deck/term discussions is a claim, tracked VERIFIED / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED against evidence. The founder sees the register before any investor does; a CONTRADICTED claim is a -1 finding with the evidence attached.

## Part 4 — Valuation and term modeling (the founder's bridge)

- The "price" is the round: target pre-money vs the investor-adjusted view the findings imply, presented as the pack's bridge — founder's ask, adjustments the evidence forces, where the round realistically lands.
- **Instrument math, modeled not hand-waved:** SAFE/note conversion at cap and discount under multiple priced-round scenarios; the **option pool shuffle** shown explicitly (a pre-money pool increase is founder dilution — the model states who pays for the pool, in shares and %); pro-rata and MFN effects across the existing SAFE stack; liquidation preference (1x non-participating baseline, flag anything else) and board composition as term findings with plain-language consequences.
- Phases (T-071): **Pre** = readiness screen ("are we ready to be diligenced?" — raise-vs-runway math, checklist completeness, GO/NO_GO on starting the raise); **Diligence** = live investor Q&A, the claims register, data-room gaps closed as they surface; **Post-Close** = the round is done: investor reporting cadence and milestone tracking against the plan the money was raised on (the preserve-and-enhance register, founder edition).
- Foresight (T-073): the pack declares its trend set — funding-market climate: VC deployment pace, median seed/Series A valuations, interest-rate environment, IPO/acquisition window, down-round and bridge-round frequency, sector heat (incl. the AI premium) — feeding the founder's timing question ("raise now or in two quarters?") as a scenario, never a prophecy.

## Guardrails

- Founder-confidential data is a hard boundary: the investor-facing view is an explicit export/share surface, nothing crosses it implicitly, and confidential findings never appear in shared artifacts.
- No legal advice: term modeling shows mechanics and consequences in plain language; the pack never recommends accepting or rejecting a term — it shows the founder what the term does.
- Reuse the pack framework, phases, claims register, valuation registry, and connectors as they are; this pack is definitions, adapters, and modeling on top of them — not a second product.

## Acceptance

- Pack registers cleanly in the framework; a Venture Raise pact can be created with counterparty type + round type, and its readiness checklist varies by counterparty type.
- Cap table import reconciles (or reports exactly why not); SAFE stack conversion modeled at cap/discount across at least two priced-round scenarios, with the pool shuffle's founder dilution stated in shares and %.
- A deck metric contradicted by billing evidence produces a CONTRADICTED claim + a -1 finding with the evidence linked; all new finding/evidence types are in VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP (contract test green).
- Investor-facing export contains zero founder-confidential findings (tested).
- Demo set builds via the generator, planted findings all surface on the demo pact, README lists them.
- Site: `/industries/venture-raise` renders in the established pattern, index lists twelve packs, page copy passes the site voice rules (available, no invented claims).
