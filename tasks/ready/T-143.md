# T-143: Wire the editorial pack illustration set into the site

Engine: claude
Model: sonnet
Lane: site
Context: standard
Touches: apps/site
Needs: none

## Why

The site's industry illustrations are minimal flat line SVGs. Kumar has approved a richer editorial illustration set (deep navy/indigo, one warm amber accent, bold flat shapes, subtle grain, highly contextual per pack) and it is already committed on main at `apps/site/public/illustrations/<slug>.webp` (commit dad2dd53) for all 17 packs: commercial-property, ecommerce-dtc, energy-generation, fintech, healthcare, hospitals, hotels, multifamily, multifamily-syndication, music-rights, pharma-biotech, professional-services, small-clinics, software-saas, startup-spv, telecom-media, venture-raise. This task puts them on the site.

## Build

1. **Point every existing industry surface at its webp.** Wherever the site selects an industry illustration today (industry pages, industry cards/listings, any hero or story slot — find the references, expected in `apps/site/lib/` and the industry page components), use `/illustrations/<slug>.webp` for the matching industry slug instead of the `.svg`.
2. **Square art in landscape slots.** The SVGs are 640x420; the webps are square (1024x1024). Present the webp inside the existing slots with a cover crop (`object-fit: cover` or the framework equivalent) so nothing distorts and no layout shifts; if a slot reads materially better with a taller aspect, adjust that slot's aspect ratio instead — builder's judgment, verified by screenshot at desktop and mobile widths.
3. **Multifamily Syndication:** if the industries data already carries a `multifamily-syndication` entry, wire its webp the same way; if it does not, leave the asset in place — its page arrives with T-106.
4. **Do not wire energy-generation, music-rights or startup-spv yet** — those packs have no site pages until T-140/T-141/T-142 land; their assets stay committed and ready.
5. **Leave the old SVGs in place.** Do not delete any `.svg` in this task (the slide-* and brand SVGs are unrelated and stay referenced regardless); removing the superseded industry SVGs is a later cleanup once the new set is proven live.
6. **Alt text** for each wired image names the industry (e.g. "Energy generation and rights" style naming per the industry's own label) — no keyword stuffing.
