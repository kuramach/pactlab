## Why
T-184 (Kumar, 2026-11-26: *"this style of data expectancy is good for all business"*) built the generic history module (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history.ts`, ADR 0057 addendum) and applied it to fintech. It was too big for one session; this is the rest, as T-184's brief says.

## Per pack
Follow T-184's fintech change and `docs/agent/packs.md` §4d, one pack at a time with checks green between packs:
- **Telecom & media:** subscribers (LAST of closing), ARPU (service revenue ÷ subscriber-months), churn (disconnects ÷ opening) from `TELECOM_SUBSCRIBER_MOVEMENTS`.
- **Professional services:** utilization (timesheets), realization (time and billing), headcount (LAST).
- **Healthcare services** and **small clinics:** visits, payer mix (commercial share), denial rate from the remittances and collections exports.
- **Ecommerce:** orders, AOV, repeat rate, CAC from the channel orders, cohorts and ad spend.
- **Pharma:** product revenue by product (billing), R&D spend (P&L key expense).
- **Software & SaaS:** ARR, GRR/NRR and logo churn by year from the SaaS engine (billing back five years), and join `pnlTrendRules`.
- **Venture raise, music rights, energy generation, startup SPV:** pick what a reviewer of that deal checks (music: income by year; energy: production by year), or state in the pack docs why history does not apply (an SPV has no operating business) and skip.

For each: five years of P&L and drivers in the demo data in every currency, from its own random generator, with one or two planted trend defects listed in the README; existing current-year files consistent with the last year; the History strip and years table through the shared components; the industry page (and its de/fr/ja catalog entries) states the history it reads.
