## Why

T-175 (ADR 0057) shipped the product-help chat inside its paths. Three pieces need paths it did not have.

## Build

1. **Metered runs in Hosting cost.** Help runs belong to no pact, so they are logged (`ai.run.metered`) rather than stored as `ai_runs`, which need a deal.
   - Add a store for pact-less AI runs: hashes, tokens, model, provider, job type, surface and, in-app, the organization. Never content.
   - Include them in Hosting cost's metered AI spend by job type.
2. **Console routing.** Add `support_chat` to the route-key CHECKs (`ai_platform_routes`, `organization_ai_routes`), `AiRouteKey` and the controllers' `ROUTE_KEYS`. Remove `storedRouteKey` in `apps/api/src/ai/model-routes.ts`.
3. **The site panel in the page.** Allow the app's origin in the site CSP's `connect-src` (`infra/cdk/lib/site-stack.ts`) and enable CORS for the public help routes only. Then render the panel on the site instead of the `/assistant` window. Keep the window as the fallback.
4. **`SUPPORT_TEAM_EMAIL`:** list it in `packages/config` and `.env.example`; `apps/api/src/main.ts` reads it directly today.

## Acceptance

- A help reply shows in Hosting cost under its job type.
- An admin can re-route `support_chat` for an organization.
- The site chat opens in the page.
