## Why (Kumar's direction, 2026-11-27)
Resellers talk to clients unsupervised. Before a reseller can access clients, they complete a video tutorial and pass a certification exam. Certification is the gate that activates their commercial presence: no pass, no referral link, no partner code, no co-marketing kit. The point is comprehension and claims discipline — resellers never receive diligence content (T-093's standing rule), never quote figures outside the kit, never promise features or dates.

## Part 1 — Tutorial modules in the reseller portal
- The four modules from the content of record, presented in sequence inside the reseller portal's onboarding flow (alongside the T-093 agreement and Stripe Connect steps). Videos are hosted as unlisted embeds (Vimeo or YouTube — Kumar records and uploads; the portal stores only the embed ids in config, so re-recording a module never needs a deploy).
- Sequenced unlock: module N+1 unlocks when module N is marked watched (player completion event, with a manual "mark watched" fallback for embed providers that don't fire one). A reseller can re-watch any unlocked module at any time.
- All strings externalized through the T-172 catalog pattern so ja/de/fr versions can follow under T-173; v1 ships English only.

## Part 2 — The exam
- Question bank stored as structured content in the repo (seeded from the content of record), versioned as a whole — publishing a new bank version is a content change, not a code change.
- Attempt mechanics per the content of record: draw 25, stratified (at least 5 pricing-compute, at least 5 rules/scenarios), question and option order shuffled, 30-minute limit, pass at 80%, pricing questions score all-or-nothing.
- A failed attempt starts a 24-hour cooldown; the retake is a fresh draw. Every attempt (draw, answers, score, timestamp) is recorded against the reseller.
- The exam never reveals correct answers after an attempt — only the score and the sections to restudy.

## Part 3 — The gate
- Reseller lifecycle extends T-093's states: `applicant → in_training → certified`, plus `recert_required` and `revoked`.
- **Only `certified` activates commerce:** the referral link resolves and attributes, the partner code is accepted at checkout, and the co-marketing kit downloads unlock. Contract tests prove an uncertified (or revoked) reseller's link/code attributes nothing.
- On pass, the reseller record is stamped with the exam version and pass date, and a certificate PDF is generated (name, date, exam version) for the reseller to download.
- **Re-certification:** publishing a new exam version moves every `certified` reseller to `recert_required`. In that state, existing attributed clients and their commissions are untouched, but **new-client attribution pauses** until the reseller passes the current version. Kumar can exempt an individual reseller with a recorded reason.
- Kumar-only admin: list resellers by state with scores and attempt history; revoke and reinstate certification manually (revocation pauses new attribution the same way, and is audit-logged).

## Rules
- Exam content is confidential by construction: the bank (with answers) is never exposed through any API the candidate's session can reach; grading happens server-side.
- No candidate data beyond the reseller record itself; attempts are retained for audit, not shared with other resellers.
- The gate is enforced in the attribution path itself, not just hidden in the UI — a copied link from an uncertified reseller must not attribute.
- CLAUDE.md testing principles hold: the gating and grading behaviour is proven at service level; one e2e journey (train → fail → cooldown → pass → link activates) tagged @regression.

## Acceptance
- A demo reseller completes the four modules in sequence (module 3 stays locked until module 2 is watched), fails an exam attempt, is refused a retake inside 24 hours, passes a fresh draw, and their referral link attributes a test client only from the moment of the pass.
- Certificate PDF generated with the exam version stamped; the admin console shows the reseller as certified with both attempts listed.
- Publishing bank version 2 moves the reseller to `recert_required`: an existing client's collected payment still accrues commission; a new client's signup does not attribute until re-certification.
- Revoking the reseller pauses new attribution immediately and the action appears in the audit log.
