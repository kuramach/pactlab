# Pactlab brand kit

Hand this directory to Claude Code when building the marketing site or any brand surface.

## Identity

- Name: Pactlab (one word; lowercase in the wordmark)
- Tagline: "Test the pact before you sign it."
- Pillar: "AI diligence. Human instinct."
- Story: a pact is a promise; a lab is where promises get tested.

## Locked B2 identity

- `apps/site/public/brand/pactlab-logo.svg` — flask outline with diagonal indigo-gradient liquid and the one-color outlined wordmark, for light backgrounds.
- `apps/site/public/brand/pactlab-logo-light.svg` — reversed lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `apps/site/public/brand/pactlab-symbol.svg` and `pactlab-symbol-light.svg` — flask-only marks.
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, and `icon-512.png` provide browser, device, and social artwork.
- The old flask-with-handshake mark and gradient-drift wordmark are retired. Do not use them on new or existing brand surfaces.

## Colors

- Deep navy `#081127` — primary text, headlines, dark surfaces
- Indigo `#6978ff` — accent, CTAs, highlights, links
- White `#ffffff` — text on dark, light surfaces
- Light section backgrounds: `#f8faff`, `#f7f9fc`

## Typography

- Manrope (sans-serif) throughout. Bold for headlines; regular for body. The SVG wordmark is outlined and has no runtime font dependency.

## Voice

- Direct, expert, zero hype. The machine does the diligence; the human makes the call.
- Never: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "supercharge", or anything that sounds like a pitch deck.
