# T-145 — Pact activity notifications: in-app inbox and email, with urgency and a digest

Engine: claude
Model: opus

Lane: platform
Context: deep
Touches: packages/domain packages/db packages/contracts apps/api apps/worker apps/web docs tasks .env.example
Needs: none
Checks: pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm test

## Why
- **The gap:** Kumar (2026-11-20): "When deals are in full flight keeping the parties in tight dance is key."
- **Today, nobody is told when a pact changes.** Pactlab emails only sign-in codes, invitations, listing interest, demo invites and operator alerts.
- **What that means in practice:** a seller's upload, a counter-proposal, a signature waiting or an exclusivity clock running out reaches the other side only if they happen to open the pact.
- **Why this comes first:** it's the foundation. T-146 (push and SMS) adds channels on top of the same events.

## Build
1. **Plan mode first.** This adds a schema, a cross-cutting event model and email cadence. Write an ADR covering the event types, recipients, urgency, digest rules and the people-data rules.
2. **Pact events.** A small, explicit catalogue of meaningful events, each with an urgency.
   - **Act now (immediate):**
     - a signature requested of you (T-136 envelopes);
     - terms proposed or countered to your side;
     - a deadline within 48 hours (exclusivity per T-119, an LOI or signing expiry);
     - an IC decision on your pact (T-118);
     - a question addressed to you.
   - **For your information (digest):**
     - a new document from the other side;
     - a finding drafted, accepted or rejected;
     - a member added;
     - a valuation scenario submitted;
     - a sync finished or failed (T-125).
   - **Recording:** events are written where the change happens, in the same transaction as the change: one row per event, deduplicated by a stable key.
3. **Recipients follow access.** A person is notified only of what they may see.
   - **Who can see what:** deal membership and role, buyer-only versus shared visibility, and target-contributor isolation (CLAUDE.md security defaults) all apply.
   - **Defence in depth:** recipients are computed server-side under RLS, never from client input.
   - **Proof:** tests show that a seller is never told about a buyer-only finding or valuation.
4. **Channels:**
   - **In-app inbox:** a bell in the top bar with an unread count, and a paginated list (T-126). Each item links to the exact place in the pact. Mark read, and mark all read.
   - **Email:**
     - "Act now" events are sent at once.
     - "For your information" events go in a digest per person, hourly or daily by preference, sent only when there is something new.
     - Emails go through the existing branded SES sender.
5. **Content rules:**
   - **Email:** names the pact and the event in plain words and links back. It never carries amounts, valuation, compensation or document text, and never named-person HR data (rule 7).
   - **The inbox:** shows only what the recipient may already see in the pact.
6. **Preferences** (Settings → Notifications):
   - per person, and per pact (an override);
   - per urgency: email immediately, email in the digest, or in-app only;
   - quiet hours in the person's time zone. "Act now" waits until quiet hours end, unless the person opts it through.
   - Sensible defaults: act now goes immediately by email; for your information goes in a daily digest.
7. **Jobs:**
   - the digest and any delayed sends run in the worker (BullMQ);
   - idempotent, so a retry never sends twice;
   - failures are recorded and visible (T-125), never silent;
   - SES bounces and complaints already suppress addresses (T-109); notifications respect that.
8. **Audit:** sends are recorded (who was notified, of what event, by which channel, when), with no message bodies stored beyond the event reference.

## Acceptance
- **A counter-proposal** produces one in-app item and one immediate email to the other side's lead, and nothing to people without access (tests).
- **A seller's upload** appears in the buyer team's daily digest, never as an immediate email (unless the person chose that).
- **Isolation:** a target contributor is never notified of buyer-only findings, valuation or terms (allow and deny tests).
- **Reliability:** quiet hours delay a send; a retry doesn't double-send; a suppressed address isn't emailed.
- **Docs:** the ADR, the runbook and `.env.example` (any new setting) are updated.
