# ADR 0055: IT & professional services: utilization, realization and the people the fees depend on (T-167)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the professional services pack of T-063 (spec §19 4a); released on merge.

## Context
- Services firms are priced on fee-earner economics that live in time-and-billing exports: utilization, realization, and how much of the book walks out if two partners leave. The T-063 pack read timesheets and headcount movements in aggregate, the management pack's utilization and the largest client's termination clause; nothing recomputed realization, fees by client or by partner, ageing, or covenant cover.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `PROFESSIONAL_SERVICES` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (DCF, ARR multiple). The T-063 targets and rules stay first and unchanged. The pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `SERVICES_TIME_BILLING` (→ `services.billing_month`: month, client, engagement kind, pseudonymous fee-earner, grade, relationship partner, hours worked and billed, standard value, billed, collected) and `SERVICES_AGEING` (→ `services.ageing_band`: report date, ledger — WIP or debtors — age band, balance). Fee-earners are pseudonymous references only (`people: PSEUDONYMOUS`); the people-column guard refuses names, contact and pay columns; a rate is never a column, only inside the standard value.
   - **Document category:** the restrictive covenant register (`COVENANT_REGISTER`, route FILE; intake expects it).
   - **A calculator, `services-practice-1`:** trailing fees, utilization (timesheets), realization, collection, the largest client, fee-earner and partner book as shares of fees, the recurring share, and the latest WIP and debtor balances over 90 days. A missing figure names the import it needs.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page.
   - **Concentration claims register** (`services-concentration-claims`): the ceilings a deck states for any one client and any one partner's book ("no client exceeds 15% of fees"), against fees by client and by relationship partner over the trailing twelve months. At or under the ceiling VERIFIED (+1, confirmed value only), more than half a point over CONTRADICTED (−1, unpriced), no twelve complete months UNVERIFIED (0); each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Realization trend** (`services-realization-trend`): fees billed ÷ standard value for every calendar year the export covers in full (never a partial year scaled up), falling every year by 3 points or more first to last (−1, unpriced); collection shown beside it.
   - **Fee reconciliation** (`services-fee-reconciliation`): the export's fees against the P&L's revenue lines by year, beyond 2% (−1).
   - **Ageing drift** (`services-ageing-drift`): each ledger's latest report in each year; a balance over 90 days 1.5× or more the year before's is −1, priced as a price reduction on working capital — low half the increase, high all of it — in the billing currency.
   - **Rainmaker cover** (`services-rainmaker-coverage`): every relationship partner whose book is 10% of trailing fees or more (by fee-earner when the export names no relationship partner), against the covenant documents — those whose heading names restrictive covenants, non-competes or non-solicitations. A sentence naming the reference with a non-compete or non-solicitation is cover; one stating there is none, or silence, is a −1 PEOPLE finding, unpriced (a retention holdback or a signed covenant at closing). The check waits until documents have been read.
   - **Revenue quality** (`services-revenue-quality`): retainers and managed services against projects and time and materials over the trailing twelve months, a neutral record with its rows.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `services-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses fee-earner and staff costs, subcontractors and associates, WIP and bad-debt write-offs, and recruitment.
3. **Directions.** Every rule outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`services.timesheet_month`, `services.headcount_month`, `services.billing_month`, `services.ageing_band`); `apps/api/src/packs/services-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds.
4. **The Practice tab** (`/deals/:id/services`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/services`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded (25 clients, fee-earners and partners at most; one row per ledger and year). "Run professional services checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No HR or legal judgment.** The pack reports documents, coverage and arithmetic. It never assesses, ranks or scores a person, and never says whether a covenant is enforceable. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; a ceiling nothing checks is UNVERIFIED, a rainmaker no document names is "nothing in the documents".
- **People data (rule 7).** Fee-earners are pseudonymous references; their fees stay in deterministic code and never reach a model; rules read only business and financial documents, never HR-classed ones. Rainmaker findings are PEOPLE-domain, so the negotiation brief withholds their details.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-063 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/professional-services/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Sablestone Consulting) gains time and billing from January 2023, the year-end WIP and debtor ageing, a three-year P&L, the information memorandum and the covenant register; billing is now the clients' fees from the export. Planted defects: (a) the top client at 27% of fees while the memorandum says no client exceeds 15%; (b) realization falling 92% → 87% → 81% across FY2023–FY2025; (c) P-01 and P-02, with 34% and 27% of fees, stated in the register to have no non-solicit; (d) WIP over 90 days 2.1× in a year. `apps/api/src/packs/services.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the services pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270114000000_services_utilization_realization_coverage` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: live PSA or practice-management adapters (each a future adapter producing the same records); a model-proposed register for covenant documents the sentence patterns do not read; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
