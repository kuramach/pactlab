## Why
Kumar (2026-11-26): *"this style of data expectancy is good for all business."*

T-183 sets the five-year rule (its ADR), the multi-year imports, and the pattern on multifamily. This applies it to every other pack.

## Per pack
For each pack in `packages/domain/src/packs/registry.ts`, apart from multifamily and syndication (done in T-183):

1. **Income and expenses:** the pack joins the shared `pnlTrendRules`, with its own key expense lines if it hasn't already. The seven packs from 2026-11-26 have done this, so check them for the five-year coverage and ask-the-seller behaviour from T-183.
2. **Its operating drivers over time:** the two or three figures whose history shows the business's strength. Each is a period-series import target (or the generic one T-183 built), with a versioned calculator, history metrics and at least one trend finding. Examples:
   - **Software / SaaS:** ARR, gross and net revenue retention, logo churn.
   - **Hotels:** occupancy, ADR, RevPAR.
   - **Commercial property:** occupancy, average rent per square foot, lease expiries by year.
   - **Healthcare, clinics and hospitals:** visits or admissions, payer mix, denial rate.
   - **Ecommerce:** orders, AOV, repeat rate, CAC.
   - **Fintech:** TPV, take rate, loss rate.
   - **Pharma:** product revenue by product, R&D spend.
   - **Professional services:** utilization, realization, headcount.
   - **Telecom and media:** subscribers, ARPU, churn.
   - **Energy, music rights, startup SPV and venture raise:** pick what a reviewer of that kind of deal would actually check. Where history doesn't apply (an SPV with no operating business), say so in the pack docs and skip it.
3. **Synthetic demo data** (`kuramach/pactlab-demo-data`), for every pack, in all its currencies:
   - five fiscal years of P&L and the operating drivers;
   - realistic movement;
   - one or two planted trend defects, each listed in the README;
   - existing current-year files kept, consistent with the last year of the history.

   Bump the pin.
4. **On screen and the website:** as T-183. The History strip and years table come from the same shared components, and each industry page states the history it reads.

## Acceptance
Per pack:
- a five-year history imports deterministically;
- the history metrics match hand-computed values;
- each planted trend defect surfaces as a cited draft;
- partial history states its coverage and asks the seller for the missing years.
