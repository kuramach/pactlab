## Build
1. **Cloud destinations in the desktop:** OneDrive, Google Drive and Box, through the seller's own sign-in in the app (`RinseDestination`). This is never the Pactlab login, and Pactlab gets no write access.
2. **Injection path (b):** a one-time import of a folder and its manifest, uploaded as a zip, through the same manifest reader and the same SHA-256 checks as the sync.
3. **First-class provenance (expand-only migration):**
   - **Per document:** a record linking the canonical copy, the seller's original and its manifest entry (audit, conversion log, checks), shown on the document and its citations. This replaces the intake-reason text.
   - **Per set:** the manifest's gap notes and source discrepancies stored. Source discrepancies become diligence signal, as draft findings or claims, with a person deciding.
4. **The hand-sourced label everywhere it is used:**
   - a claim origin `HAND_SOURCED`, in place of MANUAL with a labelled source;
   - wherever a finding or a Deal Critical (T-179) rests on a hand-sourced claim, it shows that provenance.
