## Done

- **Charts drill:**
  - a Code activity month opens that month's pull requests and who committed;
  - a Delivery month opens the Jira issues resolved that month;
  - an area bar opens the People list for that area.

  Clicks work anywhere over a month or bar, and the same links are listed under each chart for keyboards and screen readers.
- **The month page** (`/engineering/activity`), paged and filterable:
  - **Delivery:** each issue's type, priority, status, dates and reopens, with the commits and pull requests that cite it.
  - **Code:** each pull request's state, author, reviews, CI and the issues it names, plus the people who committed that month (pseudonymous labels, top 20, hidden under the team-size minimum).
  - **Previous and next month**, and a code/delivery switch.
  - **Links out** only to real systems: live connections, or the recorded apache/kafka and Apache Jira data.
- **Key people to keep** (engineering-3):
  - sole maintainers first, ordered by the commits in the areas only they maintain;
  - then coordinators, by issues created plus comments.

  Each reason states its measure. There are still no scores or ranks; the people included are unchanged from engineering-1.
