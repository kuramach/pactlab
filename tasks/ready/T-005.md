## Brief

Findings become numbers. Deterministic, versioned, auditable.

- Versioned assumptions (every scenario records its full input set).
- ARR-multiple and DCF engines in `packages/calculations` — decimal math,
  never float, never model prose.
- Scenarios + sensitivities; purchase-price bridge; findings link to
  valuation adjustments (accepted risk → adjustment → scenario impact).
- Stale propagation: changing an accepted risk marks dependent scenario
  results stale until re-run (test this).
- Approvals: scenarios are frozen on submission; frozen results are immutable.
- Transaction-type awareness per spec: LBO inputs for take-privates,
  accretion/dilution inputs for stock consideration (engines, not advice).

## Acceptance

- Changing an accepted risk marks dependent scenarios stale (test).
- A frozen valuation submission is byte-identical on re-read.

## Out of scope

Document AI, live market-data feeds (trading comps can use fixture prices).
