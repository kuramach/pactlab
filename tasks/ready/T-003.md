## Brief

The metrics engine that management ARR claims get checked against.

- Stripe fixture adapter behind the connector contract (`validateConnection()`,
  `dryRun()`); live adapter is config-only (no credentials in repo).
- Revenue ledger: normalized transactions → MRR/ARR, retention, cohorts,
  customer concentration.
- Reconciliation: management-claimed ARR vs ledger-computed ARR, with a
  reviewer approval step for the difference. Differences become draft findings.
- Explanations: every metric links to its inputs (deterministic code, decimal
  math, never float, never model prose).
- Multi-currency: amounts stored as `{amount, currency}` + converted value with
  rate/source/timestamp, per the spec's multi-currency section. ECB fixings,
  cached, point-in-time.
- Seed HealthyCo (clean), TroubledCo (inflated ARR), SparseCo (gaps/duplicates)
  Stripe-like data via `pnpm seed`.

## Acceptance

- A reviewer reconciles management ARR to transactions and approves the
  difference in the UI.
- TroubledCo's inflated ARR surfaces as a draft finding with citations.

## Out of scope

Findings workflow (T-004), valuation (T-005), other billing providers.
