## Brief
- **The page:** a dedicated download page in every site locale (T-171).
  - **Installers:** macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows and Linux, free to download.
  - **For each release:** the current version, release notes and SHA-256 checksums, read from the release feed T-178b publishes.
- **Prep invites** deep-link to it.
- **Line:** "Rinse your deal documents clean before they go in the room."
- **What it does, above the download buttons** (Kumar, 2026-11-27: *"a page to download and a small explaining its features"*): a short, plain-words section a seller understands in under a minute, built from the approved site patterns in `docs/ui-patterns.md`, with the feature cards as the existing site uses them, no new look.
  - **How it works, in three steps:**
    1. **Drop your documents in.** The whole folder, any mix: PDFs, scans, Word, Excel, CSV exports.
    2. **Rinse.**
       - Everything is converted to the format Pactlab reads.
       - Each conversion is checked against the original, so nothing is lost or changed.
       - Duplicates and broken files are flagged.
    3. **Close the gaps, then upload.** You see what the buyer will ask for and what's missing; you add the file, type it in, or note why it doesn't exist. Then you upload one finished folder.
  - **Feature cards, each one sentence** and only what T-178 and T-178b actually ship. Check each claim against the code and the runbook before it goes on the page. The candidates:
    - **Stays on your machine:** nothing leaves your computer until you upload.
    - **Checked, not just converted:** every conversion is compared with the original.
    - **The buyer's checklist up front:** the documents this kind of deal needs, and which are missing.
    - **Hand entries are labelled:** anything typed in is marked "not documentary evidence".
    - **Fewer follow-up questions:** a clean, complete set means a faster deal.
  - **Privacy line:** what is sent and when (only AI job payloads when online, under Pactlab's data terms), worded from ADR 0058, not marketing.
  - **System requirements and FAQs:** supported OS versions, file types, size limits, whether it works offline, and what happens to the originals.
  - **Translations:** de, fr and ja catalog entries for all of it, per T-171's completeness test.
  - **Wiring:** the site header or footer links to /rinse, and the seller-facing industry pages mention it in one line.
- **Trademark:** add "Pactlab Rinse" to the open trademark-clearance list before public launch.
