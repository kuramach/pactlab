# ADR 0050: Small clinics, the few-site variant: owner, referrals, premises and the multi-year P&L trend (T-161)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Builds on T-068 (the small-clinics pack) and T-061 (healthcare).

## Context
- Small clinics trade on the same evidence as larger provider groups, but their risks concentrate: one owner-operator, one referral source, one lease. T-068 shipped a clinics pack with site-level provider concentration, coding against policy and aged equipment; it did not ask those three questions, and it ran no metric calculator of its own.
- Kumar (2026-11-26): every pack takes P&L statements for several fiscal years through the import system and uses all of them, with trend findings a single year would miss.
- T-160 (the healthcare parent pack's payer-mix, denials and credentialing work) is not built yet. This task builds the shared pieces it needs in their shared homes, never forked.

## Decision
1. **The healthcare evidence, by reference and scaled.** The `SMALL_CLINICS` pack now imports healthcare's `HEALTHCARE_CLAIMS_SUMMARY` (billed, paid and denial rate by payer and month) and `HEALTHCARE_PROVIDER_ROSTER` (pseudonymous providers with credential expiry), and runs healthcare's `healthcare-credentials-expiring` and a new shared rule, `healthcare-denials-rising`, alongside the healthcare rules it already ran. The new rule lives in `packages/calculations/src/packs/healthcare/rules.ts` with the other healthcare rules, for T-160 and the hospitals variant to register; the healthcare pack's own behaviour is unchanged (its registry entry does not list it).
2. **Two import targets (ADR 0010)**, people data pseudonymous or none:
   - `CLINICS_VISITS` (`visit-summary.csv` → `clinics.visit_month`): month, service line, pseudonymous provider, the provider's role (`owner` for the selling owner-operator, `associate` or `contractor`), referral source, visits and collections. The owner is a role, never a name.
   - `CLINICS_SERVICE_LINES` (`service-lines.csv` → `clinics.service_line_year`): revenue and direct costs by service line and fiscal year, for each line's margin. Direct costs are totals, never one person's pay.
   - Margins are listed before visits: the owner check reads both and first runs when the visits arrive (an inline pack import runs the pack's rules).
3. **Four checks** (deterministic, decimal-only, drafts only, cited; `clinics-*` version 1):
   - `clinics-owner-dependence`: over the trailing twelve months, the owner's share of visits and collections, and the service lines no associate or contractor delivered. Drafted when such a line exists or the owner carries 30% of collections or more; HIGH when that line is the highest-margin one in the latest service-line year, or the share is 50% or more. A succession or transition-plan sentence among the readable documents is quoted; its absence is stated as absence. −1, unpriced: a transition agreement or an earn-out the reviewer sizes.
   - `clinics-referral-concentration`: visits by referral source over the trailing twelve months; the largest source other than self-referral at 40% or more. Direction 0 (a volume sensitivity and a relationship review); Pactlab makes no judgment on the referral arrangement.
   - `clinics-lease-term`: the lease read sentence by sentence — the expiry date, any renewal or extension option, the assignment clause — and measured in whole months from the stated target closing date (or, when none is stated, the end of the latest billed month, and the finding says so). Under 24 months with no renewal option is −1, priced as a remediation cost from the relocation estimate the documents state (half the estimate to the whole); with no stated estimate it is unpriced and the description says so.
   - `clinics-pnl-trend`: the shared P&L trend (below) with a clinic's key expense lines: clinical supplies and lab fees.
4. **The multi-year P&L trend, shared by every pack** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/pnl-trend.ts`, `pnl-trend-1`):
   - `pnlYears` reads the imported P&L (`finance.pnl_line`) through the P&L reader (`pnl-1`): every complete fiscal year, as the file states it or rolled up from twelve imported months; a partial year is never scaled up. When two imports hold the same line for the same period, the newer row is read. Withdrawn imports never reach it: the pack evidence loader reads active evidence only (T-154).
   - `pnlTrendRule({ id, expenses })` drafts, over two years or more: revenue falling in every year; gross or operating margin falling in every year by two points or more in total; and a key expense line growing faster than revenue in every year with its share of revenue up one point or more. Each is −1 (an assumption haircut the reviewer sizes) and cites the rows of every year it compares. A pack adopts it with one line and its own expense lines.
   - One imported year still reads: the calculator reports its figures; a trend needs a second year.
   - QoE and valuation already read every imported year through the T-149 reader; nothing there changed.
5. **A calculator of its own** (`clinics-metrics`, `clinics-metrics-1`, ADR 0042): the owner's share of collections and visits, owner-only service lines, the largest referral source's share, the claims-weighted denial rate, paid ÷ billed, the largest payer's share, the roster and credentials lapsing within a year, the highest service-line margin, and for every imported P&L year its revenue, growth, gross and operating margin and supply and lab ratios. A missing figure names the import it needs.
6. **Directions.** The new signals are in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`clinics.visit_month`, `healthcare.claims_month`, `finance.pnl_line`, `document.premises_lease`), and `apps/api/src/packs/clinics-directions.test.ts` holds each rule to its map entry. None proposes +1: no clinic signal confirms value, and none raises a price.
7. **Walls.** Two-party, as T-068: the buyer runs the checks; the seller is a target contributor and the pack-rule run is refused to them. No new table and no new seat.
8. **Guardrails.** Documents, dates and arithmetic only: no clinical, legal or referral-compliance judgment. Figures come only from imports and documents; what they do not state is said to be missing, never filled in. Providers are pseudonymous, the owner is a role, no patient data is accepted and nothing here reaches a model (rule 7). No new AI job type.
9. **Released** on merge: `SMALL_CLINICS` joins `RELEASED_COMPANY_TYPES`, `small-clinics` joins `RELEASED_INDUSTRY_SLUGS`, and a What's-new card announces it.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/small-clinics/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Clearbrook Clinics) gains the visit summary, service-line margins, the claims summary and roster, a P&L for fiscal years 2024–2026 (`pnl.csv`, with `pnl-line-kinds.csv` for scripted imports), the Site 1 lease and the transaction timetable. Planted: (a) 58% of visits from one referral source, (b) the lease expiring 14 months after the target close with no renewal option, (c) the selling owner as the only provider of implants, the highest-margin line, (d) clinical supplies growing 15% a year against revenue's 6%. `apps/api/src/packs/clinics.acceptance.api.test.ts` proves all seven planted findings, the calculator, and that a withdrawn P&L stops counting. The demo organization imports a folder's P&L where it has one.
- If the margins arrive after the visits, the owner finding already drafted says margins were not imported; the reviewer re-reads it (drafts are not rewritten).
- **Migration** `20270109000000_small_clinics_few_site` is expand-only: two `import_target` enum values. Rollback: nothing to undo; the values stay unused.
