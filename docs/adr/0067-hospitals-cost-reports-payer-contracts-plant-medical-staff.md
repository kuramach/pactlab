# ADR 0067: Hospitals: cost reports, payer contracts, the plant and the medical staff, checked against the documents (T-162)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the hospitals pack of T-069 (spec §19 11a) on the healthcare pack of T-160 (ADR 0051); released on merge.

## Context
- Hospitals carry evidence no other pack reads: filed cost reports, payer agreements that expire mid-hold, a medical staff credentialed department by department, published quality ratings, and a physical plant whose deferred capex hides in the P&L trend.
- The T-069 pack read the census, the payer mix, the accreditation transfer condition, aggregate physician dependence and committed regulatory capex. Nothing reconciled the cost reports against the deck, measured a payer agreement against the closing, checked privileges, set capex against revenue across years or mapped a quality claim to a report.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `HOSPITALS` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (`PAYER_MIX_EBITDA`, DCF). The T-069 rules, and the T-061 director-fee and privacy rules it reuses, stay first and unchanged.
2. **The T-160 registers are reused by reference, extended in place.** `HEALTHCARE_PROVIDER_ROSTER`, `HEALTHCARE_CREDENTIALS` and `HEALTHCARE_PAYER_CONTRACTS` are listed by id in the registry entry, never copied. Two optional columns are added in code (no migration; the healthcare files import as before): `department` on the credentialing register (where a privilege is held) and `rate_basis` on the payer contract register. The verification reader learns one more credential kind, `hospital_privileges`. Healthcare and small clinics behave exactly as before.
3. **What the pack adds, by registry entry only:**
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `HOSPITALS_COST_REPORT` (→ `hospitals.cost_report_year`: fiscal year, beds, bed days available, patient days in total and for Medicare and Medicaid, total costs, net patient revenue, as the filed report's worksheets state them) and `HOSPITALS_CAPEX` (→ `hospitals.capex_year`: capital expenditure by fiscal year and category). Aggregates only.
   - **Document categories:** filed cost report, property or facility condition assessment, quality reporting (route FILE), with the healthcare licence verification and payer contract categories.
   - **A calculator, `hospitals-operations-1`:** occupancy, the Medicare and Medicaid shares of patient days and cost per patient day from the latest cost report; admissions over the trailing twelve months (with the census by year beside the P&L, T-184c; a missing year is asked of the seller); the largest payer's share of net revenue; material payer contracts ending within a year; credentials lapsed and departments whose privileges lapse together; the latest year's capex ÷ revenue. A missing figure names the import it needs.
4. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page; the cost reports, condition assessments, quality reports and the extracts' paged copy are never read as claims. Checks whose answer a document can change wait for the documents (`docs/agent/packs.md` §8b).
   - **Cost-report claims** (`hospitals-cost-report-claims`): occupancy and the Medicare and Medicaid shares of patient days as a narrative document states them, against the cost report of the fiscal year the sentence names (else the latest). Verified within 1 point (+1, confirmed value only), contradicted beyond 3 (−1), unverified with no report (0), each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Cost-report reconciliation** (`hospitals-cost-report-reconciliation`): each year's total costs as filed against the imported P&L's cost of revenue and operating expenses, beyond 2% (−1).
   - **Payer contract register** (`hospitals-payer-contracts`): each material payer (10% or more of trailing net revenue in the payer mix, self-pay aside) against its agreement, measured from the expected closing a document states (else the analysis date); the agreement's own expiry sentence governs over the register. Ending before the closing or within 12 months after it, without automatic renewal, is −1 (HIGH for the largest payer), its rate basis stated; a material payer with no agreement on the register is 0.
   - **Medical staff** (`hospitals-medical-staff`): every licence and privilege at the analysis date (the end of the latest census or payer-mix month), a verification document's date governing. A department where at least two physicians, and half the department, share one lapsed or near-expiry privilege date is −1 (HIGH when lapsed); other lapses and rostered physicians with no licence or privileges on the register are 0.
   - **Plant and capex trend** (`hospitals-capex-trend`): capex ÷ revenue across the imported P&L years (three or more), falling every year to 75% of its first year or less. Against a narrative claim of a modern plant it is −1, cited to the claim and to a condition assessment, priced as remediation at the deferred amount the assessment states; without such a claim it is a 0 watch item. One year cannot show it.
   - **Quality claims** (`hospitals-quality-claims`): star ratings and safety grades the deck states, mapped to the reporting documents supplied: the same value VERIFIED (+1), another CONTRADICTED (−1), none stated UNVERIFIED (0), in the claims register.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `hospitals-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses salaries and benefits, supplies and drugs, purchased services, and repairs and maintenance; `hospitals-admissions-trend` (−1, admissions falling every year by 5% or more) and `hospitals-recent-run` (T-185).
5. **Directions.** Every T-162 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`hospitals.cost_report_year`, `hospitals.capex_year`, `hospitals.census_month`, two new signals on the healthcare credential and payer-contract types, and the quality signals on `document.finding`); `apps/api/src/packs/hospitals-directions.test.ts` runs every rule in the registry entry over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
6. **No new screen.** The calculator's figures and the five-year table show on Finances, the drafts on Findings and the claims on Claims, as for every pack; the hospital view stays buyer-side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`).

## Guardrails
- **No clinical, survey or legal judgment.** The pack reconciles documents and dates; it never predicts a survey outcome, assesses the quality of care, or reads a payer agreement in law. Stated in every relevant finding and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states is shown as missing, never filled in.
- **People data (rule 7).** Physicians are pseudonymous references; their rows stay in deterministic code and never reach a model; rules read only business and financial documents.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-069 was.

## Consequences

- **Migration** `20270124000000_hospitals_cost_reports_plant_quality` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- **Site mismatch corrected:** the hospitals page listed malpractice history and licensure gaps, which no check reads; both are removed, and the page now describes the cost-report, payer-contract, medical-staff, plant and quality checks.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for cost reports the CSV does not carry; a dedicated hospital tab (the generic pack views carry it); QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
