# ADR 0069: Hotels: the franchise, the PIP and comp-set performance against the claims (T-166)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the hotels pack of T-067 (spec §19 9a); released on merge.

## Context
- A hotel's price rests on its flag and its comp-set performance. The franchise agreement's term, termination rights and fees, the brand's property improvement plan (PIP), the management agreement, and the STR report behind the deck's RevPAR claim are documents and exports no check read.
- The T-067 pack priced the whole PIP from the franchise agreement's estimate, flagged the RevPAR gap to the comp set, channel concentration, seasonality and the management termination fee. Nothing measured the franchise term against the hold, set the PIP's items against the seller's budget, checked a performance claim against the benchmark, or read the P&L across years.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `HOTELS` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (NOI ÷ cap rate, DCF). The five T-067 rules stay first and unchanged.
2. **Evidence, by reference and in place.**
   - The seller's capex schedule is the commercial property target `CRE_CAPEX_SCHEDULE` (`cre.capex_item`), reused by reference; its category hint names the hotel elements (guest rooms, bathrooms, public areas and the like). No new import target, so no migration.
   - The STR benchmark (`HOTELS_STR_BENCHMARK`) gains two optional columns in code, `comp_set_occupancy` and `comp_set_adr`, for the occupancy (MPI) and ADR (ARI) indices; files without them import as before.
   - **Document categories:** franchise or brand agreement, property improvement plan, hotel management agreement, STR or performance report (route FILE).
   - **A calculator, `hotels-performance-1`:** trailing-twelve-month occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and GOP margin (operating history), the RevPAR, occupancy and ADR indices (STR benchmark), franchise and brand fees ÷ rooms revenue in the latest P&L year, seasonality from the statements where the P&L is imported by month, and the capex the seller's schedule carries. Occupancy, ADR and RevPAR carry the five-year history beside the P&L (ADR 0057). A missing figure names the import it needs.
3. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are recognised by their heading and read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page; agreements, the PIP, STR reports and the extracts' copy are never read as claims. Checks whose answer a document can change wait for the documents (`docs/agent/packs.md` §8b).
   - **Franchise register** (`hotels-franchise-term`): the term's end measured from the expected closing a document states (else the analysis date), over the hold a document states (else five years); a term ending on an anniversary of the closing ends in that hold year. Ending before the closing or inside the hold is −1, unpriced (renewal, re-flagging or an exit-cost scenario, or a renewal as a closing condition), with the agreement's termination rights, liquidated damages and fees quoted.
   - **PIP schedule** (`hotels-pip-schedule`): each item the plan states (its number, description, completion date and estimate) matched to the capex schedule by the item it names ("PIP item 2") or its element. Items due within twelve months of the closing, or before it, that the schedule does not fund (or funds below 90%) are −1, priced as an **escrow** at what is unfunded. The T-067 draft prices the whole plan as remediation; the description says to accept one or the other, never both. Nothing is drafted before the schedule is imported.
   - **Performance claims register** (`hotels-performance-claims`): occupancy, ADR and RevPAR claims against the trailing twelve months of the operating history (verified within 1 point or 2%, contradicted beyond 3 points or 5%), and the RevPAR, occupancy and ADR index claims against the STR benchmark (Σ hotel ÷ Σ comp set × 100; verified within 1, contradicted beyond 3 index points). Contradicted −1, verified +1 confirmed value only, unverified 0 (with the reason), each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Management agreement** (`hotels-management-contract`): the term, base and incentive fees and the owner's rights as stated. A sentence binding a purchaser ("runs with the hotel", "binds any purchaser"), or a term past the closing with no right to end it on a sale, survives the sale: −1, unpriced, with the cost to end it shown as arithmetic where the agreement states a multiple of the base fee (multiple × base fee × trailing gross operating revenue). The T-067 termination-fee draft carries the price.
   - **Brand fees against rooms revenue** (`hotels-brand-fee-trend`): the P&L's royalty, marketing, reservation and loyalty lines ÷ its rooms revenue, rising every year across three or more consecutive fiscal years to 1.1× the first year or more (−1); occupancy and ADR by year from the operating history are shown beside it.
   - **The shared rules:** `hotels-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1) with the key expenses rooms department, food and beverage, utilities and repairs (brand fees are read against rooms revenue by their own rule, not twice); `hotels-occupancy-trend` (−1, occupancy falling every year by 3 points or more) and `hotels-recent-run` (T-185).
4. **Directions.** Every T-166 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (new `hotels.operating_month` and `hotels.benchmark_month` entries; `pip-unfunded-near-term` on `cre.capex_item`; `franchise-term-in-hold` and `management-survives-sale` on `document.finding`; `brand-fee-outpacing` on the P&L line); `apps/api/src/packs/hotels-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
5. **No new screen.** The calculator's figures and the years table show on Finances, the drafts on Findings and the claims on Claims; the view stays buyer-side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`).

## Guardrails
- **No valuation or brand judgment.** Documents, dates and arithmetic only; the pack never opines on the flag's worth or on whether the brand would renew. Stated in the findings and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states (a closing date, a fee, a competitive set's ADR) is shown as missing, never filled in.
- **People data.** None: the operating history and the STR benchmark are property aggregates; no guest data is accepted.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-067 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/hotels/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Harbourview Hotel Holdings) gains the seller's capex schedule, a three-year P&L, the property improvement plan, the investor presentation, an STR trend report and the franchise and management agreements' term, fee and successor clauses; the STR benchmark gains the comp set's occupancy and ADR and now reads a 97.0 RevPAR index. The operating history export is unchanged. Planted: (a) PIP item 1, due 30 June 2027 — six months after the 31 December 2026 closing — with no line in the capex schedule; (b) the deck's RevPAR index of 112 against the benchmark's 97.0; (c) the franchise term ending 31 December 2029, three years after closing, with liquidated damages; (d) franchise and brand fees rising from 7.5% to 9.6% of rooms revenue across FY2023–FY2025. `apps/api/src/packs/hotels.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the hotels pact joins the demo-org pool and imports the P&L by year.
- The T-067 RevPAR-gap draft now reads a gap widening from about 0.2% to about 5.2% (it was 7% to 12%): the benchmark had to carry a 97.0 index; the rule is unchanged.
- **Site mismatches corrected:** the page said the pack reads PMS reports, "daily" revenue history and food-and-beverage P&Ls, through "adapters", with demand concentration from occupancy history. It reads a monthly operating history and the STR benchmark as file imports, the P&L by year, and channel concentration from billing; the page now says so and describes the franchise, PIP, claims, management and fee checks.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for agreements the sentence patterns do not read; live PMS or STR adapters (each a future adapter producing the same records); a dedicated hotel tab; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
