# ADR 0051: Healthcare services: multi-site provider diligence — payer mix, denials, credentialing (T-160)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the healthcare pack of T-061 (spec §19 2a); released on merge.

## Context
- Provider-services deals are priced on collections that depend on payer contracts, denial behaviour and clinicians staying credentialed. The T-061 pack read a twelve-month claims summary, the provider roster, the medical-director agreement and the privacy assessment; nothing recomputed payer mix or denials across years, checked a clinician's licence against the documents, or tied collections to a clinician.
- This is the parent pack for small clinics (T-068) and hospitals (T-069), which reuse its T-061 rules by id.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (ADR 0050's shared `pnl-trend-1`).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `HEALTHCARE` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (DCF, ARR multiple). The T-061 targets and rules stay first and unchanged, so the clinics and hospitals packs, which list T-061 rule ids, are untouched. The pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `HEALTHCARE_REMITTANCES` (→ `healthcare.remittance_month`: month, payer, payer class, claims, denied claims, charges billed, collected, receivables at month end), `HEALTHCARE_CLINICIAN_COLLECTIONS` (→ `healthcare.clinician_month`: month, pseudonymous provider reference, site, visits, collected), `HEALTHCARE_CREDENTIALS` (→ `healthcare.credential`: the register's own reference, provider reference, kind, jurisdiction, issuer, status, expiry — never the licence number) and `HEALTHCARE_PAYER_CONTRACTS` (→ `healthcare.payer_contract`). Payers and pseudonymous clinicians only; no patient, claim line or diagnosis; the people-column guard applies.
   - **Document categories:** licence verification, coding or billing audit, HIPAA risk assessment, payer contract, and the non-compete and retention schedule (route FILE; intake expects them, so a missing one is a checklist gap).
   - **A calculator, `healthcare-collections-1`:** trailing collections and charges, gross collection rate, denial rate, days in AR, commercial and government shares, the largest clinician's share, credentials expired at the analysis date and payer contracts ending within a year. Each figure states its formula and row count; a missing one names the import it needs.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each figure quoted from one sentence and verified on the page. The analysis date is the last day of the latest remittance month, never the clock.
   - **Claims register** (`healthcare-practice-claims`): the denial rate, a payer class's share of collections and days in AR as a document states them, against the remittances by calendar year — denial rate verified within 0.5 and contradicted beyond 1.5 points or when it moved the other way; payer share within 1 / beyond 3 points; days in AR within 2 / beyond 5 days. Contradicted −1, verified +1 confirmed value, unverified 0, each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Collections reconciliation** (`healthcare-collections-reconciliation`): the P&L's patient-service revenue lines against the year's collections, beyond 2% (−1).
   - **Collections trend** (`healthcare-collections-trend`): a denial rate rising every complete year by a point or more (−1), and days in AR rising every year by five days or more (−1). Partial years are left out, never scaled up.
   - **Payer-mix shift** (`healthcare-payer-mix-shift`): the payer class that pays least per claim in the latest year taking three points or more of claims from the first year (−1), with the cost stated as arithmetic (the latest year's claims at the first year's mix and the latest yields).
   - **Credentialing** (`healthcare-credentialing`): every register row at the analysis date; a verification document's sentence naming the clinician and the kind of credential governs the expiry, so the check waits until documents have been read. A state licence expired while the collections export records collections in later months is **practising on an expired licence** (−1, HIGH, unpriced: an escrow, a special indemnity or reinstatement as a closing condition). An expired credential with no later collections, one expiring within 90 days and a rostered clinician with no licence on file are 0.
   - **Clinician concentration** (`healthcare-clinician-concentration`): a clinician at 20% or more of the trailing twelve months' collections with no document naming them with a non-compete, non-solicitation or retention agreement (−1, domain PEOPLE). A retention risk, never a ranking; waits for documents.
   - **Payer contracts** (`healthcare-payer-contracts`): each payer remitting in the trailing twelve months (self-pay aside) against the register — expired without renewal while still remitting (−1), ending within 365 days without automatic renewal (0), no contract on the register (0). Nothing is drafted before a register is imported.
   - **Compliance evidence** (`healthcare-compliance-evidence`): the HIPAA assessment, coding audits, licence verifications and the non-compete schedule recognised by their headings; a coding audit more than a year old is 0. The HIPAA assessment's age stays the T-061 privacy rule's. Absence is shown on the Practice tab and the intake checklist, never drafted.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `healthcare-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1) over every imported year, with the vertical's key expenses: denial write-offs and bad debt, clinical staff costs, medical supplies, billing and collection costs.
3. **Directions.** Every T-160 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP`, and `apps/api/src/packs/healthcare-directions.test.ts` runs the rules over the demo data and checks each proposed direction against the map. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
4. **The Practice tab** (`/deals/:id/healthcare`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/healthcare`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded: credential exceptions only (active ones counted), the 25 largest clinicians, one row per payer. "Run healthcare checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No clinical or legal judgment.** The pack checks documents, dates and arithmetic. It never assesses the quality of care, whether billing, coding or a referral arrangement is lawful, or what follows from an expired licence. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states is shown as missing.
- **People data (rule 7).** Clinicians are pseudonymous references; their collections are read only by deterministic code and never reach a model or an embedding; rules read only business and financial documents, never HR-classed ones.
- **No walls added.** The pack is two-party, as T-061 was: the seller is a target contributor and sees none of the Practice tab.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/healthcare/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Riverside Care Partners) gains remittances from January 2023, collections by clinician, the credentialing and payer contract registers, a three-year P&L, the management presentation, a licence verification report, the non-compete schedule, a coding audit and a payer agreement, with planted defects (a) a denial rate rising across FY2023–FY2025 (and denial write-offs outpacing revenue) while the deck says it fell, (b) two clinicians collecting after their licences expired, (c) a payer mix shifted toward Medicaid against the deck's commercial share, (d) one clinician at 31% of collections with no non-compete on file. `apps/api/src/packs/healthcare.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the healthcare pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270110000000_healthcare_services_pack` adds four `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for documents the sentence patterns do not read; clinics and hospitals adopting the new rules (each its own change); QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged (QoE already schedules every imported period).
