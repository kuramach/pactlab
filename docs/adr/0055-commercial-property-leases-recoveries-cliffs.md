# ADR 0055: Commercial property: the rent roll against the leases, recoveries and expiry cliffs (T-165)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the commercial property pack of T-066 (spec §19 8a); released on merge.

## Context
- Commercial property diligence is documents against a summary: the rent roll against the leases, recoveries against the lease terms, expiries against the hold. The T-066 pack read the rent roll and the CAM reconciliation and drafted the anchor rollover, the Phase I condition, CAM under-recovery and a below-market anchor; nothing read the leases themselves, tied rent to the statements, checked recoveries across years, priced the obligations a buyer inherits or set the condition report against the seller's capex.
- It is the direct sibling of the Multifamily pack (T-059): same real-estate cluster, same NOI ÷ cap-rate and DCF methods, commercial lease mechanics instead of unit leases.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `COMMERCIAL_PROPERTY` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (NOI ÷ cap rate, DCF). The T-066 targets and rules stay first and unchanged. The pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Import (CSV, ADR 0010):** `CRE_CAPEX_SCHEDULE` (→ `cre.capex_item`: project, category, year, amount, status — planned, committed or completed). The seller's schedule, nothing else.
   - **Documents:** the leases (the common `LEASE` category, already required for the real-estate cluster), and two pack categories, `PROPERTY_CONDITION_REPORT` (new) and `ENVIRONMENTAL_REPORT` (the energy pack's, shared). Route FILE; intake expects them.
   - **A calculator, `commercial-property-leases-1`:** in-place base rent, the largest tenant's share, the rent-weighted average lease term and the rent expiring within two years, CAM recovered, the statements' recoveries against their recoverable lines, committed and scheduled capex — from the imports alone. A missing figure names the import it needs.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Leases and reports are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page. The analysis date is the last day of the latest billed month, never the clock; the hold runs from the expected closing a document states (else the analysis date) for the hold a document states (else five years).
   - **Leases:** a sentence naming a lease agreement with the tenant and the suite starts a lease; its term, base rent, escalation and lease type (net with the tenant's pro rata share, or gross) are read from the sentences after it. Sentences never run across a `---page---` marker, so a text copy of the leases reads the same as the PDF.
   - **Rent-roll verification** (`cre-rent-roll-verification`): rent recomputed at the analysis date (base rent raised once per anniversary of commencement) against the roll — more than 0.5% above is an overstatement (−1); a roll expiry the lease contradicts is a mismatch (−1). The lease governs.
   - **Statement reconciliation** (`cre-rent-statement-reconciliation`): the statements' base rent against the leases' rent month by month for each complete year, only once every suite on the roll has a lease read; beyond 2% (−1).
   - **Recovery reconciliation** (`cre-recovery-shortfall`): recoverable = each net lease's share × the statements' tax, insurance, repairs, security, common-area and utility lines, for the months the lease is in force; billed = the statements' recovery revenue. Short by more than 2% in every year, two years or more, is a trend finding (−1); one year short is shown, not drafted.
   - **Obligations register** (`cre-obligations`): tenant improvement allowances and leasing commissions a document says the landlord shall pay, and committed capex in the schedule, due on or after the start of the hold — one draft each, priced as a price reduction at the amount stated (−1). Earlier ones are the seller's.
   - **Concentration and cliff** (`cre-expiry-cliff`): rent share by tenant (from the leases where read) and the expiry profile by year of the hold; a tenant at 25% of rent or more expiring in years 1–2 is a cliff (−1, unpriced: renewal as a condition or a leasing reserve). Waits for documents.
   - **Condition evidence** (`cre-condition-capex-gap`): a sentence of a property condition or environmental report recommending work at an estimated cost, with no capex project naming the same building element, is drafted priced as remediation at the report's cost (−1). Nothing is drafted against a capex schedule not supplied.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `cre-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), key expenses real-estate taxes, insurance, repairs and maintenance, utilities and property management.
3. **Directions.** Every outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`cre.lease`, `cre.cam_category`, `cre.capex_item`, and `rent-unreconciled` and `recovery-shortfall` on `finance.pnl_line`); `apps/api/src/packs/commercial-property-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: the below-market anchor is +1 confirmed value, never a price input.
4. **The Leases tab** (`/deals/:id/leases`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/commercial-property`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded (200 suites and projects shown, the rest counted). "Run commercial property checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No appraisal.** Rent shares, dates and sums only: no property value, market rent or cap rate is estimated; value comes from the reviewer's own scenarios. Not legal or engineering advice. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; a suite with no lease, a missing capex schedule or an unstated closing is shown as missing.
- **No people data.** Tenants are businesses; no walls added (two-party, as T-066 was).

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/commercial-property/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Quayside Estates, Quayside Business Park) gains the eleven leases (3% escalations; billing steps up every July), the capex schedule, a three-year operating statement, the offering memorandum (closing and hold), a leasing commission letter and a property condition assessment, with planted defects (a) the rent roll overstating Pellham Engineering's and Teal Harbor Software's rent against their leases, (b) 41% of rent from Corvane Logistics, whose lease ends in year one of the hold, (c) recoveries billed at 91% of recoverable in every one of FY2023–FY2025, (d) the roof the condition assessment prices absent from the capex schedule. `apps/api/src/packs/commercial-property.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the commercial-property pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270114000000_commercial_property_leases_recoveries` adds one `import_target` value; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum value stays unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed abstract for leases the sentence patterns do not read; per-tenant recovery billings (the statements carry the total); QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
