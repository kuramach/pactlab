## Why

These deals are priced on pipeline claims and a patent estate. The checkable failures are consistency failures: a phase claim the registry filing contradicts, a patent cliff nobody priced, a milestone obligation left out of the model.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Pipeline claims register:** every asset's phase, status and timeline claim mapped to the registry filings and regulatory correspondence supplied as evidence — VERIFIED / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED (the T-083 claims pattern).
2. **Patent estate register:** patents/applications with expiry dates from documents; an expiry cliff inside the hold period is a finding priced into the bridge.
3. **Obligation recompute** (`packages/calculations`): milestone, royalty and licence payments recomputed from the agreements and checked against the seller's model; an omitted obligation is a finding.
4. **Dependence findings:** single-CMO or single-supplier manufacturing, and key-scientist concentration, from documents and rosters.
5. **Burn vs timeline trend:** R&D spend across the imported P&L years against the claimed development timeline; spend flat while the timeline accelerates is a trend finding.

## Guardrails

- **No scientific judgment, ever:** the pack checks documents, dates and consistency; it never opines on efficacy, approvability or trial design.
- **Evidence and absence:** figures come only from documents and adapters; absence is shown as absence, never filled in.
- **Website (Kumar, 2026-11-26: "Don't forget the site updates too as u bring in new packs"):** update the pack's site pages in the same PR, not only flag mismatches:
  - its industry entry and story (`apps/site/lib/content.ts`, `apps/site/lib/industry-stories.ts`) describe what the built pack actually does: the evidence it reads, the checks it runs and the metrics its calculator now produces, in plain words;
  - anything the site claims that the pack does not do is corrected or removed, and nothing is claimed beyond what ships;
  - follow the pattern T-141 and T-142 used (their pack entries, cards and stories), keep `apps/site` tests green, and list the site changes in the PR.

## Multi-year P&L and trend (Kumar, 2026-11-26 — every pack)
The pack must take imported P&L statements for multiple fiscal years through the platform import system (statements by year) and use all of them: QoE and the valuation bridge read every imported year, and the pack produces trend findings — revenue growth, gross/operating margin drift, and the vertical's key expense ratios across years — each figure traceable to the import it came from. A single-year import still works (trend findings simply report one year). Withdrawn imports are excluded from trend reads like every other read path. The demo data includes at least three years of P&L with one planted trend defect that a single-year view would miss, and the acceptance test proves it surfaces.
