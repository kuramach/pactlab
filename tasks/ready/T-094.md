## Part A — Documents (B1, B2, B3, B11)

- **B1 Multi-file upload + upload visibility.** The upload control accepts multiple files at once (file picker multi-select AND drag-drop of many files). Every uploaded document is visibly listed with its state — a user can always answer "what have I uploaded?" at a glance.
- **B2 The vanishing document.** Reproduce first: upload 3 documents, switch tabs, return — only 2 show. Determine whether the third upload never persisted or persisted but isn't rendered/refetched, and fix the actual cause. Add a regression test for upload -> navigate away -> return. A document must never silently disappear; a failed upload must say so loudly at upload time, not by absence later.
- **B3 Auto-extraction.** On upload completion, AI extraction starts automatically per document (each file from a multi-upload kicks off its own). The manual "Run AI extraction" button becomes a re-run/retry affordance, not the default path. Extraction state (queued/running/done/failed) is visible per document.
- **B11 Drive-style library.** Replace the pill row with a proper document library: rows/cards with name, type, size/pages, uploaded date, extraction status, finding count; search, sort, filter; multi-select; click opens the document in the preview pane (Part B). Must read cleanly at 20+ documents.

## Part B — Review experience (B4, B5, B6, B7)

- **B4 No page refresh on decide.** Accept/Reject in the review queue acts in place (client-side update, optimistic with visible rollback on server failure). Scroll, context, and the reviewer"s place in the queue survive every decision.
- **B5 Real document preview.** The left frame must show the actual document page rendered, with the cited quote highlighted in place, and page navigation — a reviewer verifies an extraction against the page, not against a floating snippet. Reconcile the counters (today the same screen says "page 1 of 2" and "Cited page 1 of 4"): one page concept, plainly labeled. Verify against what T-076 shipped and close the gap rather than duplicating the viewer.
- **B6 Queue header counts.** Accepted and Rejected counts (and pending) pinned at the top of the queue, updating in place; clicking a count filters the list to those items. Decided items stay reviewable.
- **B7 Said vs Observed — simplify.** The claims/verification concept currently reads as a framework the user must learn ("very confusing... we are complicating the process"). Simplify the surface: plain language, verification state presented inside the findings flow (a finding shows what supports/contradicts it) rather than as a separate conceptual destination; the standalone tab goes away per Part C. **Boundary:** the underlying claims tracking stays; if a faithful simplification proves impossible without model changes, stop and flag in the PR for Kumar's call — do not silently redesign the model.

## Part C — Navigation: 12 tabs -> 4, with automation (B13)

- Consolidate the twelve tabs (Overview, Screening, Sources, Evidence, Finances, Findings, Said vs observed, Engineering, Valuation, Foresight, Documents, Post-close) into **four**:
  1. **Overview** — phase + screening verdict, key numbers, pipeline status (done/pending/next), team, activity.
  2. **Diligence** — Documents library, Sources, Evidence, Finances, Engineering as left-rail sections; Findings + review queue here.
  3. **Valuation** — bridge, scenarios, debt & cash, Foresight as sections.
  4. **Close** — post-close registers/obligations, executed documents, final redline state.
- Every existing surface reachable in <= 2 clicks from its new home; nothing is deleted, only re-homed. URLs/deep links redirect to the new homes.
- **Automation is part of this part:** the pact pipeline progresses itself (upload -> auto-extract -> findings -> valuation refresh), and Overview's pipeline status reflects those states live. Tabs are views over a moving deal, not manual stations.

## Part D — Foresight redesign (B10)

Revises T-073 as built (state the delta in the PR). Trend-object model stays; generation and sourcing change:

- **No Generate button.** Each pack declares its key metrics; the baseline is pre-generated (at pact creation, refreshed on a cadence) and the tab opens populated. No view-time live model call (in testing, that call failed and left a banner + empty tab).
- **5-year forecasts per metric, from two sources:** published external projections pulled from the internet (e.g. inflation from official/forecaster series — source cited per series) AND an AI forecast (model + as-of recorded, as today).
- **User forecasts:** the user enters/adjusts their own 5-year path per metric, next to the AI's.
- **Outcomes on both:** the business value projection is computed under the AI forecast set and under the user's forecast set, shown comparatively, assumptions visible — never a bare number.

## Part E — Balance-sheet upload (B9)

- Debt and cash stop being typing-only. Upload a balance sheet (document/CSV/Excel) -> extracted balance-sheet items (debt, cash, other lines) presented in Finances, confirmed through the review flow, then feeding the valuation bridge — extending T-078's debt & cash model, not duplicating it. Manual entry remains as the fallback. An unconfirmed extraction never touches the bridge.

## Part F — Team and mixed-auth access (B12)

- **Team affordance:** a Team icon (member avatars + count) at top-right near Settings. The pact lead invites members by email, assigning side (buyer/seller/advisor) and role; member list per pact visible from the icon.
- **Mixed SSO design (as worked out with Kumar):** one Auth0 org per party; the pact links orgs with side/role-scoped grants — nobody joins the other side's org. Authentication is per-org: buyer org members route to their enterprise connection; seller org uses passwordless/database. Invitations resolve the invitee to their own org and its method (SSO redirect, or passwordless signup for first-time users). Non-SSO orgs get a platform MFA baseline (no IdP policy to inherit), with step-up on sensitive actions (exports, counterparty shares). Side-scoped confidentiality is unchanged: confidential ledgers never cross sides; each side sees the other side's pact members by name/role only.
- Per-customer enterprise SSO connection setup remains an Auth0 dashboard step (per T-010); this task builds the app-side flows (invite, resolution, grants, enforcement) and documents the dashboard steps.

## Guardrails

- No silent data loss anywhere: every failure in upload/extraction/decide surfaces visibly at the moment it happens.
- Confidentiality boundaries (side-scoped views, ledgers) are untouched by the IA consolidation — re-homing a surface never widens who can see it.
