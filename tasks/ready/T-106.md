Model: opus
Engine: claude

**Lane:** platform
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** pack registry + new pack definition, evidence adapters and finding types (offering side), claims register, waterfall recompute, investor seats and permissions, web (syndication pact setup, investor view, valuation), site (industry page), demo data
**Needs:** the Multifamily pack (built and launched) — its property evidence adapters and finding types are reused by reference, never forked. Patterns reused from Venture Raise (T-083: claims register, hard output split) and the linked-org/linked-deal model (T-097). Nothing open blocks the start. Kumar decided the two-pack split on 2026-11-06.

## Why

"Multifamily" covers two different deals (Kumar, 2026-11-06):

1. A seller sells a multifamily property to a buyer — the existing Multifamily pack. Two parties, property diligence, done.
2. A sponsor buys a large multifamily property AND raises the capital from many accredited investors — a syndication. Three parties and two directions of diligence: the sponsor diligences the property, and the investors diligence the sponsor and the offering.

Same property core, different seat, different evidence family, different confidentiality shape — so, exactly as with Venture Raise flipping the seat, this is a second pack, not a mode inside the first. A mode would fork the Multifamily pack internally and make every later change ask "which mode?".

## Shape

One syndication pact with two sides and a hard wall between them:

- **Acquisition side (sponsor <-> seller):** property diligence using the Multifamily pack's adapters and finding types unchanged; sponsor prices the property with the core valuation machinery (buyer personas from T-105 apply to the sponsor as buyer).
- **Offering side (sponsor <-> investors):** the new material below. Investors are seated as their own orgs with role-limited views, linked to the pact (the T-097 pattern).

The seller never sees anything about the raise. Investors never see the acquisition negotiation.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Offering claims register.** Every material claim in the PPM / investor deck — projected IRR, equity multiple, cash-on-cash, pref return, occupancy and NOI assumptions, exit cap, sponsor track record claims — is a register entry mapped to its evidence: the property findings, the sponsor's prior-deal actuals, or market data. Unverified or contradicted claims surface as findings; this register is the pack's credibility core, the same pattern as Venture Raise's deck-metrics register.
2. **Sponsor verification.** A track-record table built from evidence: prior deals with projected vs actual outcomes side by side; entity, litigation and disciplinary evidence where adapters provide it. A missing or thin track record is an explicit gap finding, never a silent blank.
3. **Fee and waterfall analysis.** Acquisition / disposition / asset-management fees, pref, promote and splits are extracted from the operating agreement and the PPM; the waterfall is recomputed from the OA terms and checked against the PPM's own description. Description-vs-math mismatches are findings.
4. **Investor seats and accreditation status.** Many investor orgs per pact; each investor sees only the investor view. Accreditation status is tracked per investor (self-certified vs third-party verified, per the offering's 506(b)/(c) shape). Verification itself is an external specialist integration (link + status + evidence pointer) — do not build verification; best-in-class rule.
5. **Investor view (published subset).** The sponsor publishes a chosen subset of property findings plus the offering analysis to investors. Sponsor-confidential material — price position vs the seller, persona-priced runs, the confirmed-value ledger — can never enter the investor view. Enforced in the tool/data layer and proven by snapshot test, the T-083 split pattern.
6. **Investor return scenarios.** On the same Sextant machinery: investor-level returns (after pref and promote) under downside / base / upside NOI paths, clearly labelled as scenarios computed from stated assumptions, never as promises.

## Guardrails

- **Diligence support only.** The pack is not legal advice, makes no accreditation determination, and performs no securities-offering or broker-dealer function; it organises evidence and flags gaps for the parties and their counsel.
- **No invented sponsor history.** The register and track-record table contain only what documents and adapters supply; absence is shown as absence.
- **Confidentiality is tested, not promised:** investor-view snapshots contain zero acquisition-negotiation content; seller-side outputs contain zero raise content.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page for this pack is written as available, in the same style as the other pack pages (standing Kumar posture).

## Demo and acceptance

- Synthetic demo: a fictional sponsor, a fictional ~200-unit property, and a fictional PPM/operating agreement with planted defects — at minimum: (a) projected IRR inconsistent with the recomputed waterfall, (b) a prior-deal claim with no actuals behind it, (c) a fee present in the OA but missing from the PPM summary, (d) one property finding the sponsor has not published to investors (to prove the wall holds in both directions).
- The demo syndication runs end to end: property findings + offering register + waterfall check + investor view. All planted defects surface as findings.
- Existing Multifamily pack behaviour and tests are untouched and green.

### Approach (reuse; nothing forked)

### The pack
- **New company type:** `MULTIFAMILY_SYNDICATION` (label "Multifamily syndication", slug `multifamily-syndication`, cluster `real-estate`).
  - **Selection:** this follows ADR 0012, where the pack is the seller's company type and there is no second selection mechanism.
  - **Registry** (`packages/domain/src/packs/registry.ts`): it references the Multifamily entry's import targets, calculator (`multifamily-metrics`), finding rules and valuation methods by spreading `PACKS.MULTIFAMILY`. It adds its own targets and rules, so a change to Multifamily flows through.
  - **Released:** `RELEASED_COMPANY_TYPES` and `RELEASED_INDUSTRY_SLUGS`.
- **Seats:**
  - **Sponsor:** opens a buyer-led pact (sponsor = buyer).
  - **Seller:** a TARGET_CONTRIBUTOR guest, as today.
  - **Investors:** guests with a new deal role **INVESTOR**, one `syndication_investors` row per investor firm (the BIDDER/`bid_id` pattern of ADR 0015, as `deal_memberships.investor_id`).
    - `isCounterpartyRole` includes INVESTOR. `app_is_buyer_side` therefore already closes every sponsor-only table, including the negotiation, personas, the confirmed-value ledger and terms.

#### PR 1: sponsor side (pack, extraction, register, track record, waterfall, demo)
- **Extraction:** prompt `offering.extract` v1 in `packages/ai/src/prompts/`, modelled on `contract-extract.ts`. It reads a PPM, operating agreement or deck the intake classifies (new intake categories `OFFERING_PPM`, `OPERATING_AGREEMENT` and `INVESTOR_DECK`, with route EXTRACT).
  - **What it proposes:**
    - fee and waterfall terms: acquisition, disposition, asset management and other fees; the pref; promote tiers and splits;
    - claims: projected IRR, equity multiple, cash-on-cash, pref, occupancy, NOI growth, exit cap, and track-record statements.
  - **Grounding:** each item carries an exact quote and page. Code keeps a number only if it appears in the cited quote (the grounding pattern of `citations.ts`).
  - **Humans decide:** items land PROPOSED. The sponsor confirms or edits each term, and only CONFIRMED terms feed the math.
- **Tables** (expand-only migration, RLS own side, the T-110 style):
  - `syndication_offerings`: equity raise, hold years, 506(b) or (c), and downside, base and upside NOI growth and exit cap;
  - `syndication_terms`: kind, value, the source document (OA or PPM), quote and page, status;
  - `syndication_track_record`: prior deals with projected and actual figures, a source pointer, and absent shown as absent. Fed by a CSV import target `SYNDICATION_TRACK_RECORD` (ADR 0010 mapping) and by deck claims.
- **Claims register:** offering claims go into the existing T-071 claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`, as in Venture's `venture-deck-metric`) as VERIFIED, CONTRADICTED or UNVERIFIED, each linked to its evidence.
- **Calculations** (`packages/calculations/src/syndication/waterfall.ts`): a deterministic, Decimal-only European waterfall.
  - **Inputs:** the confirmed terms plus the NOI path. The property's NOI comes from the Multifamily metrics; growth, hold and exit cap come from the offering.
  - **Order:** return of capital → pref → catch-up (if any) → promote tiers. Fees come out first.
  - **Outputs:** LP and GP distributions, LP IRR, equity multiple and cash-on-cash, with the conventions stated in the result, as `round.ts` does.
- **Finding rules** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/syndication/rules.ts`, registered in the pack):
  1. **PPM IRR / multiple vs recompute:** the stated figure and the recomputed one differ by more than 1 point of IRR or 0.1× → a drag finding, and the claim is CONTRADICTED.
  2. **Track-record claim with no actuals:** UNVERIFIED, a gap finding.
  3. **Fee in the OA but not in the PPM fee summary.**
  4. **PPM waterfall description vs OA math** (pref or split mismatch).
  5. **Thin or missing track record:** an explicit gap.
  6. **506(c) offering with investors not third-party verified:** sponsor-only.
- **API:** `apps/api/src/syndication/` for offering settings, terms (list and confirm), the track record, the waterfall result and the register. Every route is behind `DealAccess` own-side checks and audited.
- **Web:** `deals/[dealId]/syndication/` with the offering setup, Terms to confirm, Track record, and Waterfall check (stated vs recomputed). It is a pact section shown only for this pack, through the existing sections mechanism in `packages/domain/src/pacts/sections.ts`.
- **Demo data:** in `scripts/demo-data/generate.py`, a `multifamily-syndication` folder.
  - **Contents:** a fictional sponsor, a ~200-unit property (it can share Multifamily's generators), a PPM, an OA, a deck and a track-record CSV.
  - **Planted defects:** (a) a projected IRR inconsistent with the recomputed waterfall; (b) a prior-deal claim with no actuals; (c) an OA fee missing from the PPM summary. (d), a property finding left unpublished, comes in PR 2.
  - **Scripted model responses:** `demoModelResponse` in `demo-seed/seed.ts` gets responses for `offering.extract`, so tests and the demo run without Bedrock.
  - **Publishing:** push to `pactlab-demo-data` and bump `DEMO_DATA_COMMIT`.
- **Tests:**
  - waterfall unit tests (hand-worked cases);
  - rule tests;
  - prompt and grounding tests (a number not in its quote is dropped);
  - an acceptance test `packs/multifamily-syndication.acceptance.api.test.ts` using `acceptance-kit.ts`, where all three planted defects surface;
  - `multifamily.acceptance` unchanged and green;
  - registry cross-package test.

#### PR 2: investors (seats, accreditation, investor view, return scenarios)
- **Inviting investors:** through the ADR 0014 invitation path, with role INVESTOR and `investor_id`.
- **Investor isolation:**
  - an investor reads only their own seat and accreditation row (an `app_current_investor_id` SECURITY DEFINER function, as `app_current_bid_id`);
  - investors never see each other's identities (the `deal_memberships_select` rule extended as for BIDDER).
- **Accreditation:** per investor, `SELF_CERTIFIED`, `THIRD_PARTY_VERIFIED`, `PENDING` or `NOT_ACCREDITED`, plus a provider link and an evidence pointer. Pactlab verifies nothing itself; it only tracks status (best-in-class rule). A 506(c) offering flags unverified investors to the sponsor.
- **The investor view** is a **published, frozen snapshot**: `syndication_investor_views`, versioned, read by INVESTOR only.
  - **Built from an allow-list:** the property findings the sponsor chooses to publish, the offering claims register, the track-record table, the fee and waterfall check, and the return scenarios.
  - **Never included:** the price position versus the seller, persona-priced runs, the confirmed-value ledger, terms, and the negotiation brief. The builder can't reach them, because it reads only allow-listed fields.
- **Investor return scenarios:** the waterfall runs on downside, base and upside NOI paths and gives LP returns after pref and promote. They are labelled "Scenario computed from stated assumptions — not a projection or promise".
- **Wall tests:**
  - snapshot tests showing the investor view holds zero acquisition-negotiation content, and that seller (TARGET_CONTRIBUTOR) responses hold zero raise content (`syndication_*` returns 403/404);
  - RLS integration tests for investor-to-investor isolation;
  - planted defect (d), the unpublished property finding, absent from the view;
  - an e2e flow: the sponsor publishes, an investor signs in, sees the view, and cannot reach the negotiation.
