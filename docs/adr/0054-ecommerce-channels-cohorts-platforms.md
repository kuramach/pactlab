# ADR 0054: E-commerce & DTC: channel economics, cohort value and platform dependence, from exports and documents (T-164)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the e-commerce pack of T-065 (spec §19 6a); released on merge.

## Context
- DTC deals are priced on lifetime-value claims and on revenue that may run through storefront, marketplace and ad accounts the seller owns personally. The evidence exists — storefront, marketplace and ad-platform exports, the stock report, account records — but the T-065 pack read only cohorts, ad spend and the marketplace terms.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `ECOMMERCE_DTC` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (DCF, ARR multiple). The T-065 targets and rules stay first and unchanged; the pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `ECOMMERCE_CHANNEL_ORDERS` (→ `ecommerce.channel_month`: month, channel, platform type, platform account, orders, new customers, gross sales, discounts, returns refunded, cost of goods, fulfilment, marketplace and payment fees) and `ECOMMERCE_INVENTORY` (→ `ecommerce.stock_lot`: SKU, location, units, unit cost, received on, report date). Counts and amounts only; no customer, the people-column guard applies.
   - **Document categories:** platform account record, marketplace or channel agreement (route FILE).
   - **A calculator, `ecommerce-channels-1`:** trailing net sales, the largest platform account's share, consumer order value and contribution margin, orders per customer over twelve months, lifetime value on contribution, paid acquisition cost, LTV ÷ CAC, the return rate, stock at cost and its aged share. A missing figure names the import it needs.
2. **Exports and documents only; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Pactlab never logs in to or scrapes a marketplace, storefront or ad platform. Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page; a missing export is shown as missing.
   - **Unit-economics claims register** (`ecommerce-unit-economics-claims`): the deck's lifetime value against the recompute on a contribution basis — orders per customer over the stated horizon (every cohort observed for all of it, one order per active customer-month) × consumer contribution per order (net sales less cost of goods, fulfilment and fees, trailing twelve months). Verified within 5%, contradicted beyond 10%; a contradicted figure that matches the same orders at gross sales within 2% says it is computed on gross revenue. Paid acquisition cost against ad spend ÷ attributed new customers, verified within 2%, contradicted beyond 5%. Contradicted −1, verified +1 confirmed value, unverified 0, each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Channel revenue verification** (`ecommerce-channel-reconciliation`): net sales from the exports for each complete calendar year against the P&L's revenue lines, beyond 2% (−1).
   - **Platform dependence** (`ecommerce-platform-ownership`): each account's share of trailing net sales, and who a document headed as a platform-account record says it is registered to (the paged extracts never verify themselves). A material account (10% or more) registered to an individual personally is −1 (CRITICAL at half or more), unpriced: its transfer to the company a closing condition, an escrow or holdback the reviewer prices; a smaller or non-selling account is 0, a closing deliverable. The documents govern, so the check waits for them.
   - **Returns** (`ecommerce-return-rate-trend`): returns refunded ÷ gross sales by complete year, rising every year by a point or more (−1).
   - **Inventory ageing** (`ecommerce-inventory-ageing`): the latest stock report aged from receipt to its date; stock older than 180 days at 10% of value or more is −1, priced as a price reduction — low half of it written down, high all of it — in the currency of the billing lines.
   - **Unit economics by year** (`ecommerce-unit-economics-trend`): blended acquisition cost (the P&L's advertising and marketing ÷ new customers in the orders export) rising every year to 1.1× its first year or more, and the consumer contribution margin falling every year by 2 points or more — each its own draft (−1), so an export imported before the P&L never leaves a stale combined draft behind.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `ecommerce-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses advertising and marketing, fulfilment and shipping, marketplace and payment fees, and returns.
3. **Directions.** Every T-164 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`ecommerce.cohort_month`, `ecommerce.ad_spend_month`, `ecommerce.channel_month`, `ecommerce.stock_lot`); `apps/api/src/packs/ecommerce-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
4. **The Channels tab** (`/deals/:id/ecommerce`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/ecommerce`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded: one row per channel and per account, the 25 oldest aged lots (the rest counted). "Run e-commerce checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **Exports and documents only.** No live scraping or log-in to any platform; absence of an export or a record is shown as absence, never filled in.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences.
- **People data (rule 7).** Customers appear only as counts; the account holder is named only as a role ("the founder"), as the document states it; nothing reaches a model.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-065 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/ecommerce-dtc/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Ridgeline Outfitters) gains orders by channel (January 2023 to September 2026), the stock report, twelve months per cohort, a three-year P&L, the platform accounts schedule and the investor presentation, with planted defects (a) the deck's 12-month LTV computed on gross revenue, contradicted by the contribution recompute, (b) 64% of revenue through the Marketvale seller account registered to the founder personally, (c) 22% of stock older than 180 days, (d) blended CAC rising and contribution compressing across FY2023–FY2025. Billing is the channels' net sales, so the T-065 marketplace-majority finding reads 64% too. `apps/api/src/packs/ecommerce.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the e-commerce pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270113000000_ecommerce_channels_cohorts_platforms` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: live storefront, marketplace or ad-platform adapters (each a future adapter producing the same records); a model-proposed register for account records the sentence patterns do not read; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
