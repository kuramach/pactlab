# ADR 0053: Pharma & biotech: pipeline claims, patent cliffs and licence obligations, checked against documents (T-163)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the pharma pack of T-064 (spec §19 5a); released on merge.

## Context
- Pharma and biotech deals are priced on pipeline claims and a patent estate. The checkable failures are consistency failures: a phase the registry filing contradicts, a patent cliff nobody priced, a milestone obligation left out of the model.
- The T-064 pack read the patent schedule, the pipeline, the revenue forecast's exclusivity sentence, a supplier's open inspection observations and partner concentration; nothing checked a claimed phase against what is filed, dated a patent against the hold after closing, or recomputed what the licence agreements oblige the company to pay.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `PHARMA_BIOTECH` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation method (DCF). The T-064 targets and rules stay first and unchanged. The pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `PHARMA_TRIAL_REGISTRY` (→ `pharma.registry_trial`: registry id, programme, phase, status, start and primary completion dates — the registry records the seller supplies; Pactlab looks nothing up itself), `PHARMA_OBLIGATIONS` (→ `pharma.obligation`: the seller's model of milestones, royalties and licence fees owed) and `PHARMA_RD_ROSTER` (→ `pharma.scientist`: pseudonymous reference, function, programmes led and patent families as inventor — counts only). The registry records import before the pipeline so the checks the pipeline's import runs read them.
   - **Document categories:** registry filing or regulatory correspondence, patent record, licence or collaboration agreement, manufacturing or supply agreement (route FILE).
   - **A calculator, `pharma-pipeline-1`:** programmes, programmes with a registered trial, patent families, the milestones in the seller's model, trailing billed revenue, R&D in the latest imported year and its share of revenue. A missing figure names the import it needs.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page. Checks that read documents wait until any document has been read, so an import before the data room drafts nothing stale.
   - **Pipeline claims register** (`pharma-pipeline-claims`): each programme's claimed phase against the latest phase of its registered trials (withdrawn ones aside) — or, with none registered, the phases its regulatory correspondence names (a document whose heading says it is registry or regulator correspondence; the deck's own words never count). Reaching the claimed phase is VERIFIED (+1, confirmed value only), an earlier one CONTRADICTED (−1), nothing that bears on it UNVERIFIED (0). A data readout's timeline against the registered primary completion of the trial at the milestone's phase; a programme whose every trial is suspended, terminated or withdrawn is a contradicted status. Each lands in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Patent estate** (`pharma-patent-cliff`): each schedule row's expiry, a patent record's sentence naming the family and jurisdiction governing. A product's cliff is the first jurisdiction where its composition-of-matter protection (else all its patents) runs out. The hold runs from the expected closing a document states (else the last billed month) for the hold a document states (else 5 years). A cliff inside it is −1, priced as a price reduction on the product's trailing billed revenue (the lines that name it): low one year, high a year for each year of the hold left after the cliff — revenue at risk before the erosion the reviewer sets.
   - **Obligations recompute** (`pharma-obligations`, `packages/calculations`): every "shall pay <payee> a milestone payment of …", "a royalty of …% of …" or "an annual licence fee of …" sentence, matched to the model by counterparty, kind and, for a milestone, the event, phase and programme. Missing is −1 at the agreement's amount (a fee: low one year, high five); a lower amount is −1 at the difference; a lower royalty rate is −1, unpriced. The pipeline schedule's expected date for the triggering event is quoted. Nothing is checked before the model is imported.
   - **Dependence:** a document saying one manufacturer or supplier makes a product, with no second source described in the same document (`pharma-manufacturing-dependence`, 0 — a supply covenant or second-source qualification); a scientist leading half the programmes or named on half the patent families, with no document naming them with a retention, employment, consulting or non-compete agreement (`pharma-key-scientist`, −1, PEOPLE; a retention risk, never a ranking).
   - **Burn vs timeline** (`pharma-burn-vs-timeline`): R&D within 5% of its first imported year in every year, while a document says a milestone was brought forward from one period to an earlier one (−1). A single year cannot show it.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `pharma-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses R&D, manufacturing and supply, and royalties and milestones paid.
3. **Directions.** Every T-163 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`pharma.programme`, `pharma.registry_trial`, `pharma.patent`, `pharma.obligation`, `pharma.scientist`, and `single-source-manufacturing` on `document.finding`, `rd-flat-timeline-accelerated` on `finance.pnl_line`); `apps/api/src/packs/pharma-directions.test.ts` runs every rule in the registry entry over the demo data and checks each proposed direction against the map. The cushion asymmetry holds.
4. **The Pipeline tab** (`/deals/:id/pharma`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/pharma`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded (at most 50 rows each, the rest counted). "Run pharma checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No scientific judgment, ever.** Documents, dates and consistency; never efficacy, approvability or trial design. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; a claim nothing bears on is UNVERIFIED, a missing model is said to be missing.
- **People data (rule 7).** Scientists are pseudonymous references and counts; rules read only business and financial documents, never HR-classed ones; nothing reaches a model.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-064 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/pharma-biotech/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Calvera Pharmaceuticals) gains the registry records, the obligations model, the R&D roster, a three-year P&L, the investor presentation (closing date, hold, the accelerated readout), FDA meeting minutes, a patent record, the PX-330 licence agreement and the Product A supply agreement, with planted defects (a) the lead asset PX-410 claimed Phase 2 against a Phase 1 registry record, (b) PF-01 (US) expiring 18 months after the stated closing, inside the hold, (c) the PX-330 Phase 2 start milestone in the licence agreement and missing from the model, (d) R&D flat across FY2023–FY2025 while the deck brings the PX-410 readout forward a year. `apps/api/src/packs/pharma.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the pharma pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270112000000_pharma_pipeline_patents_obligations` adds three `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for agreements and records the sentence patterns do not read; live trial-registry or patent-office adapters (the evidence is what the seller supplies); QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
