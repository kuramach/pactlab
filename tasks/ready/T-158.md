## Why (Kumar, 2026-11-25)
- **The ask:** Kumar asked for real-estate market data, and whether Pactlab could scrape Zillow.
- **No Zillow scraping:** Zillow's terms prohibit it, so it's out. Its official data programs need an approval Pactlab doesn't have.
- **RentCast is already built** (`packages/connectors/src/property/rentcast.adapter.ts`), but it:
  - feeds only teaser pages;
  - is off in live: the deployment passes no `RENTCAST_API_KEY`.
- **What this task adds:** rents in a rent roll checked against the market, cited to their source, so a buyer sees loss-to-lease against market rent.

## Verified provider API (RentCast docs, read 2026-11-25; check again before building)
- **Request:** `GET https://api.rentcast.io/v1/avm/rent/long-term`
  - header `X-Api-Key`;
  - `address` (`Street, City, State, Zip`) or `latitude` and `longitude`;
  - optional: `propertyType` (`Apartment`, `Multi-Family`, …), `bedrooms` (0 = studio), `bathrooms`, `squareFootage`, `maxRadius`, `daysOld`, `compCount` (5–25, default 15), `lookupSubjectAttributes`.
- **Response:**
  - `rent`, `rentRangeLow`, `rentRangeHigh`;
  - `subjectProperty`;
  - `comparables[]`, each with `price`, `distance`, `correlation`, and the comparable's own fields.
- **Not yet verified:** pricing and request limits. Check RentCast's plan terms, and cap calls per pact accordingly.

## Shape

### Part A: RentCast on in live (ADR 0042 rule 4: a figure's source must be available)
- **The key is a secret:** `pactlab/<env>/credentials/rentcast-api-key`, using the existing secret-store pattern (T-136). Never in code, in `.env.example` values, or in logs.
- **CDK:** pass `RENTCAST_API_KEY` to the API task from that secret. The IAM grant is limited to that one secret.
- **Key script:** `scripts/rentcast-key.sh <env>` pastes the key with a format check, like `scripts/doc-source-key.sh` (Kumar: never long paste-in commands).
- **Runbook:** `docs/runbooks/rentcast.md`, covering creating the key, setting it, rotating it, and what turns off without it.

### Part B: Market rents for a pact
- **Where the address comes from:** the pact's property address: the listing or teaser address, or a seller-party field if one exists (find it). If there's none, the screen asks once for the address and stores it on the pact, audited.
- **One estimate per floor plan:**
  - the rent roll's units are grouped by bedrooms and bathrooms (the existing rent-roll fields; median square footage per group);
  - one RentCast call per group, with `propertyType` `Apartment` (or `Multi-Family`) and `compCount` 15;
  - typically under ten calls per property, capped per pact (the cap is set from the plan terms).
- **Stored as evidence:** a new type, `market.rent_estimate`, one item per floor plan. Each holds:
  - the estimate and its range;
  - the comparables count, and their median distance and correlation;
  - the retrieval date;
  - source `RentCast`.

  These are deterministic records, refreshed only on request, and re-reads are idempotent (fingerprint: address, floor plan and date).
- **No people data:** no owner or tenant names, and nothing about people from the comparables (their addresses are kept only as the provider returns them, and shown only on the citation view).
- **Who can run it:** buyer side only (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`). The button is **Check rents against the market**, on the Home property-metrics card (#279) and on Finances.
  - It's audited as `market_rents.retrieved`.
  - Fixture adapter and cassette as for every provider. CI never calls RentCast.

### Part C: The figures and the check
- **Multifamily calculator** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/multifamily/metrics.ts`, version bump):
  - **Market rent (annual)** = Σ the market estimate for each occupied unit's floor plan × 12.
  - **Loss-to-lease vs market** = Σ(market − in-place) ÷ Σ market, over occupied units.
  - Each shows its formula and record count.
  - While market rents haven't been retrieved, the figure is missing with `needs: ['MARKET_RENTS']`, and the Home card offers the retrieval instead of "Ask the seller".
- **Finding rule:** when in-place rent is below the RentCast low range on at least a set share of units, the pack rule drafts a finding. It cites the rent-roll rows and the RentCast evidence, the share and threshold are named in the rule, and it stays a draft for review, as all pack rules do.
- **Existing figure kept:** the rent roll's own market-rent column (today's loss-to-lease) stays. The two figures are labelled apart: "from the rent roll" and "from RentCast".
