# ADR 0068: Hotels: the franchise, the PIP, the operator and the performance claims, checked against the documents (T-166)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the hotels pack of T-067 (spec §19 9a); released on merge.

## Context
- A hotel's price rests on its flag and its comp-set performance. The franchise agreement's term, termination penalty and the brand's property improvement plan (PIP), the operator agreement's surviving terms, and the STR report behind the deck's RevPAR claim are documents no pack read.
- The T-067 pack read the operating history, the STR benchmark, billing by channel, the PIP estimate sentence and the management termination fee. Nothing set the franchise's end against the closing, the PIP's items against the owner's budget, or the deck's performance figures against the reports.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `HOTELS` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation methods (NOI ÷ cap rate, DCF). The T-067 targets and rules stay first and unchanged.
2. **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010), added by registry entry only:**
   - `HOTELS_AGREEMENTS` (→ `hotels.agreement`): the franchise and management agreements register — reference, kind, counterparty, effective and expiry dates, fee rates and the early-termination penalty. Businesses only.
   - `HOTELS_PIP_SCHEDULE` (→ `hotels.pip_item`): the brand's PIP by item, with estimated cost and due date.
   - `HOTELS_CAPEX_BUDGET` (→ `hotels.capex_budget_line`): the owner's capex budget by year and line, each line naming the PIP item it funds, if any.
   - **Document categories:** franchise agreement, PIP report, management agreement, STR or performance report (route FILE; intake expects them).
3. **A calculator, `hotels-performance-1`:** trailing occupancy, ADR and RevPAR from the operating history (with the years beside the P&L), the RevPAR index from the STR benchmark, monthly seasonality (weakest ÷ strongest month), the franchise's and management agreement's term left, the PIP total and what of it falls due within a year unbudgeted, and the latest year's brand fees ÷ rooms revenue. A missing figure names the import it needs.
4. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are recognised by their heading and read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page; the agreements, the PIP, the STR report and the paged extracts are never read as claims. Checks whose answer a document can change wait for the documents.
   - **Performance claims register** (`hotels-performance-claims`): the RevPAR index, occupancy, ADR and RevPAR a narrative document states, against the trailing twelve months — the index (Σ hotel RevPAR ÷ Σ comp-set RevPAR × 100) from the STR benchmark, the rest from the operating history. Verified within 1 point (or 1%) (+1, confirmed value only), contradicted beyond 3 (−1), unverified with no twelve complete months (0), each in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`).
   - **Franchise term** (`hotels-franchise-term`): the franchise agreement's end — its own expiry sentence governing over the register — measured from the expected closing a document states (else the analysis date). Under five years left is −1, with the penalty for leaving early (the agreement's liquidated-damages sentence governing over the register) and the agreement's termination sentences quoted; unpriced.
   - **PIP against the budget** (`hotels-pip-schedule`): PIP items due on or before twelve months after the closing that the budget lines naming them do not fully fund, one draft, priced as an **escrow** at the unbudgeted amount (currency from the agreement's PIP estimate). The whole PIP stays the T-067 remediation cost; the escrow is a holdback for timing, so the two never subtract twice.
   - **Management terms that survive a sale** (`hotels-management-survival`): sentences in the management agreement that survive a sale or bind a purchaser, quoted, with the agreement's term and fees from the register (−1, unpriced). The T-067 termination-fee rule is unchanged.
   - **Brand fees against rooms revenue** (`hotels-brand-fee-trend`): franchise, royalty, marketing and reservation fee lines ÷ the rooms revenue line across the imported P&L years (three or more consecutive), rising every year to 1.15× the first year's ratio or more (−1). One year cannot show it.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `hotels-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the key expenses rooms department, food and beverage, utilities and repairs; `hotels-revpar-trend` (−1, RevPAR falling every year by 5% or more) and `hotels-recent-run` (T-185). Brand fees are not a key expense: they have their own check against rooms revenue, so one defect never drafts twice.
5. **Directions.** Every T-166 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`hotels.operating_month`, `hotels.benchmark_month`, `hotels.agreement`, `hotels.pip_item`, and `brand-fees-outpacing-rooms` on `finance.pnl_line`); `apps/api/src/packs/hotels-directions.test.ts` runs every rule over the demo data, checks each proposed direction against the map and that the rules draft exactly the README's list. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value, never a price increase.
6. **No new screen.** The figures and the years table show on Finances, the drafts on Findings and the claims on Claims; the analysis stays buyer-side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`).

## Guardrails
- **No valuation or brand judgment.** Documents, dates and arithmetic; the pack never opines on the flag's worth, the market or whether a brand will renew. Stated in the findings and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states is shown as missing, never filled in.
- **No people data.** Every figure is a property aggregate or a business counterparty; nothing reaches a model.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-067 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/hotels/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Harbourview Hotel Holdings) gains the agreements register, the PIP schedule, the capex budget, a five-year P&L and the management presentation; the franchise and management agreements gain their term, penalty and survival clauses; the STR benchmark's comp set is re-based so the trailing index reads about 97. Planted defects: (a) PIP-01, due six months after the 31 December 2026 closing, funded by no budget line; (b) the deck's RevPAR index of 112 against the STR benchmark's ~97; (c) the franchise ending 31 December 2029, three years after closing, with liquidated damages; (d) brand fees rising from 7.5% to 9.9% of rooms revenue across FY2021–FY2025. The operating history stays 24 months, so occupancy, ADR and RevPAR history asks the seller for earlier years. `apps/api/src/packs/hotels.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the hotels pact joins the demo-org pool and imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270125000000_hotels_franchise_pip_agreements` adds three `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- **Site mismatches corrected:** the hotels page claimed GOP-margin trends, daily revenue history and franchise encumbrance findings, which no check produces; they are replaced by what ships (performance claims, franchise term, PIP against the budget, surviving management terms and brand fees against rooms revenue).
