# ADR 0050: Fintech: payments and lending economics checked against processor and licence evidence, and the multi-year P&L trend (T-159)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the fintech pack of T-058 (spec §19 1a); released on merge.

## Context
- Fintech deals are priced on volume and growth claims that live in processor exports, and on licences that can lapse quietly. The T-058 pack read the loan tape, AML alerts and the partner-bank change-of-control clause; nothing checked processed volume, take rate, loss rates against covenants, or licence status.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): a pack reads every imported year of the P&L and drafts trend findings — revenue growth, margin drift and the vertical's key expense ratios — each figure traceable to its import.

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `FINTECH` keeps its slug, cluster, valuation methods (ARR multiple, DCF) and the software path. The T-058 targets and rules stay first and unchanged; the pack adds, by registry entry only:
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `FINTECH_PROCESSOR_VOLUME` (→ `fintech.processor_month`: month, processor, processed volume, transactions, net revenue, chargeback count and amount, fraud losses) and `FINTECH_LICENCES` (→ `fintech.licence`: id, kind — money transmitter, lending, payment institution, e-money, other, sponsor bank — jurisdiction, issuer, the register's status and dates). Counts and amounts only; no merchant, cardholder or person.
   - **Document categories:** licence, sponsor-bank agreement, PCI attestation, KYC/AML programme, exam report or consent order (route FILE; intake expects them, so a missing one is a checklist gap).
   - **A calculator, `fintech-payments-1`:** trailing TPV, growth, transactions, net revenue, effective take rate, the latest quarter's chargeback and fraud-loss rates, licences lapsed at the analysis date, the loan-loss rate and the share of alerts closed without review. Each figure states its formula and row count; a missing one names the import it needs.
2. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each figure quoted from one sentence and verified on the page by the citation check, as the SPV pack does (ADR 0044):
   - **Claims register** (`fintech-volume-claims`): TPV, TPV growth, take rate and retention as a document states them, against the exports — TPV verified within 0.5% and contradicted beyond 1%, naming the processor-month the gap equals when it equals one exactly; growth within 1 / beyond 3 points; take rate within 0.05 / beyond 0.15 points; retention always UNVERIFIED (exports carry no merchant identities). Each lands in the claims register (origin `PACK_RULE`): contradicted −1, verified +1 confirmed value, unverified 0.
   - **Revenue reconciliation** (`fintech-revenue-reconciliation`): the P&L's payments-revenue lines against the exports' net revenue for each calendar year the exports cover in full; beyond 2% is a −1 drag.
   - **Licences** (`fintech-licence-status`): each register row at the analysis date (the last day of the latest processor month — never the clock). A document sentence naming the id and its expiry governs over the register, so the check waits until documents have been read (an import that arrives before the data room drafts nothing yet). Lapsed (by date or by status) is a −1 drag, unpriced: an escrow, a special indemnity or reinstatement as a closing condition. Expiring within 90 days is 0, a closing condition.
   - **Loss rates** (`fintech-loss-thresholds`): chargebacks ÷ settled transactions (or amount ÷ volume when the document says so) and fraud losses ÷ volume, by calendar quarter with all three months, against the thresholds the documents themselves state. The latest quarter breaching is −1; three quarterly rises in a row under the threshold is 0, a watch item. No threshold is assumed: with none stated, rates are shown on the Payments page and nothing is drafted.
   - **Compliance evidence** (`fintech-compliance-evidence`): the PCI attestation, the KYC/AML programme and exam reports, recognised by their headings (and on the Payments page also by the category a document was filed under). An attestation more than a year old and a consent order on file are 0, for review, cited to the heading. Absence is shown as absence on the Payments page and as a gap on the intake checklist (the pack's document categories are expected inputs), never drafted: a finding must cite its evidence, and a missing document has none.
3. **The multi-year P&L trend is a shared piece** (`pnl-trend-1` in `packages/calculations/src/pnl/trend.ts`, rule factory `pnlTrendRules` in `packs/pnl-trend.ts`): fiscal years a file states, or all twelve months of a year, never a partial year scaled up; revenue growth, gross and operating margin, and the key expenses a pack names (fintech: fraud, chargeback, credit and processing costs). Three rules per pack: the trend itself (0, a record even with one year), margin drift of 3 points or more (−1) and a key expense whose ratio to revenue rose every year to 1.25× its first year or more (−1). Rules read `finance.pnl_line` through the one evidence loader, so withdrawn imports are excluded; when two imports hold the same line and year, the newer row is read. Other packs adopt it by naming their key expenses.
4. **Directions.** Every rule outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (connectors), and a contract test (`apps/api/src/packs/fintech-directions.test.ts`) runs the rules over the demo data and checks each proposed direction against the map. The cushion asymmetry holds: a verified claim is confirmed value in the buyer-confidential ledger, never a price increase.
5. **The Payments tab** (`/deals/:id/fintech`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/fintech`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Its "Run fintech checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No legal conclusions.** Licence status and dates are reported as the register and documents state them; the pack never says whether a licence is required or a programme is compliant. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states is shown as missing.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/fintech/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Tallyport Payments) gains the processor exports, the licence register, a three-year P&L, the investor presentation, a lapsed licence certificate, the PCI attestation, an exam report and the sponsor agreement's monitoring clause, with planted defects (a) a deck TPV that double-counts one processor month, (b) a state licence that lapsed four months before the latest month, (c) the latest quarter's chargeback rate over the sponsor agreement's 0.90%, (d) fraud losses rising from 0.6% to 1.7% of revenue over FY2023–FY2025. `apps/api/src/packs/fintech.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the fintech pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270109000000_fintech_payments_pack` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for documents the sentence patterns do not read; network-program thresholds not written into a document; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged (QoE already schedules every imported period; the valuation basis reads the trailing year).
