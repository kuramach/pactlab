## Why

Services firms are priced on fee-earner economics that live in time-and-billing exports: utilization, realization, and how much of the book walks out if two partners leave. No pack reads that evidence today.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Utilization and realization recompute** (`packages/calculations`): hours, billed and collected amounts from time/billing exports; realization trend across the imported P&L years.
2. **Concentration findings:** fees by client and by fee-earner recomputed from exports; a top client or rainmaker above the deck's stated ceiling is a claims-register contradiction.
3. **WIP and AR ageing:** ageing profiles from reports, by year; ageing drift is a trend finding priced into working capital.
4. **Coverage register:** non-compete / non-solicit documents for revenue-carrying staff; a rainmaker without coverage is a finding.
5. **Revenue quality:** recurring vs project revenue split from the billing data, as a finding with its evidence.

## Guardrails

- **No HR or legal judgment:** the pack reports documents, coverage and arithmetic; it never assesses individuals.
- **Evidence and absence:** figures come only from documents and adapters; absence is shown as absence, never filled in.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is already written as available; verify it against the built pack and flag mismatches in the PR.
