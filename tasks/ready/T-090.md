# T-090 — Training video series: core curriculum + one video per pack, produced by a repeatable pipeline (pilot first)

**Lane:** docs
**Context:** deep
**Touches:** docs, site
**Needs:** demo pacts with synthetic data (HealthyCo + per-pack demo sets) as the on-screen material; T-070 showcase env or local app for screen capture

## Kumar's ask (2026-10-26)

"Are you capable of generating nice enterprise-class training video for each pack?" — answered honestly the same day, and approved: yes, with the right method. This task is that method, as a production program — **pilot first, series only after Kumar approves the pilot's quality bar.**

## The method (and what it is not)

Enterprise training video is: a real script, professional narration, **the actual product on screen**, captions, chapters, transcripts. It is not AI-generated footage — generated clips are ~10s, UI text garbles, and nobody learns a workflow from fake screens. Generated video is used only where it's genuinely good: the series intro/outro sting.

Pipeline per video (all sources committed to the repo under `training/` so any video can be re-rendered when the product changes):

1. **Script + storyboard** — narration text and the shot list (which screen, which demo pact state, which callout) written together; Kumar can red-pen the script cheaply before any production happens.
2. **Narration** — one consistent generated voice across the whole series; same voice, same pace settings, every video. Loudness-normalized.
3. **Visuals** — screen captures of the real app running the synthetic demo pacts (never a customer's data, never a mock), plus branded slide/diagram cards in the site identity (navy #081127, indigo #6978ff, Manrope) for concepts that aren't a screen (the findings->valuation loop, the direction map).
4. **Assembly** — narration + visuals + callouts rendered to 1080p MP4 with standard tooling (ffmpeg-class); chapter markers; WebVTT captions; a full transcript per video (accessibility and LMS packaging are table stakes for enterprise buyers).
5. **QC** — frame-level review of every cut + transcript proofread against the script; a checklist in the production doc, signed off per video.

## Part 1 — The pilot (gate for everything else)

Produce exactly one video end-to-end: **Core 1 — "Run your first pact"** (create a pact, connect evidence, read findings, see the bridge). Kumar reviews the pilot and sets the quality bar in writing (notes appended to this task). **The series does not proceed past the pilot without his approval** — if the bar isn't met, the pipeline gets fixed on one video, not thirteen.

## Part 2 — Core series (teach the platform once)

Five videos, ~6-10 min each, in order:

1. **Run your first pact** — the pilot; the whole loop in one pass.
2. **Evidence and connectors** — GitHub/Jira/Stripe/documents in; what normalized evidence is.
3. **Findings and the claims register** — directions (-1/0/+1), said-vs-observed, how a finding earns its place.
4. **The valuation bridge and scenarios** — drags, cushion and the confidential ledger, running what-ifs.
5. **Phases and terms** — Pre/Diligence/Post-Close, term proposals, and the redline register (T-089) as the negotiation surface.

Admin/operator training is a separate short for Kumar's own use, listed in the production doc, not in the customer series.

## Part 3 — Pack videos (one per pack, template-driven)

Twelve videos, **5-8 minutes each**, identical structure so the series feels like one product:

1. The deal this pack is for, and what kills it (the pack's lens in one minute).
2. Its evidence — what to connect/collect, what good looks like.
3. Its signature findings — the drags this pack exists to catch, shown on the pack's demo pact.
4. The worked pass — the demo pact from evidence to bridge in this pack.
5. Pitfalls — the two or three mistakes users make in this industry.

Packs in build order (site/index order): fintech, healthcare, telecom & media, professional services, pharma & biotech, e-commerce & DTC, multifamily, commercial property, hotels, small clinics, hospitals, and **Venture Raise last but separate in audience** — its video is founder-facing ("Getting diligenced: run your raise") and doubles as the founder track's core asset for T-088.

## Part 4 — Publication and upkeep

- **Home:** an in-app Academy/help section lists the series (core path first, packs by industry), each video with captions + transcript + the demo pact it used; the public site carries the series index and the Venture Raise video publicly (founder marketing, T-088).
- **Versioning:** each video carries the product version it was rendered against; when a UI flow changes materially, the production doc's re-render list names the affected videos — sources are committed precisely so a re-render is an afternoon, not a re-shoot.
- **Rhythm:** new pack = new pack video in the same template, added to the quarterly review in T-088's ops cadence.

## Guardrails

- Synthetic demo data only, on screen and in examples — a real customer's deal in a training video is a firing offense, structurally prevented by producing only against demo pacts.
- No invented capabilities in scripts: the standing posture governs marketing copy; **training teaches what the build in front of the user does** — a script line describing an unbuilt feature fails QC.
- One voice, one visual identity, one template: no per-video improvisation of style.

## Acceptance

- Pilot: script, storyboard, narration, MP4 with captions + transcript + chapters committed with all sources; Kumar's approval (or fix list) recorded on this task.
- Core series: five videos passing the per-video QC checklist, published in the in-app Academy.
- Pack series: twelve videos in template, each produced against its pack's demo pact, Venture Raise published publicly as well.
- Production doc (`training/README.md`): the pipeline, the QC checklist, the voice/style settings, and the re-render list — complete enough that a future producer (human or agent) reproduces the series without archaeology.
