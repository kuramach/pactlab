# ADR 0052: Small clinics: the few-site clinic variant of the healthcare pack — owner-operator, referrals, premises (T-161)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar directed the pack, 2026-11-26). Extends the small clinics pack of T-068 (spec §19 10a) on the healthcare pack of T-160 (ADR 0051); released on merge.

## Context
- Small clinics trade on the same evidence as larger provider groups, but the risks concentrate: one owner-operator, one referral source, one lease. The T-068 pack read patient volume, equipment and the coding summary; nothing read the T-160 collections and credentials evidence, the owner's share, where the patients come from, or how long the premises are secured.
- Kumar (2026-11-26, every pack): read every imported year of the P&L and draft trend findings, each figure traceable to its import (`pnl-trend-1`, ADR 0050).

## Decision
1. **Same pack, same company type.** `SMALL_CLINICS` keeps its slug, cluster and valuation method (DCF). The T-068 targets and rules stay first and unchanged.
2. **The T-160 evidence set is reused by reference, never copied.** The registry entry lists the healthcare targets (`HEALTHCARE_REMITTANCES`, `HEALTHCARE_CLINICIAN_COLLECTIONS`, `HEALTHCARE_CREDENTIALS`, `HEALTHCARE_PAYER_CONTRACTS`) and rule ids (`healthcare-practice-claims`, `-collections-reconciliation`, `-collections-trend`, `-payer-mix-shift`, `-credentialing`, `-payer-contracts`, `-compliance-evidence`) by id, so a change to them reaches clinics. Clinician concentration is left out: in a clinic the largest clinician is usually the seller, and the owner-operator check below reads that. The healthcare Practice page (`/healthcare`) opens for clinics pacts as it is, with the clinic's key expenses on its P&L trend. Healthcare's own behaviour is unchanged.
3. **What the pack adds, by registry entry only:**
   - **Imports (CSV, ADR 0010):** `CLINICS_SERVICE_LINES` (→ `clinics.service_line_month`: month, service line, pseudonymous provider, visits, collected, direct cost) and `CLINICS_REFERRALS` (→ `clinics.referral_month`: month, referral source, source type, visits, new patients). Providers only as pseudonymous references; sources are organisations or channels; the people-column guard applies.
   - **The lease is a document**, the common `LEASE` category; a new registry field, `requiredInputs`, makes it a required input on the intake checklist for this pack (the healthcare cluster otherwise leaves it optional).
   - **A calculator, `clinics-practice-1`:** the healthcare collections figures as they are, then the largest service line's share of collections, the highest service-line margin after direct costs and the largest referral source's share of visits. A missing figure names the import it needs.
4. **Every check is deterministic; no AI job type is added** (so no ADR 0034 gate applies). Documents are read by fixed sentence patterns, each quote verified on its page. The analysis date is the last day of the latest month in the exports, never the clock.
   - **Owner-operator dependence** (`clinics-owner-dependence`): the selling owner is the provider a document names by pseudonymous reference ("P01 is the selling owner"); with none named, nothing is drafted and the Clinic tab shows the absence. A service line on which only the owner collected in the trailing twelve months — no associate coverage — is −1 (HIGH when it is the highest-margin line or 20% or more of collections). An owner carrying 30% or more of collections or visits with no succession, transition or associate coverage described in the documents (or a sentence saying there is none) is −1. Retention risk, never a ranking.
   - **Referral concentration** (`clinics-referral-concentration`): visits by source over the trailing twelve months; one source at 40% or more of all visits is −1 (HIGH at 50%). Self-referrals and walk-ins are patients choosing the clinic, never one source. Arithmetic only: whether a referral arrangement is lawful is for counsel.
   - **Premises** (`clinics-premises`): a document whose first sentence names a lease is read for the sentence on when the term ends, the one on renewal (granted, stated as none, or not stated) and the one on assignment or a change of control. The remaining term is measured from the expected closing a document states, else the analysis date, and says which. A term ending within 24 months with no renewal option granted is −1, priced by the reviewer as relocation or renegotiation risk; assignment needing the landlord's consent, or barred, is 0, a closing condition. A copy of a lease already read is read once.
   - **The shared multi-year P&L rules** `clinics-pnl-trend` (0), `-margin-drift` (−1) and `-expense-outpacing` (−1), with the clinic's key expenses: clinical and dental supplies, laboratory fees, clinical staff, rent and occupancy.
5. **Directions.** Every T-161 outcome names an evidence type and signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`clinics.service_line_month`, `clinics.referral_month`, and the lease signals on `document.finding`); `apps/api/src/packs/clinics-directions.test.ts` runs every rule in the pack's registry entry over the demo data and checks each proposed direction against the map.
6. **The Clinic tab** (`/deals/:id/clinics`, `GET /v1/deals/:dealId/clinics`) is read-only and the buyer's own side (`BUYER_ANALYSIS_READ`; a target contributor gets 403). Lists are bounded: a clinic's service lines and their providers, the 25 largest referral sources, one row per lease. "Run clinic checks" runs the pack's rules into the one findings register.

## Guardrails
- **No clinical or legal judgment.** Shares, dates and the sentences the documents contain; never the quality of care, whether a referral arrangement is lawful, or what a lease means in law. Stated on the page, in the API view and on the site.
- **Evidence and absence.** Figures come only from imports and quoted sentences; what none states (no owner named, no closing date, no renewal sentence) is shown as missing.
- **People data (rule 7).** Providers, including the selling owner, are pseudonymous references; their figures stay in deterministic code and never reach a model; rules read only business and financial documents, never HR-classed ones.
- **No walls added.** Two-party, as T-068 was.

## Consequences
- Demo data: `pactlab-demo-data/small-clinics/{USD,EUR,GBP}` (Clearbrook Clinics) gains the reused healthcare exports, service lines, referrals, a three-year P&L, the memorandum (owner, no succession plan, target closing date), the Harbour Street lease and a licence verification, with planted defects (a) 58% of visits from one referral source, (b) the lease ending 14 months after the stated closing with no renewal option, (c) the selling owner as the only provider of implants, the highest-margin line, (d) supplies outpacing revenue across FY2023–FY2025. `apps/api/src/packs/clinics.acceptance.api.test.ts` runs it end to end; the clinics pact joins the demo-org pool and its build imports the P&L by year.
- **Migration** `20270111000000_small_clinics_owner_referrals_premises` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.
- Not in this slice: a model-proposed register for leases the sentence patterns do not read; the T-068 rules moving onto the T-160 collections export; QoE and the valuation basis are unchanged.
