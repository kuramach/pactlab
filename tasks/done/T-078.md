# T-078 — Loan terms, and debt & cash on Finances (pilot bug 12)

**Lane:** core
**Context:** standard
**Touches:** db (migration, balance-sheet repo), contracts, api (balance sheet, demo seed), web (valuation form/card, finances), e2e
**Needs:** T-077

## Why (Kumar, 2026-10-24)

After recording a loan there was nowhere to put its term or interest rate, and Finances didn't show it.

## Done

- **Migration** (expand only): nullable loan-term columns on items and on every version:
  - rate (numeric fraction), rate type and reference;
  - maturity, lender, repayment, covenants.
  - CHECKs allow terms on DEBT items only.
- **Contracts and API:**
  - the rate is typed as a percent and stored as an exact fraction (`7.5` → `0.075`);
  - terms on cash or debt-like items are refused (400 on create, 422 `TERMS_DEBT_ONLY` on revise);
  - each revision keeps its own terms.
- **Web:**
  - "Loan terms (optional)" on the form for debt items;
  - a terms line on the Valuation card;
  - Finances gets a **Debt & cash** card: totals plus a debt schedule (lender, amount, rate, maturity, repayment, source), with "Within 12 months" flagged.
- **Tests:**
  - API: terms saved, versioned, refused on cash;
  - unit: percent display;
  - e2e: record a loan with terms, then see it on Finances.
