## Why

Special-purpose vehicles are how much early-stage investing actually happens: an organizer pools many investors into a single Delaware vehicle that takes one line on a startup's cap table for one equity round. The investors' diligence problem is double — the startup's round and the vehicle's own economics — and the failure modes are checkable: cap-table math, SAFE terms, fee and carry that differ between the summary and the operating documents, side letters, accreditation gaps. No current pack covers the pooled-vehicle seat: Venture Raise (T-083) is the founder's side of the round, and T-106 is a real-estate sponsor raise, not a venture vehicle.

## Shape

One pact, three parties, two walled directions — the T-106 shape applied to a venture round:

- **Vehicle side (organizer <-> investors):** the offering diligence below. Investors are seated as their own orgs (or INVESTOR guests, per the T-106 seat pattern) with role-limited views.
- **Investment side (SPV -> startup):** diligence on the round itself — the startup's documents are evidence in the pact; the startup sees only the SPV as a single investor counterparty.

The startup never sees the investor list or the vehicle's internal economics beyond what the organizer publishes. Investors never see the organizer's side arrangements or each other's terms. New company type `STARTUP_SPV` (label "Startup equity SPV", slug `startup-spv`), released when merged.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Round claims register.** Every material claim in the startup's deck and round summary — valuation, traction, runway, prior-round terms — is a register entry mapped to its evidence (the T-083 deck-metrics pattern, claims register origin `PACK_RULE`), surfaced as VERIFIED, CONTRADICTED or UNVERIFIED.
2. **Cap-table and round-math verification** (`packages/calculations`): shares × price vs the stated round size; SAFE and convertible conversions recomputed with caps, discounts and MFN terms; pro-rata rights recorded per instrument. Deterministic; documents only.
3. **Vehicle economics.** Management fee, carry, expenses and any organizer co-invest are extracted from the SPV's operating/subscription documents; the fee load and carry are recomputed and checked against the investor-facing summary. Net-of-fees investor return scenarios (exit paths on the Sextant machinery) are labelled scenarios from stated assumptions, never promises.
4. **Side-letter register.** Any side letter in the evidence is registered organizer-side with its economic effect (fee waiver, carry difference, information rights). Preferential economics that the subscription summary does not disclose are a finding to the organizer. One investor's terms never appear in another investor's view.
5. **Investor seats and accreditation status.** Tracked per investor (self-certified vs third-party verified, per the vehicle's 506(b)/(c) shape). Verification is an external specialist integration (link + status + evidence pointer) — do not build verification; best-in-class rule.
6. **Investor view (published subset).** A versioned, frozen snapshot built from an allow-list: the round claims register, the cap-table verification, the vehicle economics check, and the return scenarios. Organizer-confidential material can never enter it — enforced in the data layer and proven by snapshot test, the T-083/T-106 split pattern.

## Finding rules (registered in the pack)

1. **Cap-table mismatch:** recomputed share counts, conversion amounts or round totals differ from the stated cap table → drag.
2. **Instrument terms vs claims:** SAFE/note terms in the signed documents differ from the deck or round summary (cap, discount, MFN, pro-rata) → the claim is CONTRADICTED.
3. **Fee/carry mismatch:** the operating documents' fee or carry differs from the investor summary → drag.
4. **Undisclosed preferential terms:** a side letter grants economics the summary does not disclose → organizer-side finding.
5. **Accreditation gap:** a 506(c) vehicle with investors not third-party verified → organizer-side finding.
6. **Missing Form D evidence:** no filing evidence for the offering → gap finding.
7. **Stale or missing round documents:** a register claim with no underlying document → UNVERIFIED gap finding.

## Guardrails

- **Diligence support only.** Not legal advice; the pack performs no securities-offering, broker-dealer or investment-adviser function and makes no accreditation determination; it organises evidence and flags gaps for the parties and their counsel.
- **No invented figures.** Startup metrics and vehicle terms come only from documents and adapters; absence is shown as absence.
- **Confidentiality is tested, not promised:** investor-view snapshots contain zero organizer-negotiation and zero other-investor content; the startup's view contains zero investor-list content.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is written as available, in the same style as the other pack pages (standing Kumar posture), with the diligence-support guardrail wording.

## Demo and acceptance

- The demo runs end to end: round register + cap-table verification + vehicle economics check + investor view. All planted defects surface as findings, and the wall tests prove the unpublished material stays unpublished.
