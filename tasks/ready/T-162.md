## Why

Hospitals carry evidence no other pack reads: cost reports, payer contracts that expire mid-hold, regulated quality reporting and a physical plant whose deferred capex hides in the P&L trend.

## What this pack adds (the delta only)

1. **Cost-report reconciliation:** occupancy, payer mix and cost figures from the filed cost reports (imported documents) reconciled against the deck and the financial statements; contradictions are findings.
2. **Payer contract register:** each material payer agreement with rate basis and expiry/renegotiation window from documents; expiry inside 12 months of close is a finding.
3. **Medical-staff register at scale:** roster vs licence and privilege status/expiry (the T-160 credentialing pattern, hospital scale); a department whose privileges lapse together is a finding.
4. **Plant and capex trend:** capex ratio across the imported P&L years plus property-condition documents as evidence; falling capex against a 'modern plant' claim is a trend finding.
5. **Quality-reporting claims:** published quality/star-rating claims as claims-register entries mapped to the reporting documents supplied.

## Guardrails

- **No clinical, survey or legal judgment:** the pack reconciles documents and dates; it never predicts survey outcomes or assesses care quality.
- **Evidence and absence:** figures come only from documents and adapters; absence is shown as absence, never filled in.
- **Marketing posture:** the site industry page is already written as available; verify it against the built pack and flag mismatches in the PR.

## Multi-year P&L and trend (Kumar, 2026-11-26 — every pack)
The pack must take imported P&L statements for multiple fiscal years through the platform import system (statements by year) and use all of them: QoE and the valuation bridge read every imported year, and the pack produces trend findings — revenue growth, gross/operating margin drift, and the vertical's key expense ratios across years — each figure traceable to the import it came from. A single-year import still works (trend findings simply report one year). Withdrawn imports are excluded from trend reads like every other read path. The demo data includes at least three years of P&L with one planted trend defect that a single-year view would miss, and the acceptance test proves it surfaces.
