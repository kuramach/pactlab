# ADR 0057: Five years of history: the expectation, the imports and the trend checks, built on multifamily (T-183)

**Status:** accepted (Kumar, 2026-11-26: *"In a deal how do u determine the strength unless u look at historicals? … this style of data expectancy is good for all business"*). Extends ADR 0042 (deterministic imports) and ADR 0050 (the shared multi-year P&L trend). T-184 applies it to every other pack.

## Context
- The demo data and most packs read twelve months: a T-12 and one rent-roll snapshot. A buyer judges strength from the trend, and the defects that sink a property deal (occupancy slipping, repairs outgrowing rent, a last year dressed for sale) are invisible in one year.
- The P&L import (`FINANCIAL_PNL`, `finance.pnl_line`) already reads years or months across the columns, a long file of line and month rows, and several files (the newer row wins per line and period). Nothing read rent or occupancy over time.

## Decision
1. **The rule, for every pack.** A pack expects up to **five fiscal years** of history, plus the current year or the T-12:
   - income and expenses: the operating statement by year or by month (the P&L import);
   - the pack's operating drivers: for multifamily, rent and occupancy.
2. **Less history is accepted, never refused.** Every history figure states its coverage ("FY2022–FY2026 (5 of 5 years)", "FY2025–FY2026 (2 of 5 years): ask the seller for FY2022–FY2024 operating statements"). The expected years are the latest year read and the four before it. A missing year is an **ask of the seller** through the existing request flow (operating years as the income statement, which a P&L import covers; occupancy years as the occupancy history), never a figure. Nothing is extrapolated: a year not imported is empty in every series and table.
3. **Deterministic, as ADR 0042 requires.** The AI may propose a multi-year file's structure; code reads every value; a confirmed format is saved and reused with no AI (a two-year file laid out like a confirmed five-year one is read the same way). Imports overlapping a period follow the existing rule: the newer row wins. Withdrawn imports never count (the evidence loader filters them).
4. **Years.** A year is labelled by the year it ends in. A year the P&L states (a column such as FY2026) is read as stated; monthly P&L lines and the occupancy history are read in twelve-month years ending with the latest imported month, so a fiscal year ending in September reads as one; the T-12 extract is the current year when the P&L does not hold it. A part year is left out, never scaled up.
5. **Imports (multifamily).** The operating statement is the core P&L import, unchanged. Two targets are added:
   - `MULTIFAMILY_OCCUPANCY` → `property.occupancy_period`: period (month or year), unit type ("All" or a type), units, occupied units, average in-place rent and concessions;
   - `MULTIFAMILY_RENT_ROLL_HISTORY` → `multifamily.unit_snapshot`: rent-roll snapshots, one row per unit per as-of date.
   *Deviation from the brief*, which suggested one target for all three forms: a rent roll is unit rows and a summary is period rows, so one shape cannot hold both without optional columns that change meaning by row. Both feed one occupancy series: monthly figures win, a snapshot fills a month they leave empty, and a year needs twelve months or a snapshot in each quarter. The current rent roll stays its own import.
6. **The calculator** (`multifamily-metrics-3`, history `multifamily-history-1`, decimal-only, every figure naming its rows): effective gross income, NOI and the expense ratio per year; NOI and revenue compound growth between the first and last year read; average in-place rent growth per year (occupied-weighted); occupancy per year as the average and the low point; the trailing three-year average vacancy (1 − the mean of the last three years' averages). The report carries a generic `PackHistory` table (years, rows, coverage, asks) that every pack's screen reads the same way.
7. **Draft findings** (never accepted automatically, each cited to the imported rows; thresholds are named constants, listed in `docs/agent/packs.md`):
   - `multifamily-occupancy-erosion` (−1): down 3 points or more first year to last, or a year's average below 90% in any of the last three years;
   - `multifamily-rent-below-expense-growth` (−1): operating expenses, or a key expense, growing 2 points a year or more faster than average rent, over the years both histories hold; one draft per expense;
   - `multifamily-noi-spike` (−1): the latest year's NOI growth 5 points or more above the earlier years' average, naming what moved (key expenses that fell against revenue, credits booked against costs, concessions): the "dressed for sale" pattern;
   - expenses outpacing income and margin drift: the shared `pnlTrendRules('multifamily', …)` (ADR 0050), with the key expenses repairs and maintenance, payroll, utilities, insurance (a premium refund is never insurance cost) and property taxes.
   Multifamily syndication inherits all of it by reference.
8. **On screen.** Home → Property metrics: a **History** strip (the shared ECharts line chart, five points) under NOI, occupancy and average rent, with the coverage, and one ask of the seller per kind of history with missing years. Finances: the years side by side (revenue, expenses, NOI, margin, expense ratio, occupancy average and low point, average rent, rent growth).

## Consequences
- **Demo data:** `pactlab-demo-data/multifamily` and `multifamily-syndication`, all three currencies, gain FY2022–FY2026 (October to September): the P&L by year (`pnl.csv`) and by month (`operating-statements-monthly.csv`), 60 months of occupancy and rent, and quarterly rent-roll snapshots for the last two years. FY2026 is the T-12, so `t12.csv` equals the last twelve months of the history, and the September 2026 occupancy matches `rent-roll.csv` (occupancy is now 88.5%, so the rent roll, billing and T-12 changed with it). Planted: occupancy slipping from about 95% to about 89%; repairs and maintenance outpacing rent; an FY2026 NOI jump from deferred repairs and a one-off insurance refund. Other packs' files are byte-identical.
- **Not in this slice:** a direction-map entry and contract test for the multifamily evidence types (the map lives in `packages/connectors`, outside this task; no multifamily type is mapped today); occupancy by unit type on screen (the import accepts it; the calculator sums the types); the other packs (T-184).
- **UI pattern:** the History strip and the years table are new patterns; they need Kumar's approval before T-184 spreads them, then an entry in `docs/ui-patterns.md`.
- **Migration** `20270116000000_multifamily_history` adds two `import_target` values; no table. Rollback: nothing to drop; the enum values stay unused.

## Addendum (T-184, 2026-11-26): the generic form, first on fintech
- **One shared module** (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history.ts`, `pack-history-1`): the P&L by fiscal year (read as `pnl-trend-1` reads it: calendar years, a stated year as stated, otherwise all twelve months) and the pack's operating drivers by calendar year from a period-series import it already has (a month per row). A driver is declarative (Σ fields; Σ fields ÷ Σ fields; the sum in the year's last month), so a pack adds history by naming its drivers, not by writing a reader. It returns the generic `PackHistory` table, coverage and asks; `withHistory` gives a figure its History strip (`historyStrip`), so Home and Finances need no pack-specific code.
- **One trend rule shape** (`driverTrendRule`): a driver moving the adverse way every year across the latest run of three or more consecutive years, by a named threshold first to last, is a −1 draft citing the years' rows. Thresholds are constants in the pack's rules.
- **Fintech** (spec §19 1c): the processor export runs back five years (no new target), the P&L is five years, and `fintech-take-rate-trend` / `fintech-loss-rate-trend` read them.
- **Calendar years outside multifamily.** Multifamily keeps its twelve months ending with the latest month (its fiscal year ends in September); other packs read calendar years, as `pnl-trend-1` already does. A pack with another fiscal year end is a follow-up option on the driver reader.
- **Not in this slice:** the driver-trend signal in `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` (`packages/connectors`, outside T-184's paths); the other packs (T-184b) and hospitals, commercial property and hotels once their rebuilds land (T-184c).

## Addendum (T-184b, 2026-11-27): every other pack, and the direction map
- **The same module, a few more shapes.** A driver may filter its rows (`numeratorWhere` / `denominatorWhere`, exact or `contains`), take fields off its numerator (`numeratorLess`), scale (`multiplier`), take the complement (`complement`), or `compute` its years where no sum expresses it. Every figure still names its rows; part years are still left out.
- **Per pack (drivers; the planted trend):** telecom & media (subscribers, monthly churn, ARPU; churn rising), professional services (utilization, realization, headcount; utilization falling), healthcare services and small clinics (visits, commercial share, denial rate; healthcare's visits falling, the clinics' steady), e-commerce (orders, net order value, repeat orders, discount rate; discounts rising), pharma (each product's billed revenue, R&D among the P&L key expenses; a product eroding), software & SaaS (ARR at December, NRR, GRR, logo churn; NRR falling), music rights (statement income; falling), energy (plant generation, oil, gas; generation falling). Each trend is a −1 draft citing the years' rows.
- **Software reads its own years.** The SaaS engine keeps 36 months for its series and cohorts, so the yearly figures come from the month-by-month recurring revenue the software rules already read (invoice lines spread over their service period), in decimals; `GET …/metrics/pack` returns them with the P&L years, and Finances shows the card even though the SaaS figures live on their own screen. Software joins `pnlTrendRules` (hosting, R&D, sales and marketing).
- **Direction map.** `VALUATION_DIRECTION_MAP` carries one `driver-trend` signal (−1, ASSUMPTION) per evidence type a driver trend reads, including the music statement lines and energy production; the `*-directions.test.ts` entries (fintech's included) point at it.
- **Not applied:** Venture Raise (a seed-stage round is priced on traction since launch, not five years) and startup SPV (a vehicle with no operating business), stated in `docs/agent/packs.md`. Hospitals, commercial property and hotels follow their rebuilds (T-184c).
- **Calendar years** throughout, as `pnl-trend-1`; statements are counted by year only when their periods cover all twelve months.
- **Demo data:** every affected pack's exports run back to January 2021 from their own random generators, so the last 24 months and the findings read from them are unchanged; P&Ls run FY2021–FY2025.

## Addendum (T-185, 2026-11-27): history of any length, and never asking for years before the business existed
Kumar (2026-11-27): *"I can have 20 years of history for a company or 2 years or 5 years. All possibilities supported in the pack right?"*

- **Five years is the minimum expected, not the maximum.** Every complete fiscal year provided is read, up to a sanity limit of 30 counting back from the latest (`packages/calculations/src/packs/history-span.ts`, `history-span-1`). Per year nothing changes: stated years read as stated, months need all twelve, the newer import wins, withdrawn imports never count.
- **The five-year view is unchanged.** The existing trend rules (`driverTrendRule`, the multifamily history rules, the shared `pnlTrendRules` through `recentTrendOf`, telecom's squeezes and pharma's R&D burn) and the five-year figures (CAGRs, trailing vacancy) read the latest five years: the period a buyer prices on. Calculator versions bump (`multifamily-metrics-4`).
- **Long-run figures over the full history** (`longRunMetrics`), on every pack's history table (`PackHistory.longRun`), each with its coverage: revenue and EBITDA (multifamily: NOI) growth over the last ten fiscal years (FY2017 ÷ FY2026 ^ 1/9, when ten years are read) and over the full history; the worst year (the largest year-on-year fall, a share for money and points for ratios, consecutive years only) and the years to regain the peak before it, or "not yet recovered"; and through-cycle averages of the operating (NOI) margin and the pack's key ratio driver (`PackHistorySpec.keyDriver`, else its first ratio driver; multifamily: occupancy) beside the last five years'.
- **A new draft, `<pack>-recent-run`** ("the recent run is better than the long run"): the last five years' margin or key ratio driver averaging `RECENT_RUN_POINTS` (3 points) or more above the full history's, over `RECENT_RUN_MIN_YEARS` (10) years or more. −1, unpriced, cited to the years' rows; the key names the years, so a re-run adds nothing. Registered for multifamily (and syndication by reference), software, fintech, healthcare (clinics by reference), telecom, services, e-commerce, music and energy. Pharma's spec is built per pact from its products and is left out. Constants in `docs/agent/packs.md` §4e.
- **A history start on the pact:** `deals.history_start_year` (nullable, expand-only, `20270121000000_history_start`), set by the deal lead or an analyst on Finances (`PUT /v1/deals/:id/metrics/history-start`, audited `deal.history_start_set` / `_cleared`), prefilled from the year built on an accepted property profile (never applied until saved). The ask of the seller for missing years, with no start set, adds "…or tell us the business started later" and, on the request, "What's the first year this business traded?".
- **The expected years** are max(latest − 4, history start) … latest. Years before the start are never asked for; a gap inside the history is (only that year). Coverage reads "FY2007–FY2026 (20 years)", "FY2025–FY2026 (2 of 2 years since the business started)", "FY2022–FY2026 (5 years; earlier years not provided)", or "… (2 of 5 years): ask the seller for …".
- **On screen:** the History strip shows the last ten years (and says the rest are on Finances); the years table shows every year and scrolls sideways past about ten, the figure names staying put; the long-run figures sit under it; the history-start question sits beside it. The strip, the table and the new figure cards reuse the existing pieces (the shared line chart, the table and the figure cards); the history-start form is a plain labelled field and `Button`.
- **Demo data:** multifamily runs FY2007–FY2026 (all three currencies), with a 2009 downturn recovered over five years and a recent NOI margin about nine points above the twenty-year average, so the new draft fires; FY2022–FY2026 are byte-identical to before. The venture raise (Loopwise) began trading in January 2024 (billing from then, a P&L for FY2024 and FY2025) and the demo organization sets its history start to 2024: "2 of 2 years since the business started". Every other pack keeps its five years.
- **Rollback:** drop `deals.history_start_year`; the calculations then read as if no start were set.

## Addendum (T-184c, 2026-11-28): hospitals and commercial property; hotels waits for its rebuild
- **Hospitals** (`hospitals-operations-2`): the census and the payer mix run back five years, read beside the P&L as admissions, patient days, the average daily census (Σ patient days ÷ the days in the year, a `compute` driver: the census's own average column is per service line and month, so it is never summed) and the government programmes' share of net revenue (payers whose names say government, Medicare or Medicaid). The admissions trend (`hospitals-admissions-trend`, T-162) now has five years to read; the long run reads admissions, not the government share, because a rising government share is not a better recent run. The filed cost reports' patient days are the census's, year by year.
- **Commercial property** (`cre-leases-2`): a new monthly import, `CRE_LEASING_HISTORY` → `cre.leasing_month` (rentable and leased square feet, base rent in force, square feet whose lease ended), read beside the P&L and the CAM recovery ratio as occupancy, base rent per leased square foot (× 12) and lease expiries. The multifamily occupancy reader counts units and reads rent-roll snapshots; a commercial park lets square feet, so the drivers read area through the shared module rather than reuse the unit reader. A new draft, `cre-occupancy-trend` (−1, occupancy falling every year by 3 points or more, `OCCUPANCY_DECLINE_POINTS`).
- **Statement rent against the leases** (`cre-rent-roll-verification`): a P&L year is compared with the leases on file only when every one of them was in force for the whole year. Before a lease began, or after one ended, the space earned rent under a lease that is not on file; comparing such a year flagged rent the leases cannot explain as an overstatement.
- **Hotels:** not in this change. Its rebuild (T-166) is still in `tasks/ready`, so the history follows it (T-184d).
- **Migration** `20270126000000_commercial_property_leasing_history` adds one `import_target` value; no table.
